Newport · Business grant

Net Zero Newport: up to £30,000 toward commercial solar.

Newport's decarbonisation programme match-funds SMEs cutting their building's carbon — and solar and battery are exactly the kind of project it supports. Here's how it works.

What the programme offers

01

Match funding

The grant covers up to 50% of eligible project costs, to a maximum of £30,000 per business.

02

Free technical support

On-site advice to help you identify and plan the right decarbonisation measures for your building.

03

£250,000 fund

Backed by the UK Shared Prosperity Fund and delivered by Newport City Council.

04

For local SMEs

Open to small and medium-sized enterprises with premises inside the Newport City Council area.

Good to know

Newport grant FAQs

It's aimed at small and medium-sized enterprises that own or occupy a building within the Newport City Council area and want to reduce its carbon emissions. Eligibility criteria apply, so the first step is a conversation with the programme team.

Eligible decarbonisation measures — which can include solar PV, battery storage and energy-efficiency works — covering the equipment, its installation and commissioning. We'll scope a compliant project with you.

It's match-funded: the grant covers up to 50% of eligible project costs, to a maximum of £30,000, drawn from a total programme fund of £250,000 backed by the UK Shared Prosperity Fund.

Applications were shown as closed for the 2025/26 year at the time of writing. It's a recurring programme, so we recommend registering interest early and lining up a scoped project so you're ready for the next round.

We can't guarantee an award, but we help by scoping an eligible system, preparing the technical documentation and quotes the application needs, and timing it around the funding round.
