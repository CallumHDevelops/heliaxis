Everything on a page is a layer: each heading, photo, logo, shape and background. The **Layers** tab on the left lists them, top first, with a label for each kind, such as **TEXT**, **LOGO**, **BG** (background), **BTN** (button), **IMG** (photo) or **SHAPE**.

![Layers over a printer template](/help/apps/design-studio-template.jpg "Layers lists the artwork top first, with the printer template as the bottom row")

## Pick, hide and lock

- Click a row to pick that layer, the same as clicking it on the page. It’s the easy way to reach something small, or something under something else.
- Click the eye to hide or show it. A hidden layer is left out of the check and of your export.
- Click the padlock to lock it, so it can’t be moved, resized or turned by accident. Click it again to unlock it. The padlock at the top right does the same for whatever you’ve picked.

## Put layers in order

Pick a layer, then use the buttons under **Align** on the right: **Bring to front**, **Forward**, **Backward** and **Send to back** (hover over a button to see its name). The **Layers** list follows.

To move several things as one, pick them (hold Shift and click each, or drag a box round them), then click **Group** or press Ctrl+G. **Ungroup** (Ctrl+Shift+G) splits them again.

## Type exact sizes

With something picked, **Position · mm** on the right shows **X** and **Y** (measured from the top-left corner of the sheet, bleed included), **W**, **H**, the angle and **Op** (opacity, as a %). Type a number and press Enter.

**Keep proportions**, under the sizes, decides whether **W** and **H** change together:

- Ticked: type a new **W** and the height follows. It starts ticked for photos.
- Unticked: **W** and **H** change on their own, so you can type a panel’s exact size. It starts unticked for shapes and photo frames.
- Logos and icons always keep their proportions: the box says **(logos and icons always do)**.

**Align** lines up what you’ve picked with the **Safe** area or the **Trim** (the cut line). With several things picked, it lines them up with each other.

## The printer template layer

When a design has a printer template, it’s a row of its own, marked **GUIDE**: **Printer template**.

- It’s the bottom row, behind everything. If you choose **On top** on the **Design** tab under **Printer template**, it moves to the top row and sits over your artwork.
- It’s always locked: you can’t pick or move it on the page. It’s a guide, left out of your PDF unless you tick **Include the printer template** when you export.
- The eye hides it (the label on the page then says **hidden**) and shows it again the way it was.
- The bin removes it from the design. The message "Printer template removed — your artwork is unchanged." has an **Undo** button to bring it back.

The **Design** tab has the same controls on its **Printer template** card: **On top**, **Behind** and **Hidden**, an **Opacity** slider to make the template fainter, and **Remove template**. See [Design over a printer’s template](/admin/help/apps/design-printer-template).
