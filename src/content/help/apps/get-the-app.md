**Apps › Get the app** is open to everyone. (If you have neither studio, the menu shows a plain **Get the app** link instead of **Apps**. On a phone it’s under **More**. The **Android app** card on the dashboard, under **On your phone**, opens it too.)

The page asks **Which device are you looking to install on?** and shows four choices, each with its logo: **macOS**, **Windows**, **iOS** and **Android**. It starts on the one it thinks you’re using; click another to see that one instead. Only the steps for the device you pick are shown.

## Windows or macOS: Metis on your computer

Click **Windows** or **macOS** for **Metis**, the Heliaxis assistant as an app.

- **Download for Windows** or **Download for Mac**, with the version, release date and file size.
- Open the file and follow the steps, then sign in with your usual HAP email and password.
- Press **Ctrl+Shift+H** anywhere to ask Metis something. She’s the same Metis as in HAP, with the same chats, and what she makes appears in HAP straight away.
- It updates itself. When a new version is ready it shows a **Restart to update** bar.
- If it says **isn’t ready to download yet**, an admin hasn’t published that version. Ask them.

> **Good to know:** Until the app is code-signed, Windows may show a blue **Windows protected your PC** screen (choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and a Mac may say it cannot verify the developer (right-click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again). You only do this once.

## iOS: iPhone and iPad

There’s no download. Heliaxis is added to your Home Screen from Safari and then works like an app. The page shows the steps, or **You’re using the Heliaxis app from your Home Screen** once you have. Metis is in the app too.

## Android: phone and tablet

Click **Android** for the Heliaxis app.

- **Version** and release date, the file size, and **Download the app**. Opened inside the Android app, it also says **You’re up to date**, or **Update available** with the build you have (the button then says **Download update**). **What’s new** lists the changes in this version.
- **Send it to your phone**: a QR code to **Scan with your phone’s camera** (not shown on narrow screens), or **Or email yourself a download link**. Type an address you read on your phone and click **Email me the link**.
- **Installing**, **Updates** and **What works where**: the install steps, and which tools work on a phone, which need a bigger screen, and which work offline.

> **Good to know:** The emailed link and the QR code are personal to you and last 7 days. They open a page with a one-tap download, with no need to sign in. If one has run out, it says **This link has expired**: send yourself a new one from this page.

## Appearance

Under the device steps, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match phone** (follows your phone’s own light or dark setting).
