**Apps › Get the app** is open to everyone. (If you have neither studio, the menu shows a plain **Get the app** link instead of **Apps**. On a phone it’s under **More**. The **Android app** card on the dashboard, under **On your phone**, opens it too.) What it shows depends on the device you open it on.

![The Get the app page](/help/apps/get-the-app.jpg "The Android download, Appearance, and Send it to your phone")

## On a computer or Android phone

The page is headed **Heliaxis for Android**.

- **Appearance**: choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match phone** (follows your phone’s own light or dark setting).
- **Version** and release date, the file size, and **Download the app**. Opened inside the Android app, it also says **You’re up to date**, or **Update available** with the build you have (the button then says **Download update**). **What’s new** lists the changes in this version.
- **Send it to your phone**: a QR code to **Scan with your phone’s camera** (not shown on narrow screens), or **Or email yourself a download link**. Type an address you read on your phone and click **Email me the link**.
- **Installing**, **Updates** and **What works where**: the install steps, and which tools work on a phone, which need a bigger screen, and which work offline.
- **On an iPhone or iPad?**: the iPhone steps, further down.

> **Good to know:** The emailed link and the QR code are personal to you and last 7 days. They open a page with a one-tap download, with no need to sign in. If one has run out, it says **This link has expired**: send yourself a new one from this page.

## On an iPhone or iPad

The page is headed **Heliaxis for iPhone**, with the same **Appearance** choice. There’s no download: you add Heliaxis to your Home Screen from Safari, and it then works like an app. The page shows the steps, or **You’re using the Heliaxis app from your Home Screen** once you have.
