The brand guidelines page holds everything about how Heliaxis looks: the logo, colours, fonts, icons and the rules for using them. It’s the place to send a printer, a workwear supplier or an agency.

## Open it

On the main dashboard, under **Workspace**, click the **Brand guidelines** card. It isn’t in the top menu.

You need **Brand guidelines** access. An admin ticks it for you on the **Users** page; admins can always open it.

## What’s on it

- **Logo**: the wordmark on light and reversed on dark, and the spark mark.
- **Colour**: the palette with each colour’s code, such as Ink #211F18 and Solar gold #F8BC1E.
- **Typography**: the three fonts (Ezra, Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono) and what each is for.
- **Iconography**: the icon library.
- **Social & templates**: profile pictures, headers and sample posts.
- **Buttons**, **Texture** (the cross-hatch card fills), **Voice** (straplines and tone) and **Rules** (**Do** and **Don’t**).

## Download the brand kit

Scroll to **Download the brand kit** at the bottom and click a file to download it. There are logo PNGs for light and dark backgrounds, the spark and icon sheet as SVG, the card-fill textures, social profile pictures and banners, sample posts, and a print-ready PDF of the guidelines.

## Share it with someone outside Heliaxis

The page lives at heliaxis.co.uk/branding. Anyone who isn’t signed in to Heliaxis with Brand guidelines access sees **Partner access** and a **Password** box instead. Give them the partner password, they click **View brand guidelines**, and their browser remembers it for 30 days.

> **Watch out:** Everyone outside Heliaxis shares the same partner password, so only give it to people who need it.

> **Good to know:** If the page says access isn’t configured yet, no partner password has been set. Ask an admin to set one before you share the link.

For making leaflets and other artwork with these brand pieces built in, see [The design studio](/admin/help/apps/design-studio).
