The **Apps** menu holds the tools that sit alongside the CRM: the **Design studio** for quick on-brand artwork, the **Social media studio** (a separate app that opens already signed in), and **Get the app** for putting Heliaxis on your phone. The **Brand guidelines** page, with the logos and brand kit, opens from its card on the dashboard. Everyone can open **Get the app**; the others need access from an admin.
