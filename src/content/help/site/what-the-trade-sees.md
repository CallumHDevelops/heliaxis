This is the trade’s side, so you know what they’re looking at when they ring. They never log in: the link is theirs alone.

1. They get an email from Heliaxis, and a WhatsApp too if they’ve agreed to job messages on WhatsApp ([Job messages to trades on WhatsApp](/admin/help/site/whatsapp-job-messages)), with a link to subcontractor.heliaxis.co.uk. It offers to **Install the app** (“Heliaxis Site”) so it works with no signal, or they can carry on in the browser.
2. The job pack: **Notes from the office**, **When**, **People**, **Access and parking** (from the survey), **The system**, and **Drawings and design**. Everything is kept on their phone and syncs when they have signal.
3. They work through the steps: questions and photos, before, during and after. Some answers raise a flag. **Fix this** stops them signing. **Urgent** and **For the office** tell you, but they can still sign.
4. **Check and sign**: they sign the declaration below. The scaffolder signs at “up” and again at “down”. The electrician’s pack is longer (serials, inspection, string tests, switch-on, battery, certificate) and can end with **Customer sign (optional)**: “Please hand the phone back.”
5. Straight after, they see their PO on screen (**Your purchase order**, **What you’ll be paid**, the terms), or “Your PO will be sent once the office has checked one thing.”
6. Later they get **Green light ✓** (“We’ve reviewed your photos and results. Nothing needs putting right.”), or **Return to site required** with what needs putting right, the fix-by date and **Put it right**.
7. Their home screen lists their Heliaxis jobs and **Your POs and payments**.

> **Good to know:** The roof checks are the roofer’s alone: how the panels are mounted, hooks in the rafters, a solid frame, fixings made for outdoors, air behind the panels, the mounting kit’s fire rating, tiles dressed flat and the cable entry sealed. The electrician isn’t asked them again: their PV inspection record fills its Roof and panels part from the roofer’s sign-off. If the roofer signed off before these questions were added, those boxes are left blank and say so.

## The declaration they sign

The roofer’s, as an example, reads:

*I confirm that:*

- *the roofing work at (the job’s address) is complete and was carried out to the design, the manufacturer’s instructions and the standards required by my Subcontractor Agreement with Heliaxis Limited;*
- *the answers, photos and readings I have given are true and accurate; and*
- *I have told Heliaxis about any defect, damage or problem I found or caused.*

*I understand Heliaxis will rely on this declaration to raise my purchase order and complete the customer’s certification and handover, and will review my photos and results. If anything needs putting right, I will return to site to fix it under my Subcontractor Agreement.*

Each trade’s declaration names their own work (scaffolding, roofing, electrical). A put right has the same wording for “the work to put right”, with what you asked for quoted.

What happens in the office when they sign is in [Sign-offs and the automatic labour PO](/admin/help/site/sign-offs-and-labour-pos).
