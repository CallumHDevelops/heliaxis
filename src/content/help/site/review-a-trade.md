After a trade signs off, their PO goes and a review task is made for the job owner ([Sign-offs and the automatic labour PO](/admin/help/site/sign-offs-and-labour-pos)). This is how you do the review.

1. On the Site tab the trade’s card says **Waiting for review** and the button is **Review their work**, with the number of open flags. You can also open it from the **Open flags** panel. From the review task, open the job’s **Site** tab and press **Review their work**; the notification opens the Site tab for you.
2. The review drawer opens with **Your review** at the top: “Their PO has gone. Check the photos and answers, then give it the green light, or ask them to return to site to put something right.”
3. Look through the tabs:
   - **Answers**: each step, where each answer came from, and flags with **Resolve** and “What was done (optional)”;
   - **Photos**: by slot; tap one for full screen and **Download**;
   - **Serials and reads**: label photos read for you; press **Confirm** once you’ve checked them against the photo;
   - **Sign-off**: who signed, when, the phone and the declaration;
   - **PO**: the money;
   - **Events**: the history.
4. All good? Press **Green light**. They’re told (late in the evening it waits for the morning). If flags are still open, it asks first.

![The review drawer for Dave Price, with Green light, Return to site required and the PO tab.](/help/site/s16-review-drawer-po.jpg "The review drawer: Your review (Green light or Return to site required) and the PO tab with the money.")

Something to put right? Press **Return to site required** instead: see [Ask a trade to return to site](/admin/help/site/return-to-site).

> **Good to know:** The scaffolder’s “up” and “down” are reviewed separately.
