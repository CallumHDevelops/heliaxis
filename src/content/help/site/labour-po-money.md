A labour PO is the agreed rate × the quantity. The quantity is the panels the roofer signed for, 1 for a fixed price, or the agreed days or hours.

## Worked example: Price Roofing

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| £30.00 per panel × 16 panels | £480.00 |
| Not VAT registered: no VAT | £0.00 |
| CIS verified (net): 20% deducted | −£96.00 |
| **What they’ll be paid** | **£384.00** |

## VAT

VAT comes from their registration. Not registered means no VAT. Otherwise it’s reverse charge, VAT 20% or zero-rated, as chosen when they were added ([Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade)).

## CIS

- CIS is only ever taken from labour, never from VAT or materials.
- Gross status is 0%, net is 20%, higher is 30%, and it’s 30% if nobody has verified them with HMRC.
- Record the verification on **Team › Subcontractors ›** their firm **› CIS verification**.
- CIS is rounded down to the penny, so they’re never short.

> **Watch out:** A labour PO is frozen as it was issued. To change the money, change the rate on the Site tab (reopen the sign-off first if the PO has gone), then press **Issue again**. See [When a labour PO is held instead of sent](/admin/help/site/held-labour-pos).

For where labour POs sit beside the supplier orders on a job, see [Labour POs](/admin/help/purchase-orders/labour-pos).
