A held PO waits on the Site tab and in Orders as **Held**, with what it needs.

## A missing detail

The PO says what’s missing, for example:

- “Add the agreed rate to issue the PO”
- “No quantity to charge yet: add it on the Site tab”
- “Choose the VAT for this PO on the Site tab”
- nobody to send it to, usually worded like “No email for them, and WhatsApp can’t go (Dave hasn’t said they’re happy to get job messages on WhatsApp): add an email to send the PO”

Fix the detail, then press **Issue PO**.

## Something to check

The roofer’s panel count, for example “The roofer signed for 18 panels but the design has 16: check the count before issuing” (or “…but their strings add up to…” when the design has no count). Check, then press **Issue anyway**.

## Holds from site settings

Two holds come from site settings: an Urgent flag, or the scaffold not down yet. They’re off unless Callum switches them on. Check, then press **Issue anyway**.

> **Good to know:** Holding a PO when there’s an Urgent flag, holding the scaffold PO until it’s down, a VAT default and location stamps on photos are all off by default. They have no screen yet: ask Callum if you need one switched on.

## Other buttons

- **Raise the PO again** if it was cancelled or deleted in Orders.
- **Reopen sign-off** lets them change answers and sign again from the same link. The PO stays; issue it again if the money changes.

> **Watch out:** **Issue again** is always there once a PO has gone: on the trade’s card, in the review drawer and on the PO’s sheet. Pressing it sends them a new version that says it replaces the one already sent, so only press it after changing the money. When it’s needed, the card says **Changed since it was sent: issue it again**.

> **Watch out:** A labour PO is frozen as it was issued. To change the money, change the rate on the Site tab (reopen the sign-off first if the PO has gone), then press **Issue again**.
