When a trade signs, the app does all of this by itself:

1. Their sign-off is saved with their signature, the time, the phone and the declaration they signed.
2. A **labour PO** is raised: the agreed rate × the quantity. The quantity is the panels the roofer signed for, 1 for a fixed price, or the agreed days or hours. It’s emailed with the PDF straight away, at any hour, and sent on WhatsApp too if they’ve agreed to job messages on WhatsApp. If neither can reach them, it’s held (see [When a labour PO is held instead of sent](/admin/help/site/held-labour-pos)).
3. The job owner gets a notification, such as “Roofer signed off at 12 High St · PO-0105 · £384.00 to pay”, and the job’s Activity says the same. Urgent flags are in the same notification.
4. A review task is made, such as “Review Dave Price’s roofing photos, HX-0042”, due the next working day.
5. Their report PDF is made. The scaffolder’s sign-offs fill the scaffold dates. The electrician’s marks the job commissioned and moves it to Paperwork & handover (see [How a job flows](/admin/help/projects/how-a-job-flows), step 8).

![The Site tab after the roofer signs: Done, PO-0105 issued and waiting for review, one open flag.](/help/site/s15-site-tab-waiting-for-review.jpg "After the roofer signs: Done, his PO issued and waiting for review, one flag, and Review their work (1 flag).")

> **Good to know:** In these pictures, “Panel model AIKO-A450-MAH54Mw isn’t the design’s (Aiko Neostar 2P 450W)” is a flag the app raised by itself. The roofer typed in the model from the panel label, and it didn’t match the design. It’s a typical example of something to check in your review.
