Once a trade is on the job ([Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade)), send them their job pack from their card on the **Site** tab.

1. On their card, press **Send the job pack**. It goes by email, and by WhatsApp too if they’ve agreed to job messages on WhatsApp (see [Job messages to trades on WhatsApp](/admin/help/site/whatsapp-job-messages)).
2. Or press **Copy link** and paste it yourself, or **WhatsApp from my phone**, which opens WhatsApp on your phone with the words ready.

## Check it went

- The card shows what went: “Job pack sent by email 10:42”, “waiting to send at 07:00” (job packs sent late in the evening wait for the morning), or “didn’t go” with **Send again**.
- If no WhatsApp went, the note at the top of the tab says why, for example “Dave hasn’t said they’re happy to get job messages on WhatsApp, so it went by email.”
- The status pill moves through **Not sent**, **Sent**, **Opened**, **Started**, **Signed off** and **Done**. You’ll also see lines such as “1 phone has opened this link”, “Last synced 5 min ago” and a photo count.

![The Site tab after sending job packs, with cards for the scaffolder, roofer and electrician.](/help/site/s12-site-tab-job-packs-sent.jpg "The Site tab after sending: three cards (Sent or Opened), what went and when, the job strip, and the electrician’s certificate card.")

The Site tab refreshes every 30 seconds while it’s open.

## Change something after it’s gone

Press **Edit**, make the change and press **Save**. Their job pack updates on their phone by itself the next time it syncs.

**Send the job pack again** won’t send a second message on the same link (the note may still say “Sent by email”, but that’s the first one), so tell them with **WhatsApp from my phone** or a call.

> **Good to know:** The Ready to book checklist item “Electrician and subcontractors confirmed, RAMS issued” ticks itself once every trade has had their pack. The strip across the top shows who’s done what: Scaffold, Roof, Electrics, Handover, each ticked as it’s finished.

> **Watch out:** **New link** makes a fresh link, and the old one stops working at once on every phone. Use it if a link went to the wrong person. **Take off the job** stops their link straight away.

What happens next on their side is in [What the trade sees on the site app](/admin/help/site/what-the-trade-sees).
