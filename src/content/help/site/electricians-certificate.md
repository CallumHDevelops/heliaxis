Under the electrician’s card on the Site tab, once they’ve had their pack, there’s an **Electrical certificate** card. You can see it at the foot of the Site tab picture in [Send the job pack](/admin/help/site/send-the-job-pack).

- It shows the certificate’s state and what’s still missing.
- **Sign the design part** (EIC route): Heliaxis’s designer signs the design section and the next inspection. On a solar or battery job that one signature also signs the customer’s system summary.
- **Download the EIC** (or the draft), **Print version** and **Look at it as it stands**. If it says “Changed since the certificate was made”, press **Make it again**.
- **Photo record (PDF)** makes a PDF of all their photos when you ask for it.
- If their certificate isn’t one the app knows, it says “…they can’t finish. Choose which certificate they’re doing.” Press **Change it**.

Which certificate they do is chosen when you add them: see [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade).

## Notify building control

Do this within 30 days of finishing. First choose who notifies: **Their own scheme** (with the scheme and registration number) or **Heliaxis**.

### Heliaxis notifies

1. Copy the values into the scheme’s portal with each **Copy** button: **Installation address**, **Type of work**, **Completion date** and **Certificate reference**.
2. Fill in **Notified on** and **Reference**.
3. Press **Save**.

### Their own scheme notifies

The card says “Ask (their first name) to notify (their scheme) … and send you the reference”, with no values to copy.

1. When they send the reference, fill in **Notified on** and **Reference**.
2. Press **Save**.

> **Good to know:** The paperwork deadline **Notify Building Regs (NICEIC / NAPIT)** runs 30 days from commissioning. See [Paperwork deadlines at a glance](/admin/help/projects/deadlines).
