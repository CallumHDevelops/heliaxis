Trades get their job pack, PO and review messages by email. To send them on WhatsApp as well, tick **Happy to get job messages on WhatsApp** for that person, once they’ve said yes:

- For a new person: on **+ New person** when you add them to a job ([Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade)).
- For someone already set up: on **Team › Subcontractors ›** their firm **› Site contacts**.

Their card on the Site tab then shows “WhatsApp OK”.

## When a WhatsApp goes

A WhatsApp only goes when all four are true:

1. they have a mobile;
2. **Happy to get job messages on WhatsApp** is ticked for them;
3. WhatsApp is connected;
4. that message’s template is approved (**CRM › WhatsApp templates**, at [/admin/enquiries/templates](/admin/enquiries/templates)).

Otherwise it goes by email only, and the Site tab says why, for example “Dave hasn’t said they’re happy to get job messages on WhatsApp, so it went by email.”

> **Watch out:** If they reply STOP, job messages on WhatsApp switch off for them and go by email only, and the job owner gets a notification. If they later reply START, tick it again on their site contact.

> **Watch out:** No email for them? Then nothing can reach them unless WhatsApp can go. Add an email on their firm’s **Site contacts** (**Edit**).

> **Once switched on:** A subcontractor’s **Site contacts** need site-commissioning.sql.
