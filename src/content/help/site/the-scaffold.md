The scaffolder signs off twice on their phone: when the scaffold is up and when it’s down.

## When it’s up

- When the scaffolder signs it off as up and tagged, the roofer’s and electrician’s cards get a button such as **Tell Dave (roofer) they’re clear to start**.
- If it’s up but not tagged, the scaffolder’s card has **Tell the roofer and electrician not to use it**.

## When it’s down

The scaffolder signs again when it’s down, with their “strike” photos. Reminders go if those photos don’t arrive.

## What the app fills in

The scaffolder’s sign-offs fill **Scaffold up** and **Scaffold down** in [Dates and team](/admin/help/projects/dates-and-team) by themselves, if they’re empty. The Complete checklist item **Scaffold removed** ticks itself after the down date.

> **Good to know:** The scaffolder’s “up” and “down” are reviewed separately. See [Review a trade’s work](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade).
