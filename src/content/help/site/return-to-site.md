Use this from the review drawer when something needs putting right (see [Review a trade’s work and give the green light](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade)).

## Send the notice

1. Press **Return to site required**.
2. Fill in **What needs putting right**, for example “Two tiles are left lifted above the left of the array. Dress them back flat.”
3. Use **Point to their photos (optional)** if it helps.
4. Check **Put right by**, which starts at 7 working days.
5. Tick **It affects safety or the home (the agreement gives 7 working days)**, if it does.
6. Press **Send return to site**.

It goes now as the written notice (clause 12.3), by email, and by WhatsApp too if they’ve agreed to job messages on WhatsApp. (The form’s own line says “by email and WhatsApp”; the WhatsApp only goes if it can.) Their PO shows **On hold: return to site required**.

![The Return to site required form with what needs putting right, a photo picked, the date and the safety tick.](/help/site/s17-return-to-site-form.jpg "Return to site required: what needs putting right, one of his photos picked, put right by, and the safety tick.")

## When they’ve put it right

They put it right on their phone: what they did, **Photos of the fix**, the declaration and their signature.

![The roofer’s phone: Return to site required, the fix-by date, Put it right and the PO on hold.](/help/site/s18-trade-return-to-site-phone.jpg "What the roofer sees: Return to site required, the fix-by date, Put it right, and his PO on hold.")

Their card then says **Put right: waiting for review**, and a new task “Review … put right photos” is made, due the next working day.

![The roofer’s card in the office: Put right: waiting for review, and the PO on hold.](/help/site/s18b-site-tab-put-right-waiting.jpg "Back in the office: the roofer’s card says Put right: waiting for review, and his PO is on hold.")

1. Press **Review their work**. Under **Your review** you’ll see **Asked:**, **What they did:** and the photos of the fix.
2. Press **Green light**, which lifts the hold, or **Return to site required** again (“Their put right isn’t enough…”).

**History: n returns to site** keeps every notice as it went, the put right and its declaration.

> **Good to know:** If the notice didn’t reach them, the card says so, for example “Return to site by email didn’t go: …” with **Send again**. In the review drawer, **Your review** says “The written notice hasn’t reached them” with **Send the email again** or **Send the WhatsApp again**. Call them too.

> **Good to know:** Return-to-site notices go at once, even late in the evening.
