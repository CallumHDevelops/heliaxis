A project is the same record as the sale. Nothing is copied: the signed quote, deposit, survey, photos, documents, WhatsApp and emails are already on it. Every job from **Won** to **Complete** is a project. It keeps its reference, such as HX-0042. A second job for the same customer becomes HX-0042-2.

## The delivery stages

These are the stages in order, as the screens name them.

1. **Won**: the customer signs the quote in their portal.
2. **Design & approvals**
3. **Ready to book**
4. **Install booked**: an install date is saved. The app moves the job here by itself.
5. **Installing**: the install day arrives. The app moves the job here by itself.
6. **Commissioning**
7. **Paperwork & handover**: the electrician signs off, or the pack is sent. The app moves the job here by itself.
8. **Complete**

A job can be marked **Lost** at any point.

## The journey

1. **Won.** The customer signs the quote in their portal and the app moves the job to Won by itself, or you move it yourself. It now appears on Operations › Projects. Check the deposit and the survey are linked: the Won checklist ticks itself when it can see them.
2. **Design & approvals.** Attach the final design (an OpenSolar or SimpliHeat link, or a Design document) and fill in **System details** ([System details and design details](/admin/help/projects/system-details)). Decide the **DNO route** (G98 or G99) under Paperwork, and apply for G99 if it’s needed ([Paperwork and deadlines](/admin/help/projects/paperwork-and-deadlines)). Order kit from the **Orders** tab ([Order kit for a job](/admin/help/purchase-orders/order-kit)).
3. **Ready to book.** G99 approved if it was needed, kit ordered, scaffold booked. Add the trades on the **Site** tab and send them their job pack ([Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade)). The **Handover** tab appears now: start its **Before the install day** list ([The Handover tab](/admin/help/handover/the-handover-tab)).
4. **Install booked.** Put the install date in **Dates and team** ([Dates and team](/admin/help/projects/dates-and-team)). An install date on a job at Won, Design & approvals or Ready to book moves it to Install booked by itself. The customer’s countdown messages start, and with Microsoft 365 connected the install goes into Outlook.
5. **Installing.** On the install day the app moves the job to Installing by itself. The trades work through their job pack on their phones, and their photos land on the job.
6. **Sign-offs.** Each trade signs off on their phone. The app then raises and sends their labour PO, makes a review task for the job owner and sends the owner a notification, all by itself. The scaffolder signs twice: when the scaffold is up and when it’s down. See [Sign-offs and the automatic labour PO](/admin/help/site/sign-offs-and-labour-pos).
7. **Your review.** Check each trade’s photos and answers, then press **Green light** or **Return to site required**. A return to site puts their PO on hold until they’ve put it right. See [Review a trade’s work](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade).
8. **Commissioning, then Paperwork & handover.** When the electrician signs off, the app marks the job commissioned and moves it to **Paperwork & handover** by itself. The paperwork deadlines start (MCS, G98, Building Regs) and a “Handover call” task is made. The electrician’s test records and certificate drop into the handover pack.
9. **Handover pack.** Fill the gaps on the **Handover** tab (MCS certificate, warranty insurance, wiring diagram and so on), then press **Send to customer** ([Send the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/send-the-pack)). The pack goes into their portal. Sending also moves the job to Paperwork & handover by itself if it isn’t there yet. Then upload the final invoice and record the payment ([The final invoice and payments](/admin/help/handover/final-invoice)).
10. **Complete.** Final invoice paid, scaffold down, review request sent. Move the job to **Complete** ([Finish a job](/admin/help/projects/finish-a-job)).

> **Good to know:** You can always move a job yourself, with the stage button or **Move to …** on the checklist. The app only ever moves a job forward, and clearing a date never moves it back.
