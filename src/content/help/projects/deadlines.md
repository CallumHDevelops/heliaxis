Each deadline becomes a task 2 working days before it’s due (the BUS voucher: 14 days before). A deadline that has passed puts the job in **Needs attention** on the Projects page. To record the paperwork, see [Paperwork and deadlines](/admin/help/projects/paperwork-and-deadlines).

| Deadline | When | Closes when |
|---|---|---|
| Register the MCS certificate | 10 working days after commissioning | The MCS certificate number is entered |
| Tell the DNO (G98) about the new system | 28 days after commissioning (G98 jobs) | The Commissioning item “G98 notice or G99 commissioning form sent to the DNO” is ticked or Not needed. On the Handover tab, **Mark it sent to the DNO** ticks it. |
| Notify Building Regs (NICEIC / NAPIT) | 30 days after commissioning | The Building Regs reference is entered |
| Buy the insurance-backed guarantee | 7 days after the MCS certificate is registered | The guarantee reference or the Guarantee registered date is saved |
| Chase the DNO about the G99 application | 45 working days after it was submitted | The DNO status is Approved |
| BUS voucher expires | When the voucher runs out (3 months from issue unless a date is saved) | The redeemed date is entered |
