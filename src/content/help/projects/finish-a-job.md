A job is ready to finish once the final invoice is paid, the scaffold is down and the review request has gone.

1. Work through the Complete checklist on the Overview:
   - **Final invoice paid**: ticked when you record the final payment (see [The final invoice and payments](/admin/help/handover/final-invoice));
   - **Scaffold removed**: ticks itself after the down date;
   - **Review request sent**: ticks itself.
2. Move the job to **Complete**, with the stage button or **Move to …** on the checklist.

The job leaves Live and shows under **Complete** on the Projects page ([The Projects list](/admin/help/projects/projects-list)).

> **Good to know:** On the Board, **Complete** at the end opens the completed jobs in the List.
