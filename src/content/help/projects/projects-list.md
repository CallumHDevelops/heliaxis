Across the top of the Projects page: **Projects**, the **Sales** | **Projects** switch, a summary such as “8 live · 2 need attention”, the search box (**Name, postcode, phone or HX-ref**), **List**, **Schedule** and **Board**, **Purchase orders** and **+ New project**. The page refreshes itself every minute and remembers the view you used last.

> **Once switched on:** The **Purchase orders** button appears once purchase-orders.sql has been run.

The page opens on the List. For the other two views, see [The Schedule and the Board](/admin/help/projects/schedule-and-board).

## Use the List

1. Choose what to show: **Live**, **Needs attention**, **On hold**, **Mine**, **Complete** or **Lost**. Each shows its count.
2. Narrow by product if you want to: **Solar PV**, **Battery**, **Heat pump**, **EV charger**, **Infrared heating**, **LED lighting**.
3. Read the rows in three groups: **Needs attention**, **Coming up** (by next date) and **Not booked**.
4. Tap a row to open the job.

The columns are **Ref**, **Customer and job**, **Stage**, **Next date** (install days and the team, or a survey), **Checklist** (this stage’s ticks, such as 3/5, amber when a key item isn’t done) and **Flags**.

![The Projects list with Needs attention, Coming up and Not booked groups.](/help/projects/s03-projects-list.jpg "The List: Needs attention, Coming up and Not booked, with the stage, next date, checklist and flags for each job.")

The **Needs attention** count (and “2 need attention” in the summary) includes jobs on hold and complete jobs. The Live list shows only live ones, so its Needs attention group can be shorter than the count. That’s why the picture shows 2 but lists 1.

## What puts a job in Needs attention

- A deposit still unpaid 7 days after the job started.
- The DNO quiet for 45 working days on a G99 application.
- A paperwork deadline that has passed.
- A BUS voucher expiring within 30 days.

Other flags are just information: **On hold**, **Deposit due**, **DNO waiting N days**, a paperwork deadline coming up, **Possible duplicate**.

> **Good to know:** **Mine** is the jobs you own, meaning you’re the **Owner** on Job details.

To add a job that isn’t on the list yet, see [Start a project](/admin/help/projects/start-a-project).
