## The next step

On a job’s page, the next step sits in the middle with the tabs.

- If there’s no next step you’ll see **+ Add a next step so this doesn’t go quiet**.
- Otherwise you’ll see the current one with **✓ Done**, **Snooze ▾** (**Later today**, **Tomorrow 9am**, **In 3 days**, **Next Monday**) and **Edit**.

The next step turns red when it’s overdue.

## Add a task

1. On the **Tasks** card on the right, press **+ Task**.
2. Type **What needs doing?**
3. Pick **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Fri** (or a date) and who it’s for.
4. Press **Add task**.

Tick a task to finish it. On a phone, the job shows up to two tasks that are due today or overdue, and **All tasks (n)** for the rest.

The **+** button at the bottom right adds a **Task**, **Note**, **Log a call**, **Next step**, **Photos**, **Site visit**, **Email** or **Move stage**. The green button opens the customer’s WhatsApp.

## Tasks the app makes for you

- Paperwork deadlines, 2 working days before they’re due (the BUS voucher: 14 days before). See [Paperwork deadlines at a glance](/admin/help/projects/deadlines).
- “Review Dave Price’s roofing photos, HX-0042” when a trade signs off, and “Review Dave Price’s put right photos, HX-0042” when they send a put right. Both are due the next working day. See [Review a trade’s work](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade).
- From the electrician’s sign-off: “Handover call: go through the pack … with Jane”, due the next working day; “Set up monitoring remotely” when it couldn’t be done on site; and for an EV charger or heat pump, a DNO task such as “Notify the DNO about the EV charger (connect and notify)” or “Check the DNO accepted the heat pump application”, depending on the electrician’s answer.
- For the handover pack: “Upload the MCS certificate into the handover pack” once the MCS number is in, “Upload the warranty insurance policy into the handover pack” once the guarantee reference is in, and “Upload the Building Regulations certificate into the handover pack” once the Building Regs reference is in. From Ready to book: “Upload the wiring diagram for the handover pack” and “Upload the design calculations for the handover pack (or mark them not needed)”, due the working day before the install, and on G99 jobs “Upload the G99 application, the DNO’s acceptance and the installation document”. Each closes itself when its item is Complete or Not needed.
- “Send the final invoice”, when the handover pack goes.

> **Once switched on:** The handover pack tasks need handover.sql.

## Metis

Metis can add a note to a job, set its next step, create tasks for anyone, add an opportunity, and draft emails and WhatsApps. Drafts are only drafts: nothing goes without you.

Try: “Called Jane Smith: she wants the battery in the garage. Remind me Friday to confirm with the electrician.” What she did shows as chips under her reply.

> **Watch out:** Metis doesn’t send job packs, raise POs or move stages. Do those on the job.

For next steps and tasks on a job that’s still in sales, see [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks). For the tasks the app adds by itself, see [Tasks the app makes by itself](/admin/help/tasks/automatic-tasks).
