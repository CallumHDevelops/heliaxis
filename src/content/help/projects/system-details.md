Both cards are on a job’s Overview. Fill them in during Design & approvals.

## System details

On phones this card is folded; tap it to open it.

1. Fill in what applies: **System size (kWp)**, **Panels**, **Panel model**, **Inverter**, **Inverter (kW)**, **Battery**, **Battery usable capacity (kWh)**, heat pump, EV charger, **Export limit (kW)**, **MPAN** and **Monitoring**. Only the fields for the job’s products show.
2. Press **Save system**.

## Design details for site paperwork

This card is closed until you open it. It holds what the trades’ forms and the handover documents need, such as strings, optimisers, mounting, battery class and back-up.

1. Open **Design details for site paperwork**.
2. Fill in the figures from the design.
3. Press **Save design details**.

Empty stays empty: the site app and the documents never guess.

> **Good to know:** Purchase orders and the handover pack are filled in from these details, so fill them in before you order kit. See [Order kit for a job](/admin/help/purchase-orders/order-kit).

> **Once switched on:** System details need crm-v3.sql. See [What needs switching on](/admin/help/projects/what-needs-switching-on).
