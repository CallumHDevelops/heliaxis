On a computer the header runs across the top. The customer is on the left, the next step and the tabs are in the middle, and **Tasks** is on the right (or inside the WhatsApp panel when that’s open). The page updates itself every 30 seconds, so a colleague’s change appears by itself.

![A project page for Jane Smith at Install booked, with the header, stage path, next step, tiles and Tasks.](/help/projects/s07-project-page-overview.jpg "A project’s page: header and stage path, next step, Everything for this job tiles, the Handover pack card, and Tasks.")

## The header

- **← Projects** takes you back. Tap the customer’s name for their details.
- Under the name: the reference, products, postcode, value and the next date (for example “Install Tue 3 Nov · 2 days”), and **Other projects (n)** if they have more jobs.
- **Call**, **Directions** and **Email**.
- The stage button (such as **Install booked ▾**) opens the stage list. The main button beside it does the stage’s next move, usually something like **Move to Installing →**. If key checklist items aren’t done, it says **Move to Installing anyway** and asks you once.
- The stage path shows **Sold ✓** (the sales stages, folded up), then the delivery stages. Tap a step to move there. It asks first.
- **⋯** has **New project for Jane**, **Merge with another customer…**, **Copy link to this job** and, for admins, **Delete this job**.
- Badges you may see: **On hold: reason**, **Lost**, and “Looks like the same person as …”.

> **Admins only:** **Delete this job** is only in the **⋯** menu for admins.

For the next step and the Tasks card, see [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).

## The left column

- **Customer**: mobile, WhatsApp permission, email and site address.
- **Job details** (press **✎ Edit**): **Job name**, **Products**, **Value £ (inc. VAT)**, **Owner**, **Site address**, **Site postcode**, **OpenSolar project link**, **SimpliHeat project link**, **On hold** (with “Why?”) and **Notes**. Putting a job on hold takes it off the Live list and stops its countdowns.
- **Customer portal**, **Quote & deposit** and **Grid connection (DNO)**. The DNO status is **Not started**, **Applied — waiting**, **Approved**, **Rejected / changes** or **Not needed**, and it shows on the customer’s tracker.

## On a phone

You get the header, a one-line stage bar with the main button, the next step, up to two tasks that are due today or overdue, and **All tasks (n)** for the rest, then the tabs. The customer column is under the **Details** tab.

The tab bar scrolls sideways. Swipe it to reach **Site**, **Handover** and **Details**.

![Jane Smith’s job on a phone, with Move to Installing, the next step, tasks and the tab bar.](/help/projects/s10-project-page-phone.jpg "A job on a phone: stage line with Move to Installing, next step, tasks, then the tabs.")

For what each tab holds and when it appears, see [A job’s tabs](/admin/help/projects/project-tabs). For the same page while a job is still in sales, see [A customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/customer-page).
