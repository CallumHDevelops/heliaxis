## On a computer

- **Operations** in the header has **Projects** and **RAMS**. **Finance** has **Purchase orders**.
- Each menu shows only what you have access to. Without RAMS access, Operations has just Projects; with RAMS access only, it has just RAMS. It’s still called Operations either way.
- To search from any page, use the search box in the header, or press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac). It finds customers and jobs by name, postcode, phone or HX-ref.
- The addresses haven’t changed ([/admin/projects](/admin/projects) and [/admin/projects/orders](/admin/projects/orders)), so old bookmarks still work.

## On a phone

- Tap **More**. Under **Operations** you’ll find **Projects**. Under **Finance** you’ll find **Orders**, which is Purchase orders.
- **RAMS** is on the bottom bar, one tap away on site. You only see it if you have RAMS access.
- The header has no search box. Use the one at the top of the Dashboard, the CRM or the Projects page.

![The phone More sheet with Projects under Operations, Orders under Finance, and RAMS on the bottom bar.](/help/projects/s02-phone-more-menu.jpg "On a phone, tap More: Projects is under Operations and Orders under Finance, side by side. RAMS stays on the bottom bar.")

> **Good to know:** The More sheet only shows group headings (Operations, Finance and so on) when you have more than six tiles. With fewer, it’s one grid. Headings with only one tile under them share a row, which is why Operations and Finance sit side by side.

## From the CRM

The **Sales** | **Projects** switch at the top of the CRM goes straight to Projects.

## Who can see them

Projects and Purchase orders need **CRM** access. RAMS needs **RAMS** access. An admin ticks these on Users.

> **Once switched on:** **Purchase orders** appears in the Finance menu once purchase-orders.sql has been run. See [What needs switching on](/admin/help/projects/what-needs-switching-on).
