RAMS is our separate app for risk assessments and method statements.

- On a computer it sits under **Operations** with Projects (on its own there if you have RAMS access but not CRM).
- On a phone, **RAMS** is on the bottom bar.

## Signing in

RAMS signs you in with one tap. The first time, you prove your email with a code sent to it. You need RAMS access here, and a RAMS account with the same email.

> **Once switched on:** One-tap sign-in needs sso.sql and the RAMS keys set in Vercel. Without the keys, RAMS opens at its own sign-in. With the keys but not the file, you get **Open RAMS without signing in**, where asking for a code says sso.sql needs running; use **open RAMS and sign in as usual** on that page. See [What needs switching on](/admin/help/projects/what-needs-switching-on).

## Link a job to its RAMS project

1. Open the job and go to **Overview › Dates and team › Subcontractors**.
2. Paste the **RAMS project ID** (from the RAMS app).
3. Press **Save dates**.

The firms booked on it and their status show underneath. You can see this in the picture in [Dates and team](/admin/help/projects/dates-and-team).

**Open RAMS** beside the ID opens the RAMS app, signed in, at its usual start page, whether or not an ID has been saved.

> **Watch out:** **Open RAMS** doesn’t go straight to the project. Find it there.

## The checklist item

The Ready to book checklist item “Electrician and subcontractors confirmed, RAMS issued” ticks itself when the RAMS project ID is in and every firm has confirmed its crew, or when every trade on the Site tab has had their job pack ([Send the job pack](/admin/help/site/send-the-job-pack)).

## Our team

**Team › Our team** opens the Heliaxis operatives in RAMS.

For opening RAMS from the menu, and why it might ask you to sign in, see [Open RAMS and the social media studio](/admin/help/getting-started/open-rams-and-social).
