## The Docs tab

1. Choose the type: **Quote**, **Invoice**, **DNO**, **MCS certificate**, **Warranty**, **Handover pack**, **Design**, **Contract**, **Receipt** or **Other**. Once switched on there are also **Commissioning**, **Certificate** and **Purchase order**.
2. Set **Show in their portal** on or off.
3. Press **⬆ Upload**. PDFs, photos, Word and Excel files all work.
4. Once switched on, an MCS certificate, warranty, invoice or receipt asks **Add it to the handover pack as**, with the item already chosen and ticked.

Each document then has **👁 In portal** or **Private**, **✍ Needs signing** or **No signing** (quotes and contracts), **Mark as signed** (for one signed on paper), **💬 Send** (WhatsApp), **Move into the handover pack** and **✕** to delete. Documents the app made also have **Print version** (see [Digital and print versions of documents](/admin/help/projects/print-versions)).

Older versions of documents the app made are kept. **Archived (n)** shows them.

![The Docs tab listing documents the app made, each with Print version, In portal and Send.](/help/projects/o4-docs-tab-print-version.jpg "The Docs tab: documents the app made, each with Print version, In portal, Send and the handover pack item it fills.")

> **Watch out:** A quote signed in the portal can’t be deleted or changed. It’s the contract evidence. To get a quote signed, use **Send quote for signing** on the portal card, not the upload.

> **Once switched on:** The **Commissioning**, **Certificate** and **Purchase order** types need site-commissioning.sql. **Add it to the handover pack as** needs handover.sql.

## Photos

The categories are **Survey**, **Site**, **Progress**, **Snags**, **Completion** and **Other**. Trades’ photos from the site app arrive under **Site**, which the customer doesn’t see. Progress and Completion photos show in the customer’s portal.

## Surveys

Press **Start solar survey** or **Add heat pump survey (SimpliHeat)**. Each survey shows its status, surveyor and red flags, with **Report →** once it’s finished.

> **Once switched on:** The **Surveys** tab and tile, and **Start solar survey**, need surveys.sql.
