The **Dates and team** card is on a job’s Overview.

![The Dates and team card with survey, install, team, scaffold dates and the RAMS project ID.](/help/projects/s08b-dates-and-team.jpg "Dates and team: survey, install start and days, team, scaffold dates, and the RAMS project ID with Open RAMS.")

## What’s on the card

- **Survey** (or **Home visit** for jobs with no solar, battery or heat pump): **Date and time**, **Send booking link** and **Find a time**.
- **Install**: **Starts** (date and time), **Working days**, **Team** (tap who’s going), **Scaffold up**, **Scaffold down**, **Commissioned** and **Handed over**.
- **Subcontractors**: **RAMS project ID** and **Open RAMS** (see [RAMS](/admin/help/projects/rams)).

## Change a date

1. Change anything on the card.
2. Press **Save dates** (or **Cancel**).

The lines underneath say who’s told (“They’ll be told on WhatsApp”) and whether it’s in Outlook (“In the Outlook calendar ✓”, or “Goes into Outlook once Microsoft 365 is connected”).

An install date on a job at Won, Design & approvals or Ready to book moves it to Install booked by itself, and the customer’s countdown messages start. Clearing a date never moves a job back.

## Dates the app fills in

The scaffolder’s sign-offs fill **Scaffold up** and **Scaffold down** by themselves if they’re empty. The electrician’s sign-off fills **Commissioned** by itself. See [Sign-offs and the automatic labour PO](/admin/help/site/sign-offs-and-labour-pos).

> **Good to know:** The **Schedule** view on the Projects page shows these installs and scaffold dates for the next 8 weeks. See [The Schedule and the Board](/admin/help/projects/schedule-and-board).
