Everything Heliaxis makes comes in two versions from the same layout.

- The **digital** one, with the cream page and dark bands, is what’s emailed, put in the portal and put in the handover pack.
- Beside it is **Print version**: “The same document without the cream page and dark bands, to save ink when you print it.” It’s made when you open it, and never stored or sent.

![Purchase order PO-0101 as emailed, with a dark header and cream page, beside its print version on white.](/help/projects/o5-po-digital-and-print-versions.jpg "PO-0101 as emailed (digital, left) and its Print version (right): the same layout without the cream page or dark bands.")

## Where you’ll find Print version

- The Docs tab ([Documents, photos and surveys on a job](/admin/help/projects/documents-photos-surveys)).
- The Handover tab: each document and the pack line ([The Handover tab](/admin/help/handover/the-handover-tab)).
- A PO’s sheet, by **Download PDF** ([Labour POs](/admin/help/purchase-orders/labour-pos)).
- The review drawer’s PO tab ([Review a trade’s work](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade)).
- The electrician’s certificate card ([The electrician’s certificate](/admin/help/site/electricians-certificate)).
- The customer’s portal.
- The trade’s PO on their phone. That one needs signal.

> **Good to know:** Uploads and other people’s documents (a certificate the electrician brought, a manual) have no print version.
