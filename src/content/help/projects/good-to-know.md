## Everything stays up to date

Projects refreshes every minute and a job page every 30 seconds. The Site and Handover tabs refresh every 30 seconds while they’re open. A colleague’s change appears by itself.

## The evening rule

Messages to trades and customers sent after 20:30 wait until 07:00. POs and return-to-site notices are the exception: they go at once.

You’ll see this on the Site tab as “waiting to send at 07:00” ([Send the job pack](/admin/help/site/send-the-job-pack)), and when you give a trade the green light late in the evening ([Review a trade’s work](/admin/help/site/review-a-trade)). When you send the handover pack outside sending hours, the message waits but the documents go in now ([Send the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/send-the-pack)).

## Admins only

> **Admins only:** On a job, only an admin can use:
>
> - **Delete this job** ([A project’s page](/admin/help/projects/project-page)).
> - **Send anyway (admin)** on the handover pack ([Send the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/send-the-pack)).
> - Marking a site item Not needed in the handover pack ([Upload and manage handover documents](/admin/help/handover/upload-and-manage-documents)).
> - Removing a payment ([The final invoice and payments](/admin/help/handover/final-invoice)).

## Site settings

Holding a PO when there’s an Urgent flag, holding the scaffold PO until it’s down, a VAT default and location stamps on photos are all off by default. They have no screen yet: ask Callum if you need one switched on. See [When a labour PO is held instead of sent](/admin/help/site/held-labour-pos).
