Both views are on the Projects page. Press **Schedule** or **Board** at the top; the page remembers the view you used last. For the List, see [The Projects list](/admin/help/projects/projects-list).

## Schedule

The next 8 weeks from this Monday, one row per week. Each day shows **Install** (or **Install 1/2** and **Install 2/2** for a two-day job) with the team, plus **Scaffold up** and **Scaffold down**. Today is marked, and quiet weeks say “nothing booked”.

At the top, **Not booked yet** lists jobs at Ready to book with no install date.

![The Schedule view with installs, scaffold dates and Not booked yet at the top.](/help/projects/s04-projects-schedule.jpg "The Schedule: installs (1/2, 2/2), scaffold up and down, today marked, and Not booked yet at the top.")

To book an install or scaffold date, open the job and use [Dates and team](/admin/help/projects/dates-and-team).

## Board

On a computer, the Board has one column per delivery stage.

- Drag a card to another column to move the job, or press **Move** on the card.
- Empty stages fold to a thin rail.
- **Complete** at the end opens the completed jobs in the List.

![The Board view with a column per stage and Move on each card.](/help/projects/s05-projects-board.jpg "The Board: a column per stage and Move on each card. Empty stages and Complete fold to thin rails.")

> **Watch out:** The Board needs a screen at least 1024 pixels wide. On a phone you get the List.
