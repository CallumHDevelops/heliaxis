A **contact** is a person you deal with. They’re either **part of an organisation** (the facilities manager at a school, a housing association’s asset manager) or **on their own** (a builder who works alone, an architect, a landlord). Customers, the people who came in as enquiries, are listed here too, so every person is in one place.

## The list

- **People we deal with** are the contacts you’ve added. A grey pill says **On their own**; otherwise the pill is their organisation’s type.
- **Customers** come from Enquiries. Their page is their job’s, so tapping one opens it. With nothing typed, the 40 newest show; search to find the others.
- The **chips** (Everyone, In an organisation, On their own, Customers) filter the list and show how many are in each.
- **Search** looks at the name, role, organisation, postcode, mobile (however it was typed) and email.

## Add a contact

**Add contact** asks for a name; role, mobile, email and notes can wait. Pick an **Organisation**, or leave it as **None: on their own**. Adding someone to an organisation that has no contacts makes them its primary contact; for a later one a tick makes them primary.

Contacts aren’t customers. They don’t appear in Enquiries or the reports, don’t get a customer reference, and never receive automatic WhatsApps.

## Moving a contact

Open their page and **Edit**: change **Organisation** to put them in one, move them to another, or set it to None. Jobs dealt with through them go with them (the sheet says how many), and each job’s timeline says so. If they were the primary contact, the next one takes over.

> **Once switched on:** Contacts appear once organisations.sql has been run. See [Switch a feature on (run a database file)](/admin/help/admin/run-a-database-file).
