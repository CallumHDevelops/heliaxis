You can email a customer straight from their page, so it’s on their timeline for everyone.

## Write it

1. On the customer’s page, press **Email** under their name (or the yellow **+**, then **Email**).
2. The **Email Jane Smith** sheet opens. The line under the heading says where it goes from:
   - “sent from the shared Heliaxis mailbox; replies appear on the timeline.” once Microsoft 365 is connected.
   - “opens your email app (Microsoft 365 not connected yet) and is logged here.” before that.
3. Optionally, press **⚡ Saved replies** and pick one. It’s added to the message with their name filled in.
4. Type a **Subject**. With Microsoft 365 connected, an empty subject goes as “Your Heliaxis project”.
5. Write the message. It starts “Hi Jane,”.
6. To attach files (Microsoft 365 only), press **📎 Attach** and tick documents from this job (📄) or the company library (📚).
7. Press **Send email** (or, before Microsoft 365 is connected, **Write in email app**, then send it from your email app).

If they have no email address, the sheet says “No email address for this customer — add one under Details.” See [Change a customer’s details, postcode or site address](/admin/help/customers/edit-customer-details).

## What happens

- The email goes on the job’s **Activity** tab. Sent from the shared mailbox it shows as **Email sent**, and their reply comes back as **Email received**. Written in your email app, the app notes what you wrote (it can’t see whether you pressed send).
- Emailing counts as getting in touch: a job still at Lead moves to Contacted.

> **Watch out:** Attachments can be 3 MB in all. Anything bigger isn’t attached: from Won onwards the email gets a line with their portal link to download it instead; before that the email isn’t sent, and the app suggests sending the file on WhatsApp.

> **Good to know:** A saved reply with the customer’s link in it only works once the link exists: from Quote sent (see [Send a quote for signing](/admin/help/customers/send-quote-for-signing)). Saved replies are set up in CRM settings: see [Add a saved reply](/admin/help/admin/saved-replies). To connect the shared mailbox, see [Connect Microsoft 365 (shared mailbox and Outlook)](/admin/help/admin/microsoft-365).
