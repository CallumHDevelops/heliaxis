The search box (“Name, postcode, phone or HX-ref”) is at the top of the CRM page (on a phone, tap the magnifying glass first). On a computer, press **/** anywhere on the page to jump to it.

## What you can type

- A name: **rob evans**, **evans rob** and **rob np15** all find Robert Evans. Every word you type must match, in any order, and accents don’t matter (“sian” finds Siân).
- A postcode, with or without its space.
- Part of a phone number (at least 4 digits).
- A reference: **HX-0042**, **hx42** or **0042** find the customer and every one of their jobs. **HX-0042-2** finds just that job.

## Pick a result

As you type, a list shows up to 8 matching jobs, with their stage, reference and phone number. Names that start with what you typed come first, then open jobs.

- Click one (or use the arrow keys and Enter) to open it. A job from Won onwards opens in Projects.
- Up to 4 customers who match but have no job on the page (no job yet, or none among the 1,000 most recently changed jobs the page holds) show at the bottom. **Open their job** opens their latest open job (or their latest job, if none is open). **Create opportunity** makes a new one for them at Lead.
- **Show every match in List →**, or Enter without picking one, opens the List on **Live** with only the matches. To see a Complete or Lost match, pick that stage in the List’s stage picker. A **Search:** chip shows what you searched for; press **✕** on it to clear it.

**Esc** clears what you typed.

> **Good to know:** The search in the page header (see [Search for a customer or project](/admin/help/getting-started/search)) and Metis can find customers too. This box searches the CRM’s customers and jobs.

To add someone who isn’t there yet, see [Add a customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer).
