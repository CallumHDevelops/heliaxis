Two round buttons sit at the bottom right of every customer’s page (on a wide screen, at the bottom of the middle column): the green WhatsApp button on top and the yellow **+** below it.

## The yellow +

It opens **Add to Jane Smith** (with the customer’s full name). Pick:

| Button | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Task** | A new task: who and when |
| **Note** | **Add a note** |
| **Log a call** | **Log a call**, with a button to ring them |
| **Next step** | The follow-up reminder |
| **Photos** | Pick a category (**Survey**, **Site**, **Progress**, **Snags**, **Completion**, **Other**; it starts on **Site**), then take a photo (**📷 Take site photo**) or choose from the **🖼 Gallery** |
| **Site visit** | **Log a site visit** |
| **Email** | An email to them (the tile says “No email yet” when there isn’t one). See [Email a customer](/admin/help/customers/email-a-customer) |
| **Move stage** | The list of stages |

The last tile, marked ★, is the stage’s own next move, for example **Send booking link** at Lead and Contacted (**Add survey date** until online survey booking is switched on), **Start survey** at Survey booked (once the survey tool is switched on), **Send quote for signing** at Surveyed and **Chase quote** at Quote sent. A job with no solar, battery or heat pump gets **Add home visit date** instead of the survey tiles.

See [Add a note, log a call or a site visit](/admin/help/customers/notes-calls-visits) and [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).

## The green WhatsApp button

It shows how many unread messages they have.

- On a computer or tablet it opens the customer’s chat in the WhatsApp panel.
- On a phone it opens the chat full screen. **Back to job** brings you to the same place on their page.
- If they have no mobile yet, the **Customer** card opens for editing and the page says “Add Jane’s mobile to message them on WhatsApp.” Add it and save; any chat on that number then links to them.

Inside 24 hours of their last message you can type freely. Outside that, WhatsApp only allows an approved template. See [The WhatsApp panel](/admin/help/whatsapp/the-whatsapp-panel), [The 24-hour window and when you need a template](/admin/help/whatsapp/24-hour-window) and [WhatsApp templates](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates).

> **Good to know:** Sending a WhatsApp or an email counts as getting in touch: a customer at Lead moves to Contacted.
