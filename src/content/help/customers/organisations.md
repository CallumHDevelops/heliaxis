An **organisation** is a business or public body you deal with: a commercial customer, a landlord or housing association, a school or council, a partner who refers work to you (a builder, an architect, a letting agent), or a supplier. Its **contacts** are the people there. A job (opportunity) can be linked to an organisation and to the person you deal with.

## The list

Open **CRM › Organisations**. Each row shows the organisation, its primary contact, how many contacts and jobs it has, and its postcode.

- **Search** looks at the name, the postcode (with or without its space), the phone number however it was typed, the email, the company number, and the names, roles and emails of the people there. Every word you type must match.
- The **chips** filter by type and show how many there are of each. Customers who came in as enquiries aren’t here: they’re people, under [Contacts](/admin/help/customers/contacts).
- **Add organisation** asks for a name and a type to start with. Phone, email, website, address, company number, VAT number and notes can wait. You can add the first person there at the same time; they become the primary contact.

If the name you type is already an organisation, the sheet says so and links to it, but doesn’t stop you adding another.

## Details that are checked

- A phone number needs at least 6 digits; an email must look like one.
- A website is saved as a link (https:// is added if you leave it off). Only web addresses are accepted.
- A company number is 8 characters (01234567, or two letters and six digits for Scotland and Northern Ireland). A VAT number is GB and 9 digits.

> **Once switched on:** Organisations and Contacts appear once organisations.sql has been run. Until then the page says so and the rest of the CRM works as before. See [Switch a feature on (run a database file)](/admin/help/admin/run-a-database-file).
