Open a contact from **CRM › Contacts**, from an organisation’s page, from the search, or from a job’s **Organisation & contact** card.

## The top

Their name, role, organisation (a link, with its type; **On their own** if they have none) and, for a primary contact, **Primary contact**. Buttons: **Call**, **WhatsApp** (their chat in the CRM if their number has one, otherwise WhatsApp on your device), **Email**, and **✎ Edit**.

## Jobs

The jobs they’re dealt with through, open ones first. **Link a job** finds one by its customer’s name, postcode, phone number or HX reference. If the contact is in an organisation the job is linked to it too; if they’re on their own the job keeps whatever organisation it has. **Unlink** takes them off a job (the job and its organisation stay).

## Details, notes and moving

**Edit** changes any detail, including their **Organisation**: pick one, or **None: on their own**. Their jobs go with them. **Delete this contact** removes them altogether; their jobs stay, without a contact.

From a job’s page, the **Organisation & contact** card does the same the other way round: see [A customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/customer-page).
