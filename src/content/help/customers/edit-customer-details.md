A customer’s own details (name, mobile, email, postcode) are on the **Customer** card. Where a job’s work is (site address and site postcode) is in **Job details**. Both are in the left column of their page (the **Details** tab on a tablet or phone).

## Change their name, mobile, email or postcode

1. On the **Customer** card, press **✎ Edit**.
2. Change **Name**, **Mobile**, **Email** or **Postcode**. The name can’t be empty, and the mobile must look like a phone number.
3. Press **Save**.

- A new mobile re-links WhatsApp: any chat on that number now shows on their page. The timeline notes “Mobile changed to …”.
- With no mobile, the card shows **Add their mobile** instead.

## What happens to the site postcode

Under the **Postcode** box the card tells you what will happen to this job:

- “The site address on this job changes too.” The job only had a copy of the old postcode (or none), so it follows. This is for correcting a mistake, such as a lead form’s “88888”.
- “This job has its own site address: change it in Job details.” The job is at a different postcode, so it stays as it is.

Only sales jobs follow. A job from Won onwards keeps its site postcode, because by then a survey or booking has checked the site: a later change is a house move, not a correction. If any of their other jobs changed too, the card says so. Each job that moved gets a timeline line such as “Site postcode changed from 88888 to CF39 8YY with the customer’s”, and a job named after the old postcode takes the new one.

A postcode that doesn’t look like a UK one shows “Check postcode: it doesn’t look like a UK one” next to the site address.

## Change where the work is

For a job at a different property (a second home, a business):

1. On **Job details**, press **✎ Edit**.
2. Change **Site address** (“If different from the customer’s”) and **Site postcode**.
3. Press **Save**.

**Directions** in the header then goes to the new address. For the rest of **Job details** (owner, value, products, links), see [Change a job’s owner, value, products or links](/admin/help/customers/job-details).

## Other numbers

After a merge, the **Customer** card may list **Other numbers**: the other record’s phone or email, with where it came from. Search and incoming WhatsApps and emails still find the customer by them.

- **Use for WhatsApp** (a phone) or **Make main** (an email) makes it their main one. The current one is kept as another number.
- **Remove** forgets it: search and incoming messages won’t find them by it any more.

## WhatsApp permission

The switch next to **WhatsApp** records whether they’ve agreed to WhatsApp messages from us. It asks you to confirm. Switching it off means no automatic WhatsApps (reminders, follow-ups, smart replies), though you can still message them yourself. If they replied STOP, or someone here switched it off, it only comes back on once you confirm they’ve asked us to start again. The timeline notes each change. See [WhatsApp permission and STOP](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-permission).
