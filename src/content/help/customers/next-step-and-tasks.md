Every live customer should have a next step: the one thing that happens next, and when. Tasks are for anything else that needs doing on the job, by anyone.

## Set a next step

1. On the customer’s page, press **+ Add a next step** (or **Edit** on the next step bar, or the yellow **+**, then **Next step**).
2. Type what’s next, or tap one of **Call back**, **Chase survey photos**, **Book survey**, **Send quote**, **Chase quote** or **Book install**.
3. Under **When**, tap **Later today** (before 5pm only), **Tomorrow 9am**, **In 3 days** or **Next Monday**, or pick a date and time.
4. Press **Save**.

When it’s due, the job’s owner gets a notification, “Follow up: Jane Smith” (everyone with CRM access, if the job has no owner). It goes at the time you picked, evenings included. If you pick a time that’s already gone, the sheet warns you the reminder will go off straight away.

> **Good to know:** The sheet says “You’ll get a notification when it’s due”, but the reminder goes to the job’s owner, not to whoever set it. To remind yourself on someone else’s job, add a task for yourself instead.

The bar then shows the next step and when it’s due (“Overdue · …” when it’s late). From there:

- **✓ Done** clears it.
- **Snooze ▾** moves it to **Later today**, **Tomorrow 9am**, **In 3 days** or **Next Monday**.
- **Edit** changes it; **Clear** in the sheet removes it.

Sales jobs with no next step and no open task show under **No next step** on the List. **My work** on the CRM home also says how many sales jobs have no next step. The bar isn’t shown on Complete or Lost jobs, and their reminders don’t go out.

## Add a task

1. Press **+ Task** on the **Tasks** card (or the yellow **+**, then **Task**).
2. Type what needs doing.
3. Pick when: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Fri** (**Next Fri** on a Friday), or a date.
4. Pick who it’s for. It starts as the job’s owner (or you, if it has none); **Nobody yet** leaves it unassigned.
5. Press **Add task**.

The **+** sheet’s **New task** has a few more choices: quick titles (**Send quote**, **Send invoice**, **Send EV guide**, **Chase for photos**, **Book survey**, **Check funding**, **Order kit**), **Next week**, and **High priority**. Anyone with CRM or Tasks access can be picked. If the task is for someone else, they get a notification.

## Work through tasks

The **Tasks** card lists open tasks, overdue first, then soonest, then those with no date. It shows 5 (3 on a phone); **Show all (n)** (on a phone, **All tasks (n)**) shows the rest.

- Tick the circle to mark one done. **Show n done** lists finished ones; tick again to open one again.
- The clock opens **Snooze the task**: **Tomorrow** or **Next week** moves it straight away, or pick a day under **Pick a date** and press **Save**. You can change **Whose task** there too.

Tasks and next steps also show in [My work](/admin/help/customers/my-work) on the CRM home. For tasks the app makes by itself on projects, see [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).

> **Good to know:** While a job is on hold, its task reminders don’t go out. See [Lost, on hold and reopening](/admin/help/customers/lost-and-on-hold).
