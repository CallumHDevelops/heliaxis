Each job has one private link for the customer. There’s no login: anyone with the link can open it, so it’s only sent to the customer. The page says “This is your private link. Please don’t share it.”

![The customer’s quote page on a phone, with View quote and the signing form.](/help/customers/portal-quote.jpg "What the customer sees at Quote sent: their quote to read and sign.")

## When the link works

| Job stage | What the link opens |
| --- | --- |
| Lead to Surveyed | Nothing yet |
| Quote sent | **Your quote**: just the quote to read and sign |
| Won to Complete | **Your project**: the full portal |
| Lost | Nothing |

The link is made when you send the quote for signing, or when you first share it from the **Customer portal** card once the job is Won. If a job goes back to an earlier sales stage or is Lost, the link stops working until it reaches Quote sent or Won again.

## The quote page (Quote sent)

- “Hi Jane” and the job’s name.
- **Your quote from Heliaxis** (or **Your contract from Heliaxis**) with **View quote** and the signing form (see [Send a quote for signing](/admin/help/customers/send-quote-for-signing)). If no quote is marked to sign, it says the quote is on its way.
- **Questions?** with **WhatsApp us** and the office email.

It shows nothing else: no dates, photos or other documents. Signing moves the job to Won, so the page reloads as their full portal, with “Quote accepted by … Thank you!” at the top and **Pay your deposit** if there is one.

## The full portal (Won onwards)

- The one thing that matters most right now, such as “12 days until your installation”, “Your quote is ready to read and sign” or the survey date.
- **Sign your quote** with **View quote** and **Accept and sign**, if a quote is set to both **👁 In portal** and **✍ Needs signing** in Docs and nobody has signed yet; once signed, “Quote accepted by … Thank you!” with **View it**.
- **Pay your deposit**, if a deposit is set and not yet paid. See [Take a deposit by card](/admin/help/customers/card-deposits).
- **Progress**: **Enquiry received**, **Survey**, **Your quote**, **Order confirmed**, **Design & approvals**, **Grid connection (DNO)** (for jobs that need one), **Installation** and **Handover & certificates**, with dates and notes such as “Deposit due” or “Applied — waiting for approval”.
- The handover pack, once there is one (see [The customer’s side of the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/customers-side)).
- **Your documents**: only the documents set to **👁 In portal** on the **Docs** tab. See [Documents, photos and surveys on a job](/admin/help/projects/documents-photos-surveys).
- **Photos**: only **Progress** and **Completion** photos. Survey, site and snag photos stay private.
- **Questions?**, naming the job’s owner (“Esther and the team are here to help”).

The customer can switch the page between light, dark or matching their phone.

## On the Customer portal card

The **Customer portal** card on the customer’s page appears from Surveyed. From Quote sent it shows the link’s buttons: **WhatsApp it** or **Send on WhatsApp**, **Copy link**, **Email it** (at Quote sent) and **Open** (to see it as they do). From Won it shows a small progress bar and “Now: …”.

**Reset link (if it’s been shared by mistake)** makes a new link. The old one stops working at once, so send them the new one.
