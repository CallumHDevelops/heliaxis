Open a customer from anywhere in the CRM (a card, the search, My work, Latest notes). Each opportunity has its own page. Once it’s Won, the same page is the project’s page: see [A project’s page](/admin/help/projects/project-page) for what changes then.

## The header

- **← CRM** takes you back. Tap the customer’s name to see their details.
- Badges: **On hold** (with the reason), **Lost**, or “Looks like the same person as …” with **Merge…** (see [Merge a customer who’s in twice](/admin/help/customers/merge-duplicates)).
- The stage button, such as **Contacted ▾**, opens **Move stage**. Next to it is the stage’s main button, such as **Mark contacted** or **Send quote for signing**. See [Sales stages](/admin/help/customers/sales-stages).
- **⋯** has the rarer actions: **New project for Jane**, **Merge with another customer…**, **Copy link to this job**, and, for admins, **Delete this job**. Depending on the job it can also have **Mark Won** (at Quote sent), **Find a time** (on a survey day), **Join this job with another of Jane’s jobs…** or **Not the same person as …**. There’s no **⋯** on a phone: use the stage button to move the job, and the links at the bottom of the **Details** tab for **New project for Jane** and **Delete this job**.
- Under the name: the job’s reference (and **Customer HX-…** if it differs), products, postcode, value (or **No value yet**), the next date (“Survey Tue 3 Nov, 11:45” or **Nothing booked**) and **Other projects (n) ▾** when they have more jobs.
- **Call**, **Directions** and **Email**. After you tap **Call** and come back within 30 minutes, the page asks “Log the call with Jane?”: **Answered** opens the call log, **No answer** logs “Called, no answer.” as a call, **Skip** does nothing.
- The stage path: every sales stage, with ✓ on the ones done and how long it’s been at this one. Tap a step to move there (it asks first). **Delivery (7) ▸** shows the stages after Won.

## The middle

- The next step bar (not on Complete or Lost jobs). With none set it says **+ Add a next step** “so this doesn’t go quiet”. See [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).
- The tabs: **Overview**, **Activity** (with “2 new” when they have unread WhatsApp messages), **Photos**, **Docs**, then **Surveys** once the survey tool is switched on (more tabs appear from Won). A sales job, or a Lost one, opens on **Activity**. See [The Activity tab](/admin/help/customers/activity-tab), and for **Photos**, **Docs** and **Surveys** see [Documents, photos and surveys on a job](/admin/help/projects/documents-photos-surveys).

## The left column

- **Important**: shown if the job is on hold or a survey flagged something.
- **Customer**: **Mobile**, **WhatsApp** (permission switch and whether a chat is linked), **Email**, **Site address**, **Source** (where they came from and when), **Owner**, and **Other numbers** after a merge. Press **✎ Edit** to change them: see [Change a customer’s details](/admin/help/customers/edit-customer-details).
- **Other projects for Jane**, when they have more than one job.
- **Job details**: products, value, job name, what they put on the form, OpenSolar and SimpliHeat links, dates and notes, with **✎ Edit**. See [Change a job’s owner, value, products or links](/admin/help/customers/job-details).
- **Customer portal**, from Surveyed. Before that it says it appears at Surveyed. See [Send a quote for signing](/admin/help/customers/send-quote-for-signing).
- **Quote & deposit · DNO**, from Quote sent.
- At the bottom: when it was created and how long it’s been at this stage, **New project for Jane**, and (admins) **Delete this job**.

## Tasks and the round buttons

**Tasks** sits on the right on a computer (inside the WhatsApp panel when that’s open). The green WhatsApp button and the yellow **+** sit at the bottom right (on a wide screen, at the bottom of the middle column). See [The + and WhatsApp buttons](/admin/help/customers/plus-and-whatsapp-buttons).

## On a tablet or phone

Below 1280px wide, the left column moves to a **Details** tab. A tablet shows a short **Customer** card (mobile, site, WhatsApp) beside Tasks, with **All details →**. A phone shows the header, a one-line stage bar with the main action, the next step and up to 3 tasks, then the tabs.

The page reads itself again every 30 seconds while it’s open. Opening it never marks WhatsApp messages as read.
