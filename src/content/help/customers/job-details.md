**Job details** is the card under **Customer** in the left column of a customer’s page (on a tablet or phone, the **Details** tab). It holds what belongs to this one job; the person’s own name, mobile, email and postcode are on the **Customer** card.

## Change it

1. On **Job details**, press **✎ Edit**.
2. Change what you need:
   - **Job name**: can’t be left empty.
   - **Products**: **Solar PV**, **Battery**, **Heat pump**, **EV charger**, **Infrared heating**, **LED lighting**, **Other**.
   - **Value £ (inc. VAT)**.
   - **Owner**: anyone with CRM or Tasks access, or **Nobody**.
   - **Site address** and **Site postcode**: see [Change a customer’s details, postcode or site address](/admin/help/customers/edit-customer-details).
   - **OpenSolar project link** and **SimpliHeat project link**: each must start with https://.
   - **Why was it lost?**: only on a Lost job.
   - **On hold**, with why: see [Lost, on hold and reopening](/admin/help/customers/lost-and-on-hold).
   - **Notes**: anything about the job worth keeping in view.
3. Press **Save** (or **Cancel** to leave it as it was).

Survey and install dates aren’t here: they’re under **Overview**, in **Dates and team**.

## What each one changes

- **Owner**: who the job belongs to. The owner gets the job’s next-step reminders and the “hasn’t been contacted” alert, the job shows under **Mine** on the Board and List, and reports count it for them. Changing the owner doesn’t send them a notification, so tell them. Website, Meta and WhatsApp leads get an owner by themselves if a rota is set up: see [Choose who gets new leads](/admin/help/admin/new-leads).
- **Value**: shown on the job’s card and header, and added up in the Board’s column totals and the **Quotes out** count.
- **Products**: shown on cards and the header. A job whose products include no solar, battery or heat pump (an EV charger or LEDs only, say) needs no survey, so its survey button becomes **Add home visit date**.
- **OpenSolar** and **SimpliHeat**: the card shows **Open** to go straight to the design. The SimpliHeat line shows on heat pump jobs, or once a link is saved.

The card shows **Value inc. VAT**, the job’s own name (when it says more than the customer’s name), **From the form** (what they put on the enquiry form), the links (or “Not linked”), the dates, and the notes.
