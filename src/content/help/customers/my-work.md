**My work** is the left-hand column of the CRM home (on a phone, it comes after **Needs reply**).

## What’s in it

- **Waiting for you**: booking requests to confirm, once online survey booking is switched on. Tap it to open Surveys and diary.
- **Today**: today’s surveys, home visits and install days, with the time, the customer and their reference. A survey has **Start survey** once the survey tool is switched on; anything else has **Directions**. Up to 3 show, then “2 more today →”. **Diary →** opens the diary once survey booking is switched on.
- **My tasks** (or **Tasks** on **Everyone**): tasks on jobs that are assigned to you, and the next steps on jobs you own, grouped **Overdue**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This week**, **Later** and **No date**. If you also have Tasks access, your own tasks that aren’t on a job show here too. Overdue and Today are always open, and Tomorrow is when it has 5 or fewer; tap a folded group to open it.
- **Done today**: what’s been ticked off today.
- A nudge such as “4 leads have no next step yet”, with **Work through them →**. It counts every job at a sales stage (Lead to Quote sent) with no next step. The link opens the List on **No next step**, oldest first.

On a phone, **Today** is one line (such as “Today: 3 visits” or “Nothing booked today”) that opens the Home page, the nudge isn’t shown, and the bottom of the CRM home has **Open the pipeline list (n) →**.

Each row shows the customer and their reference (tap it to open the job), a **Next step** tag if it’s the job’s next step rather than a task, a **Deadline** tag for paperwork deadlines, and when it’s due. A small red dot means high priority.

## Mine or everyone’s

Switch between **Mine** and **Everyone**. With **Everyone**, each row shows the initials of the person it’s for. The CRM remembers your choice.

Tapping the **Follow-ups due** count shows what’s due by today for everyone. **Show everything** goes back to the full list.

## Tick, snooze or open

- Tick the circle to mark it done. It moves to **Done today**.
- Press the clock to snooze it. **Tomorrow** and **Next week** move it straight away; or pick a day under **Pick a date** and press **Save**. For a task on a job you can also change **Whose task** here.
- The arrow button opens the job. The green button opens the customer’s WhatsApp chat (when they have a mobile).

## Add a task

1. Press **+ Task**.
2. Type what needs doing.
3. Find the customer by name, postcode, phone number or HX-ref, and pick their job. You can leave it empty if you also have Tasks access; otherwise you must pick one.
4. Pick **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Fri** (**Next Fri** on a Friday) or a date, and who it’s for. It starts as you.
5. Press **Add task**.

If it’s for someone else, they get a notification. It shows in their My work.

For a job’s own Tasks card and its next step, see [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).
