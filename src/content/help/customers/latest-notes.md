**Latest notes** is the middle column of the CRM home. It shows notes, calls, site visits, surveys, emails and signed documents from every job, plus WhatsApp chats, newest first and grouped by day (**Today**, **Yesterday**, then dates).

## Filter it

- **Everyone** or **Mine**. **Mine** shows the notes you wrote and the WhatsApps you sent. The CRM remembers your choice.
- The chips: **All**, **Notes**, **Calls**, **Site visits**, **Surveys**, **Emails**, **Docs & signatures** and **WhatsApp**.
- **Stage changes**: switch it on (with **All** picked) to see every stage move and the other updates the app notes, such as “Task added”. Big moments show on **All** even with it off: a job moving to Won or Complete, a quote signed, a survey finished.

## What a row does

- A note row shows the kind, the customer, their reference, the stage, the time and who wrote it. Long notes end with **more**. Tap the customer’s name to open their job on the Activity tab, at that note. The green button opens their WhatsApp chat in the WhatsApp panel (where there’s no panel, it opens the job).
- A WhatsApp row is one chat on one day: “WhatsApp · 6 messages” (“6 messages from you” on **Mine**), the customer and the last message. Tap the name to open the chat at that message (in the WhatsApp panel on a computer or tablet, full screen on a phone). The arrow button opens their job. Chats brought in from the WhatsApp Business app are marked **Imported**.

Notes brought in by an import, and the app’s own automatic messages, don’t show here.

On a phone the first 10 show, then **More notes →**. **Load more** fetches older ones. The list refreshes every minute, and when you come back to the tab.

## Add a note from here

1. Press **+ Note**.
2. Under **Customer**, type a name, postcode or HX-ref and pick the job.
3. Under **What was it?**, pick **Note**, **Phone call** or **Site visit**.
4. Write it in **Note**.
5. Press **Save note**.

> **Good to know:** Logging a phone call or a site visit counts as getting in touch: a customer still at Lead moves to Contacted. A plain note doesn’t move anything.

To add notes from the customer’s own page, see [Add a note, log a call or a site visit](/admin/help/customers/notes-calls-visits).
