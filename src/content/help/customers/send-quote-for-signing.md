The customer reads and signs the quote on their phone. Signing moves the job to **Won** by itself.

## Before you start

Upload the quote on the **Docs** tab with the type **Quote** (or **Contract**). If you press **Send quote for signing** with no quote there, the page opens **Docs** for you to upload one first. See [Documents, photos and surveys on a job](/admin/help/projects/documents-photos-surveys).

## Send it

1. On the customer’s page, press **Send quote for signing**. It’s the main button at Surveyed (and the ★ tile under the yellow **+**), and it’s also on the **Customer portal** card.
2. The card lists the job’s quotes and contracts. Pick the one to sign.
3. Press **Send for signing**. The card says “Ready ✓ Now send them the link.”
4. Send the link: **WhatsApp it** (the message is written for you), **Email it** (sent from the shared mailbox once Microsoft 365 is connected, otherwise it opens your email app), or **Copy link** to send it another way. **Open** shows the page as they’ll see it.

What happens:

- The job moves to **Quote sent**.
- That document is marked **✍ Needs signing**; any other quote on the job loses the mark.
- The timeline notes “Quote … sent for signing”.

To send a different version, press **Send a different quote** on the card.

## What the customer does

Their link opens **Your quote from Heliaxis**. They:

1. Press **View quote** to read it.
2. Type **Your full name** and sign in the **Sign here** box.
3. Read the 14-day wording: “You can change your mind and cancel within 14 days of signing, free of charge.”
4. Can tick “Please start work within the 14 days…” if they want work to start sooner.
5. Tick “I accept this quote and Heliaxis’s terms and conditions.”
6. Press **Sign and accept**.

## When they’ve signed

- The job moves to **Won**, and everyone with CRM access gets a notification: “🎉 Quote accepted: …”.
- The timeline says who signed and whether they asked to start within the 14 days.
- **Quote & deposit** on their page shows “Accepted and signed online by … on …”, and a fingerprint of the exact file they signed.
- Their same link becomes the full portal. If there’s a deposit, they can pay it there.

Only the first signature counts. A signed quote is the contract evidence, so it can’t be deleted or changed.

## Chasing, or signed another way

- At Quote sent the main button is **Chase quote**, which opens a WhatsApp to them, already written.
- If they said yes another way, use **⋯ › Mark Won** (on a phone, pick **Won** with the stage button). If they signed on paper, record it with **Mark as signed** on the document in **Docs**.

> **Watch out:** You can only send for signing at a sales stage, and only until they’ve signed. A Lost job has to be moved back to a sales stage first. Once a job is Won, set the document to **👁 In portal** and **✍ Needs signing** in Docs instead.
