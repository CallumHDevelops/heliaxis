**Activity** is the customer’s timeline, newest first, grouped by day. A sales job opens on this tab.

## Add to it

The box at the top adds a **Note**, **Log a call** or **Site visit**. See [Add a note, log a call or a site visit](/admin/help/customers/notes-calls-visits).

## Filter it

| Chip | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everything |
| **Notes & calls** | Notes, calls and site visits |
| **WhatsApp** | Their WhatsApp messages |
| **Email** | Emails sent and received |
| **Stages & checklist** | Stage moves and other updates the app noted |
| **Docs & payments** | Documents, signatures, deposits and invoices |

## What you’ll see

- Notes, calls and visits with who added them and when.
- Emails: **Email sent** and **Email received** once Microsoft 365 email is connected (with who sent it, or “Outlook”), and **Email** for other emails noted on the job.
- Updates the app adds by itself, such as stage moves (“Lead → Contacted”), “Mobile changed to …” or “WhatsApp permission turned on by …”.
- WhatsApp messages. On a computer with the WhatsApp panel on the page, a run of messages folds into one row (“WhatsApp · 3 messages”) with **Show in chat →**, which opens the chat at that message. Without the panel, the messages show as bubbles, with photos, videos, voice notes and files.
- An entry that belongs to one of their other jobs has **Other project:** and its name.

It shows 50 at a time; **Load older** shows more. Opening a note from **Latest notes** opens this tab at that note.

> **Good to know:** Looking at this tab never marks WhatsApp messages as read. The WhatsApp panel and the chat do that.
