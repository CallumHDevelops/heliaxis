Open an organisation from the list, from the search, or from a job’s **Organisation & contact** card.

## The top

The name, its type, and buttons for **Call**, **Email**, **Website** and **✎ Edit**. The buttons only show what you’ve saved.

## Contacts

Everyone at the organisation, the **Primary** one first. Each person has **Call**, **WhatsApp** and **Email**:

- **WhatsApp** opens their chat in the CRM when their number already has one. If it hasn’t, it opens WhatsApp on your phone or computer. Nothing is sent from the CRM to a contact.
- A button is greyed out when that detail hasn’t been saved.

Under each person: **Edit**, **Make primary** (the old primary is unmarked; there is always one primary), and **Delete** (this removes them altogether; to keep them as a contact on their own, open their page and set their organisation to None). Tap their name for [their page](/admin/help/customers/contact-page).

**Add contact** adds someone here. The first contact an organisation has is its primary contact.

## Jobs

The jobs linked to this organisation, open ones first, with who they’re dealt with through. **Link a job** finds a job by its customer’s name, postcode, phone number or HX reference; pick who at the organisation it’s dealt with through. **Unlink** takes a job off (the job itself doesn’t change). You can also link from the job’s own page: see [A customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/customer-page).

## Details and notes

The company number links to Companies House, so you can check it there. **Delete this organisation** keeps its contacts (they become contacts on their own) and its jobs (they’re unlinked; each job’s timeline says so). It asks first.

> **Watch out:** An organisation isn’t a customer. A person who WhatsApps the business number from an organisation still arrives as a new chat, as any new number does, so you can open their chat from here once it exists.
