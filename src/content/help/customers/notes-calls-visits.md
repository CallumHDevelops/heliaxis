Everything you add shows on the customer’s **Activity** tab and in **Latest notes** on the CRM home.

## On the customer’s page

1. Open the **Activity** tab.
2. At the top, pick **Note**, **Log a call** or **Site visit**.
3. Type in the box. It grows as you type, and **Save** appears.
4. Press **Save** (or Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter on a Mac).

Or press the yellow **+** and pick **Note**, **Log a call** or **Site visit**. The sheets are **Add a note**, **Log a call** and **Log a site visit**; press **Save**. **Log a call** also has a button to ring their number.

## Straight after a call

Tap **Call** in the header to ring them. When you come back to the page within 30 minutes it asks “Log the call with Jane?”:

- **Answered** opens **Log a call** so you can write what was said.
- **No answer** saves “Called, no answer.” on their timeline as a call, so it counts as getting in touch too.
- **Skip** saves nothing.

## From the CRM home

Press **+ Note** on **Latest notes** (or, on a phone, the yellow **+**, then **Note** or **Log a call**). Pick the customer’s job, choose **Note**, **Phone call** or **Site visit**, write it and press **Save note**.

## What it changes

- A logged call or site visit counts as getting in touch. If the customer is still at **Lead**, they move to **Contacted**, and the “new lead” and “hasn’t been contacted” notifications clear for everyone.
- A note doesn’t move the stage.
- Nothing is sent to the customer.

> **Good to know:** To chase or remind yourself later, set a next step or add a task instead. See [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).
