Open the order from the job’s **Orders** tab or the Purchase orders page.

## Confirmed and delivered

- Press **Mark confirmed** when the supplier confirms.
- Press **Mark delivered** with what arrived (“Change a number if something was short”). **Not delivered after all** undoes it.

## Their invoice

1. Under **Their invoice**, fill in **Invoice number**, **Total inc VAT £** and **Paid on**.
2. Press **Save invoice**.

It tells you if the invoice is more or less than the order.

## Changed an order that’s gone?

It says “You’ve changed this order since it went to …”. Press **Email it again** (without Microsoft 365, **Send it again**).

## Cancel or delete

- **Cancel order** (with a reason) keeps it on the job as Cancelled. It doesn’t tell the supplier, so call them.
- **Delete draft** is for one that never went.

> **Watch out:** The supplier can’t be changed once an order has gone. Cancel it and make a new one ([Order kit for a job](/admin/help/purchase-orders/order-kit)).

A supplier PO’s statuses are Draft, Sent, Confirmed, Delivered and Cancelled (plus Late). See [Statuses at a glance](/admin/help/projects/statuses).
