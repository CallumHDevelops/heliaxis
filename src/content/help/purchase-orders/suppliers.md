Suppliers live on the Purchase orders page. Press **Suppliers** at the top (beside **Orders**).

## Add a supplier

1. Press **+ Add a supplier**.
2. Fill in **Supplier name**, **Email for orders**, **Contact name**, **Phone**, **Our account number with them**, **Address** and **Notes**.
3. Press **Add supplier**.

You can also press **+ Add a supplier** while making an order ([Order kit for a job](/admin/help/purchase-orders/order-kit)).

## Change or remove one

- Each supplier has **Edit**.
- A supplier you’ve never ordered from has **Delete**.
- One you’ve used has **Archive** instead, so its orders keep their supplier.
- **Show archived** and **Bring back** undo an archive.

> **Watch out:** The supplier can’t be changed once an order has gone. Cancel the order and make a new one.
