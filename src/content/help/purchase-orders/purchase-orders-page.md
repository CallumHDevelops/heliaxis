## Open it

- On a computer: **Finance › Purchase orders**.
- On a phone: **More › Orders**.
- The **Purchase orders** button on the Projects page.
- The link at the foot of each job’s Orders tab.

![The Purchase orders page with Finance highlighted, grouped Late, Drafts, Later and No date.](/help/purchase-orders/s22-purchase-orders-page.jpg "Finance › Purchase orders: every job’s orders, grouped Late, Drafts, Later and No date, with filters and Orders | Suppliers.")

## At the top

- A summary, such as “3 open · £2,425.00 on order ex VAT · 1 due in the next 7 days”.
- The search box (**PO number, HX-ref, customer or supplier**).
- The chips **Live**, **Draft**, **Open**, **Awaiting delivery**, **Delivered** and **Cancelled**.
- An **Every supplier** filter.
- **Orders** and **Suppliers** ([Add and manage suppliers](/admin/help/purchase-orders/suppliers)).

## The groups

Live orders are grouped: **Late**, **Due in the next 7 days**, **Drafts: not sent yet**, **Later** and **No date**.

Tap a row to open it. The job link at the top of the sheet takes you to that job’s Orders tab.

To work with an order once it’s open, see [Confirm, receive and pay a supplier order](/admin/help/purchase-orders/move-an-order-on) or [Labour POs](/admin/help/purchase-orders/labour-pos).

> **Once switched on:** The Purchase orders page, its menu entry and the button on Projects appear once purchase-orders.sql has been run. Purchase orders need **CRM** access.
