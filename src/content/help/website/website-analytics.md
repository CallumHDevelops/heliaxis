**CMS › Website analytics** shows the visitor figures for heliaxis.co.uk. They come from Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, and the page shows Umami’s own dashboard full screen. **Open in Umami ↗** opens the same dashboard in a new tab.

## What’s counted

- Visits to the public website: the pages, the blog and the tools (such as the solar estimator).
- Not the staff app, sign-in pages, or customers’ personal links (their project page and survey booking link).
- Umami doesn’t use cookies, so visitors are counted whether or not they accept the cookie banner.

## What the main figures mean

Umami names and lays out its own dashboard, so the wording can change when Umami updates it. The main figures are:

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors | Different people (strictly, different devices) who came to the site in the period |
| Visits | Separate trips to the site. One visitor who comes back on Tuesday and Friday is two visits |
| Views | Pages opened. A visit that reads three pages is three views |
| Bounce rate | The share of visits that saw only one page and left |
| Visit duration | The average time a visit lasted |

Below those, Umami lists the top pages, where visitors came from (Google, Facebook and so on), and their browsers, devices and countries. You can change the date range on Umami’s dashboard.

## Reading it

- A page with lots of views but a high bounce rate is being found but not leading anywhere: check its call to action.
- A jump in visitors from one source usually means an advert or post is working.
- Visitor figures don’t show who got in touch. For enquiries and where they came from, use [CRM reports](/admin/help/reports/crm-reports-page).

> **Good to know:** If you see a card headed **Connect free Umami share link** instead of figures, analytics hasn’t been connected yet. Ask an admin.

To see where people click on a page, use the [click heatmap](/admin/help/website/heatmap). For how these figures sit beside CRM reports, see [Website visitors and heatmaps](/admin/help/reports/website-visitors).
