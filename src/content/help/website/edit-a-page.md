Every page is a stack of sections (a hero, a grid of features, an FAQ, a quote form and so on). You edit a page by changing what’s in each section.

## Open the page

1. Go to **CMS › Manage CMS**.
2. Find the page under **Your pages** and click **Edit page**.

To jump to another page while you’re in the editor, click the page name in the middle of the toolbar.

## Change the words

- Click any heading, paragraph or button text in the preview and type. Press **Enter** to finish a one-line piece of text.
- Or pick the section in the **Sections** tab: its settings appear underneath, headed **Edit:** and the section’s name, with a box for each piece of text.
- In headings, wrap words in `**double asterisks**` to show them in solar gold, for example `Cut your bills with **Welsh sunshine.**`

## Change a button’s link

- Right-click the button in the preview. A **Button link** box opens: type a link and click **Save link** (or press **Enter**), or click **Get a quote**, **Funding**, **Contact** or **Blog** to use that link straight away.
- Or use the section’s link field in the **Sections** tab, which has a list of pages and page sections to pick from.

## Change a picture

Pick the section, then use **Choose** (from the image library), **Upload** or **Clear** next to the picture. In **Image + text** and **Install gallery** sections you can also drag the photo in the preview to move it in its frame, scroll to zoom, and click it for its **Image SEO & metadata** (alt text and so on). See [Images on the website](/admin/help/website/images).

## Add, move and remove sections

- Add: click **+ Add section** at the bottom of the layout list and pick one, or drag one from that list onto the preview where you want it. A section you click is added at the very bottom of the page, below the footer, so drag it up into place.
- Move: drag a section by its **⋮⋮** handle in the layout list and drop it higher or lower in the list, or at the right spot on the preview.
- Remove: click the **×** on the section in the list, then **Delete section**. This can’t be undone.
- Space around a section: each section’s settings end with **Spacing**: **Pad top (px)**, **Pad bottom (px)**, **Margin top (px)** and **Margin bottom (px)**.

Hover over a section in the layout list, or in the **+ Add section** list, to see a small preview of it.

> **Watch out:** Some new sections arrive with example wording, such as sample testimonials, figures and prices. Replace it with real details before you publish.

> **Good to know:** The footer is a **Site footer** section on each page, not one shared footer. Changing it on one page doesn’t change it on the others.

## Saving

There’s no need to press anything: the editor saves as you go. The status at the top right shows **Saving to Supabase…** and then **Saved to Supabase**. **Save** saves straight away. If you try to leave before a save has finished, your browser warns you.

> **Watch out:** The whole website is saved as one document. If two people edit at the same time, the last one to save can wipe out the other’s changes. Agree who’s editing, and keep the CMS open in one tab only.

Your changes are saved, but they aren’t on the public website yet. To put them live, see [Preview and publish the website](/admin/help/website/preview-and-publish).

## Section types

| Section | What it’s for |
| --- | --- |
| **Hero** | The big banner at the top of a page |
| **Trust bar**, **Accreditation banner**, **Client banner**, **Brand banner** | Logos and accreditations |
| **Stat bar**, **Grid section**, **Process steps**, **Feature explorer** | Figures, features and how-it-works |
| **Image + text**, **Rich text** | A photo with words, or free text |
| **Home / Business**, **Pricing plans**, **Funding & finance** | Options and ways to pay |
| **Case-study cards**, **Install gallery**, **Testimonials** | Your work and reviews |
| **Estimator band**, **CTA band**, **Quote form**, **Contact form** | Getting people to get in touch |
| **FAQ**, **Site footer** | Questions and the bottom of the page |
