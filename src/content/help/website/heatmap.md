**CMS › Heatmap** shows a page of the public website with its visitors’ clicks painted over it. The more clicks in a spot, the hotter the colour: blue for a few, then green, yellow and orange, to red for the most.

> **Admins only:** The menu item shows for anyone with Analytics access, but the click figures only load for admins. Anyone else sees an **unauthorized** message.

![The click heatmap](/help/website/heatmap.jpg "Clicks over the homepage, with the controls on the right")

## The controls

A small set of controls floats over the page. If it’s in the way, drag it by its handle (it says **Drag to move** when you point at it).

- **Clicks**: the period (**24 hours**, **7 days** or **30 days**) and how many clicks the page had in it.
- **↻** reloads the clicks. **↗** opens the live page in a new tab.
- **Search**: the page you’re looking at, by name and address. Type in **Find a page…** to pick another: the list shows up to 8 matching pages, the ones with the most clicks first, with each page’s count. You can also type an address starting with `/` and press **Enter**.

The page list covers the fixed pages (such as **Home**, **Blog**, **Solar estimator**), and every published CMS page, including blog articles.

For the visitor figures, see [Website analytics](/admin/help/website/website-analytics) and [Website visitors and heatmaps](/admin/help/reports/website-visitors).

## Reading it

- Hot spots on buttons and links mean they’re working: people see them and click.
- Hot spots on things that aren’t links (a photo, a heading, an icon) mean people expect them to do something. Think about making them a link.
- Little heat on your main button means people aren’t finding it, or aren’t convinced yet. Try moving it higher or changing its words.
- Heat fading lower down usually means fewer people scroll that far.

## Things to know

- Clicks are recorded on the public website only, never in the staff app. They’re anonymous: just the spot on the page, not who clicked.
- Clicks from phones and computers are shown together. Each click is stored as a position across and down the page, but a page is laid out differently on a phone, so phone clicks won’t sit exactly over the same button on the computer layout.
- Nearby clicks are grouped into small squares (about a twentieth of the page each way), so a hot spot shows an area rather than a precise point.
- If the page says **No clicks on / yet** (with that page’s address), nobody has clicked on it in that period. Try a longer period.
- If a page has changed a lot recently, older clicks may line up with things that have moved. Use **24 hours** or **7 days** to see the new layout’s clicks.
