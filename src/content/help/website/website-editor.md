**Manage CMS** opens the editor for heliaxis.co.uk. It has two screens: a dashboard listing every page, and the page editor itself.

> **Good to know:** The editor needs room. On a phone, its card on the main dashboard says **Best on a tablet or computer**.

## The dashboard

The dashboard is headed **Website**. From top to bottom:

- **← Main dashboard** takes you back to the staff app.
- A row of counts and shortcuts: **Website pages**, **AI blogs** (opens the Blog), **Enquiries**, **Analytics** and **View live site**.
- **Create**: **+ New page**, **+ Landing page**, **+ Case study**, **+ From template** and **+ Bulk pages**. See [Add a new website page](/admin/help/website/add-a-page).
- **Your pages**: a search box (**Search by title or path…**, or press **/** to jump to it) and a filter for **All pages**, **Home**, **Landing**, **Case study** and **Page**. Pages are split into **Live pages** and **Draft pages**. Each card shows how many sections the page has and has **Edit page**, **Preview** and **Delete** buttons. Blog articles aren’t listed here; they’re on the [Blog page](/admin/help/website/blog).
- **Leads & form submissions**: the three newest enquiries. Click one for its details, or **Manage all →** to open Enquiries. They only load if you also have Enquiries access; without it the card says **Sign in required**.
- **Site & admin**: **Image library**, **Brand logos**, **Mega menu**, **Blog**, **Analytics** and **Click heatmap**.

## The page editor

Click a page card (or **Edit page**) to open it. The editor has:

- **A toolbar** along the top. Click **Heliaxis** at the left to go back to the dashboard. The page name in the middle is a menu for jumping to another page (**Search by title or path…**, or **All pages**). On the right: the save status, **Save**, the **⋯** menu (**New page**, **Save as template**, **AI page builder**, **Export data**, **Export HTML**), **Preview** and **Publish**.
- **Three tabs on the left**: **Sections** (the page’s layout and the settings for the section you’ve picked), **Images** and **SEO**.
- **The preview on the right**, with **Desktop** and **Mobile** buttons to see the page at each size. You can type straight into the preview.

<!-- shot: website/cms-editor.jpg | Sections on the left, the live preview on the right | The page editor -->

The button at the top right shows your initials and email. Its menu has **Main dashboard**, **View site**, **Blog**, **Enquiries**, **Subcontractors** and **Analytics** (each only if you have that access), **Users** (admins) and **Sign out**. The **🐛 Report** button at the bottom right sends a bug report or feature request.
