**CMS › CMS guide** teaches someone new to build, check and publish pages on heliaxis.co.uk. It takes about two hours in all, and they can stop and come back: progress is saved in their browser.

## How it works

- **Ten lessons** in the list on the left, from the four golden rules to a weekly routine. The percentage at the top shows how far through you are.
- Each lesson has **steps to tick off** as you do them on the real screens. A button next to a step, such as **Open Manage CMS**, opens the right screen in a new tab.
- **Watch out** and **Good to know** boxes cover the mistakes that matter most.
- **Check yourself**: a short quiz. Pick an answer and you’re told straight away whether it’s right and why. A lesson is complete when every step is ticked and every question is right.
- **Want the detail?** links go to the matching Help pages.

## For whoever is training someone

1. Give them access: an admin ticks **Website CMS** for them on the **Users** page (see [Who can use the website tools](/admin/help/website/who-can-use-website-tools)).
2. Ask them to type their name in the box on the left, and work through the lessons in order.
3. Press **Print the handout** for a paper copy: every lesson with empty tick boxes, and the quiz answers on the last page for you.
4. When they finish, **Print the completion sheet** shows each lesson’s result, with a line to sign.

> **Good to know:** The guide teaches the **practice page** method: they make a page of their own to try things on, tick **Hide from search engines**, and delete it at the end of lesson 7. Nothing in the guide asks them to press **Publish now**.

> **Watch out:** Progress is saved on the computer and browser being used, not on the person’s account. A different computer, or clearing the browser’s data, starts it again. **Start again** at the bottom of the lesson list clears it on purpose.

## If the guide is out of date

The lessons name real buttons and screens. If the CMS changes, the lesson that mentions the change needs updating too: it’s kept in `src/lib/cms-guide.ts`, with the date it was last checked at the top of the page.
