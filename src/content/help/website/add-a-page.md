New pages start as drafts. Nobody outside the office sees them until you publish them from the editor: open the page and click **Publish**. Publishing another page doesn’t put them live (see [Preview and publish the website](/admin/help/website/preview-and-publish)).

## From the dashboard

Go to **CMS › Manage CMS** and use the buttons under **Create**:

| Button | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **+ New page** | A choice of **Blank page** (a page called "Untitled page" with a hero and footer to build on), **AI page builder** or **Bulk create** |
| **+ Landing page** | A gallery of 15 campaign landing pages (residential solar, battery storage, grants, EV charging, care homes and more). Scroll each preview, then **Use this →** |
| **+ Case study** | A gallery of 3 case-study layouts. **Use this →** on the one you want |
| **+ From template** | Layouts you or a colleague saved with **Save as template** |
| **+ Bulk pages** | Several AI-written pages at once |

The new page opens in the editor straight away, ready to change. **AI page builder** and **Bulk create** are for admins: see [Write a page with the AI page builder](/admin/help/website/ai-page-builder).

> **Watch out:** The landing-page and case-study layouts are filled with example content: figures such as **1,200+** installs and a **4.9★** rating, made-up customers and savings, and testimonials marked as illustrative. Replace every one with real details, or delete it, before you publish.

## From inside the editor

1. Click **⋯** in the toolbar, then **New page**.
2. Type a **Page title**. The **URL slug** (the web address, such as `/solar-panels-cardiff`) fills itself in to match; you can change it.
3. Click **Create page**.

This page starts empty. Add sections with **+ Add section** (see [Edit a website page](/admin/help/website/edit-a-page)).

## Names and addresses must be unique

No two pages can share a title or a URL slug. The editor tells you if one is already taken. Only one page can be the homepage (slug `/`), and addresses under `/blog/` are kept for blog articles, which you create from the [Blog page](/admin/help/website/write-a-blog-article).

## Next steps

- Set the page’s search-engine title and description on the **SEO** tab: [Page settings and SEO](/admin/help/website/page-settings-and-seo).
- To link to the page from the website’s top menu, see [Edit the website menu and top bar](/admin/help/website/menu-and-top-bar).
- To reuse this layout later, see [Save a page as a template](/admin/help/website/save-as-template).

## Delete a page

On the dashboard, click **Delete** on the page’s card, then **Delete page**. This can’t be undone. The site must keep at least one page.

> **Good to know:** A deleted page stays on the public website until the next time someone presses **Publish**.
