> **Admins only:** Only admins can generate pages. Anyone else who tries sees an error.

## One page

1. Open the AI page builder:
   - on the dashboard, click **+ New page**, then **AI page builder**, or
   - in the editor, click **⋯**, then **AI page builder**.
2. In **What should this page cover?**, describe the page in a sentence or two: the topic, the points to cover and the call to action.
3. Under **Where should it go?**, choose **Add to the current page** or **Create a new page** (and give the **New page title**).
4. Click **Generate page**. It reads the top-ranking pages on the topic, then writes the sections and a search-engine title and description. This can take up to about two minutes: keep the window open.
5. Look through the proposed sections. Click **Regenerate** for another go, or **Insert** (it says how many sections, for example **Insert 6 sections**) to add them.

The sections land in the editor like any others, so you can change every word. A new page starts as a draft, with a footer added at the end. With **Add to the current page**, the new sections go at the very bottom of the page (below its footer, if it has one), so drag them up into place.

## Many pages at once (Bulk create)

The CMS holds a set of ready-written content briefs for pages the website should have.

1. On the dashboard, click **+ Bulk pages** (or **+ New page**, then **Bulk create**).
2. Tick the briefs you want, or **Select all new**. Briefs whose page already exists are marked **already exists** and can’t be ticked.
3. Click the **Generate** button (it says how many, for example **Generate 3 pages**). Each page takes a minute or two; its line shows **Queued**, **Writing…**, then **✓** and the number of sections, or **✗** with the reason.
4. **Stop after current** stops the run once the page being written is done.

Each page lands under **Draft pages** for you to review.

> **Watch out:** Keep this the only CMS tab open while bulk create runs, and don’t edit pages in another tab or computer at the same time. The CMS saves the whole site in one go, so a save elsewhere could undo the new pages.

> **Watch out:** Always read AI pages before publishing. Check every claim, figure and price is true for Heliaxis.
