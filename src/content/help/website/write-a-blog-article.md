> **Admins only:** Only admins see the **New article** box and can generate articles.

## Write it

1. Go to **CMS › Blog**.
2. In **New article**, describe the topic, for example `Solar PV for Cardiff homes — practical tone, ~800 words`.
3. Click **Generate**. When it says **Draft ready**, check the **Page title** and the **Slug (URL)** (the end of the web address).
4. If you want to change more before it goes into the CMS, click **Edit full draft**: you can change the **SEO title**, **SEO description**, **Hero tags**, **Hero headline**, the introduction, both blocks of body text (**Rich text 1** and **Rich text 2**), the call to action and the **Home / business cards**. **Hide details** folds it away again.
5. Click **Create CMS page**. The article opens in the CMS editor.

> **Watch out:** Close any other tab where the CMS editor is open (yours or a colleague’s) before you click **Create CMS page**. That editor doesn’t know about the new article, and because the CMS saves the whole website in one go, its next save would remove it.

The app fills the website’s standard article layout: a hero with tags, an introduction with a picture, body text, a call to action, more body text, a form and home and business cards. It may pick a matching stock photo for the introduction.

## Check and edit it

The article is a draft. In the editor, read it through and change anything that’s wrong, the same way as any page (see [Edit a website page](/admin/help/website/edit-a-page)). Its address always starts `/blog/`.

> **Watch out:** AI can get facts wrong. Check every figure, price, grant and claim is true before it goes live.

## Put it live

Go back to **CMS › Blog** and use **Schedule** or **⋯ › Publish now**. See [Publish, schedule or take down a blog article](/admin/help/website/publish-a-blog-article).

> **Good to know:** If the **New article** box shows a red note about an API key and **Generate** is greyed out, AI writing isn’t set up yet. Ask an admin.
