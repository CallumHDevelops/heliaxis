Every picture on the website comes from one shared image library. Uploading resizes and compresses the picture for you.

## Upload pictures

Any of these work:

- On the CMS dashboard, under **Site & admin**, click **Image library** (the page is headed **Media library**), then **+ Upload**.
- In the editor, open the **Images** tab and use **Upload image(s)**.
- On a section’s settings in the **Sections** tab, click **Upload** next to its picture. If you upload a single picture here, it goes straight into that section.

You can pick several files at once.

## Put a picture in a section

1. Open the page in the editor and click the section.
2. Next to the picture in its settings, click **Choose**, then pick a picture from the library. (Or **Upload** a new one, or **Clear** to remove it.)
3. In **Image + text** and **Install gallery** sections, you can then drag the photo in the preview to move it inside its frame, and scroll or use **+** and **−** to zoom.

## Alt text and other details

In the section’s settings, click the **Section SEO** card under the picture to open **Image SEO & metadata**. (In **Image + text** and **Install gallery** sections you can also click the photo in the preview.)

- **Alt text** (marked **Required for SEO**): describe what’s in the picture. Screen readers read this out and Google uses it. Tick **Decorative image** instead if it’s only decoration.
- **Title attribute**, **Caption**, **Meta description** and **Keywords** are optional.
- **Loading**: **Lazy** (the usual choice) loads the picture as the visitor scrolls to it; **Eager** loads it straight away, which suits a picture at the very top of the page.
- **Replace image…** swaps in a different picture.

Click **Save & use in section** to keep your changes.

## Tidy the library

In **Image library** (or the **Images** tab), click pictures to select them, then:

- type a **Category label** and click **Set category** to group them (filter by category with the drop-down), or
- click **Delete selected**.

The **×** on a single picture removes just that one.

> **Watch out:** Removing a picture from the library also removes it from every section that uses it. This can’t be undone.

## Brand logos

**Brand logos** on the dashboard is the library of manufacturer logos (GivEnergy, SolarEdge and so on). Click **+ Add logo** to upload one: it goes into the banner straight away. Type in the box under a logo to rename it. Tick **banner** on a logo to show it in the manufacturer banner (the **Brand banner** section) on the website; untick to hide it. **×** deletes the logo at once, with no question asked.

Like everything else in the CMS, new pictures and logo changes reach the public website when it’s next published (see [Preview and publish the website](/admin/help/website/preview-and-publish)).
