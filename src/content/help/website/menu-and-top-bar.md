On the CMS dashboard, under **Site & admin**, click **Mega menu**. The page shows the top bar settings, a live preview of the menu, and the settings for the menu item you’ve picked.

## The top bar

**Top bar (thin strip above the logo)** is the dark strip at the very top of every page. Untick **Show** to hide it. You can change:

- **Accreditations (left text)**
- **Rating value** and **Opening hours**
- **Phone (shown)** and **Phone link (tel:)**. Typing the phone number fills in the link for you.

## Menu items

Each item along the menu bar is either a plain link or opens a drop-down ("mega") panel.

1. Click an item in the preview bar to pick it. Drag items along the bar to reorder them.
2. Change its **Label**.
3. For a plain link, untick **Enable mega menu (columns, links & featured panel)** and set **This item links directly to**: pick a page from the list or type a path such as `/solar-estimator`.
4. For a drop-down panel, tick **Enable mega menu (columns, links & featured panel)**. Then:
   - each **Column** has a **Column heading** and its links. Click a link’s icon to change it, type its label, pick where it goes, and add an optional excerpt under it. Drag the **⋮⋮** handle to reorder links. **+ Add link** adds one; **×** removes a link and **remove column** removes the whole column, both straight away.
   - the featured panel on the right has a **Featured title**, **Featured text**, **CTA label** and **CTA links to**, with a **Dark texture** or **Light texture** background, or your own photo.

**Remove top-level item**, then **Remove item**, deletes the item you’ve picked and its columns.

## Add items quickly

The buttons at the bottom add common links in one click: **+ Estimator**, **+ Blog**, **+ Funding**, **+ FAQs** and **+ Contact**. If the menu already has an item with that name, the button turns that item into a plain link instead of adding a second one. **+ Add top-level item** adds a new one, and **+ Add column** adds a column to the current drop-down.

## Put it live

Menu and top bar changes save as you go but only show on the website after you click **Publish** in the toolbar, then **Publish now**. The box says **Header menu published with** the number of items. If you still see an old link, hard-refresh the homepage.

> **Watch out:** As with any publish, this puts every page and draft in the CMS live too. See [Preview and publish the website](/admin/help/website/preview-and-publish).
