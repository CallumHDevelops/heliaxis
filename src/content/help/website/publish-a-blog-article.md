All of these are on **CMS › Blog**, on the article’s line.

> **Watch out:** **Delete** changes the saved website straight away. If the CMS editor is open in another tab (yours or a colleague’s), its next save puts the article back, as a draft. Close or reload any open editor before you delete. **Schedule**, **Unschedule** and **Unpublish** are safe: an editor that was already open can’t undo them.

## Preview

Click **⋯**, then **Preview**. The article opens in a new tab with a bar saying **Preview — not published**. **← Back to articles** takes you back.

## Publish now

1. Click **⋯**, then **Publish now**, then **Publish**.
2. The CMS opens and publishes. Wait for **Published — live on your site**.

Only this article goes live, along with any saved changes to pages and articles that are already live. Other draft and scheduled articles, and draft pages, stay as they are. See [Preview and publish the website](/admin/help/website/preview-and-publish).

## Change an article that’s already live

1. Click **Edit** on the article and make your changes in the editor. They save as you go but aren’t on the website yet.
2. Click **Publish** in the editor’s toolbar, then **Publish now**.

As with **Publish now**, saved changes to other live pages and articles go live with it, but no draft page and no article that isn’t live.

## Schedule it for later

> **Admins only:** Scheduling, unscheduling, unpublishing and deleting need an admin.

1. Click **Schedule** on a draft.
2. Pick the **Date** on the calendar (or **Today** or **Tomorrow**) and the **Time** (**Hour**, **Minute**, **AM** or **PM**). Above the calendar, the box spells out the full date and time you’ve chosen. Past days can’t be picked, and minutes go in steps of 5.
3. Click **Confirm schedule**. The badge changes to **Scheduled** and the date.

The time must be at least 30 seconds away.

> **Watch out:** The article goes live exactly as it was when you scheduled it. If you edit it afterwards, click **Unschedule** and schedule it again, or your changes won’t be in the live version.

> **Good to know:** The app puts it live by itself within a minute or so of the time, from its every-minute job. Publishing the website before then doesn’t put it live early. If the app can’t put it on the website, it stays **Scheduled** and the app tries again the next minute. If an article is still **Scheduled** well after its time, ask an admin to [check the every-minute job is running](/admin/help/admin/every-minute-job).

## Cancel a schedule

Click **Unschedule**, then **Unschedule** again. The article goes back to being a draft.

If its time has come and it’s already gone live, there’s no schedule to cancel: you’ll see a message saying so, and its badge changes to **Live**. To take it off the website, use **Unpublish** (below).

## Take an article off the website

Click **⋯**, then **Unpublish**, then **Unpublish**. It leaves the website and becomes a draft you can edit and publish again later. If the app can’t take it off, you’ll see a message and it stays on the website, still **Live**: click **Unpublish** again.

It stays off the website until it’s published again, with **Publish now** or from its editor. Publishing the website for anything else doesn’t bring it back.

## Delete an article

Click **⋯**, then **Delete**, then **Delete**. It’s removed from the CMS, and from the website if it was live. This can’t be undone.
