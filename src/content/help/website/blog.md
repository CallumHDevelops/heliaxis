**CMS › Blog** (the page is headed **AI Blog**) lists the articles on heliaxis.co.uk/blog. Articles are written into a fixed article layout by AI, then edited and published in the CMS like any page. They’re kept off the CMS dashboard’s page list so they don’t get mixed up with website pages.

## At the top

- **Drafts**, **Scheduled** and **Live**: how many articles are in each state.
- **New article**: the box for writing a new one with AI. Only admins see it; everyone else sees **Only admins can create posts.** See [Write a blog article with AI](/admin/help/website/write-a-blog-article).

## Articles

Search by title or address with **Search title or slug…**, or filter with **All**, **Drafts**, **Scheduled** and **Live**. The list shows 10 at a time.

Each article shows its title, its address (such as `/blog/solar-for-cardiff-homes`) and a badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Not on the website yet |
| **Scheduled** and a date | Set to go live by itself at that time |
| **Live** | On the website now |

And these buttons:

- **Edit** opens the article in the CMS editor (see [Edit a website page](/admin/help/website/edit-a-page)).
- **Schedule** (drafts), **Unschedule** (scheduled articles) or **View** (live articles: opens it on the website).
- **⋯** for **Preview**, **Publish now** or **Unpublish**, and **Delete**.

See [Publish, schedule or take down a blog article](/admin/help/website/publish-a-blog-article).
