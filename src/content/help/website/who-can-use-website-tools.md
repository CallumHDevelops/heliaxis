The **CMS** menu only shows the pages you can open. An admin gives access on the **Users** page (the **Users & access** card on the dashboard), by ticking boxes under **Portal access**. Admins can open everything.

## What each tick gives you

| Tick in Portal access | Menu items | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Website CMS** | **Manage CMS**, **Blog** | The page editor, image library, brand logos, mega menu and the Blog page |
| **Analytics** | **Website analytics**, **Heatmap** | Visitor figures, and the heatmap page (but see below) |

## Jobs only admins can do

| Job | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Write a page with the **AI page builder** or **Bulk create** | CMS editor |
| Write a new article in **New article** | Blog |
| **Schedule**, **Unschedule**, **Unpublish** or **Delete** an article | Blog |
| See the click figures on the heatmap | Heatmap |

Everyone with **Website CMS** can edit pages, add and delete pages, change the menu and pictures, and publish the website, including with **Publish now** on the Blog page.

> **Watch out:** Because anyone with **Website CMS** can publish, and any publish puts everyone’s saved changes to live pages live, agree in the office who publishes and when.

## If something’s missing

- No **CMS** menu at all: you don’t have either tick. Ask an admin.
- **Manage CMS** is there but **Website analytics** isn’t (or the other way round): you have one tick and not the other.
- The **Leads & form submissions** card on the CMS dashboard says **Sign in required**: that card also needs the **Enquiries** tick.
- On a phone, these are under **More**.

> **Admins only:** Change someone’s access on the [Users](/admin/users) page. Every tick is explained in [What each access tick allows](/admin/help/admin/access-ticks).
