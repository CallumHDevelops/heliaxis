## Upload a document

1. Press **+ Add a document**, or **Upload** on an item.
2. Choose **Which is it?**
3. Choose **Show it in their portal now** (with **Tell Jane it’s there** by WhatsApp or email) or **Hold it until the pack is sent**.

Uploading an MCS certificate offers to read the number: “Read from the certificate: … Save it as the MCS certificate number?” Press **Save it**.

You can also add a document from the Docs tab: an MCS certificate, warranty, invoice or receipt asks **Add it to the handover pack as** ([Documents, photos and surveys on a job](/admin/help/projects/documents-photos-surveys)).

## Helpers on particular items

- **Copy for MID**: everything the MCS Installation Database asks for, in its order.
- **Notify building control** (see [The electrician’s certificate](/admin/help/site/electricians-certificate)).
- **G98 data sheet** and **G99 data sheet**: for filling in the DNO’s forms; never in the pack.
- **Mark it sent to the DNO**.
- **Still to put right**: printed on the completion sign-off with a done-by date.
- **Use “…”** with a design file’s name, for the wiring diagram.
- **Final invoice** (see [The final invoice and payments](/admin/help/handover/final-invoice)).

## Not needed

**Not needed** asks why, and records it with your name. **Needed after all** puts it back.

> **Admins only:** Items from site (test records, certificate, system summary) can only be marked Not needed by an admin.

## Each document

Each document has **Download**, **Print version**, **Show in portal** or **Hide from portal**, **Replace**, and **Archive** (kept, but the customer won’t see it).

For which items you upload and which the app makes, see [Who makes each handover document](/admin/help/handover/who-makes-each-document).
