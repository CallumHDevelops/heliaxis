1. On the **Handover** tab, press **Send to customer**.
2. Check the sheet. It lists **Still to come from site:**, and **Still needed from the office:** with a button for each office item that has to be in first. The pack can go once everything from site is in. Office items that can follow are listed under “On its way after”, and show as “On its way” in their portal.
3. Tick **WhatsApp**, **Email** or both, and check the message (“Hi Jane, your Heliaxis handover pack is ready…”).
4. Press **Send to customer**, or **Put it in their portal** if neither is ticked.

![The Send the handover pack sheet with items still to come from site, WhatsApp and Email ticks, the message and Send anyway (admin).](/help/handover/s24-send-pack-to-customer.jpg "Send the handover pack: still to come from site, WhatsApp and Email, the message, and Send anyway (admin).")

## What happens when you send

- Everything held goes into their portal.
- The combined PDF is built first if needed.
- The app moves the job to **Paperwork & handover** by itself, if it isn’t there yet.
- Outside sending hours the message waits, but the documents go in now.
- A “Send the final invoice” task is made.

After that, the tab shows **Send again** in place of **Send to customer**, and **Next: the final invoice** ([The final invoice and payments](/admin/help/handover/final-invoice)).

> **Admins only:** Before the site items are in, admins see **Send anyway (admin)**. The missing items show as “On its way”. Everyone else sees “It can go once these are in. An admin can send it sooner.”

What the customer sees is in [The customer’s side of the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/customers-side).
