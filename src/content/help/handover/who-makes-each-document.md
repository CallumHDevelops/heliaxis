Every item on the Handover tab is made in one of four ways.

| Made by | Examples | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **The app**, from the job | Covering letter, System summary and sign-off, Workmanship warranty, Completion sign-off, Switching your system off and on, Maintenance schedule, maintenance plan, the receipt, the combined pack PDF | Fill in what it asks (**Fill it in**, **Where the switches are**, **Summary details**, **The plan as agreed**, **Sign the design part**). Press **Regenerate** when it’s **Out of date**. |
| **The electrician’s sign-off** | PV inspection, string test and switch-on records, battery records, the electrical certificate | Nothing. They show **Waiting** until the electrician signs off. Use **Open the Site tab** if one is stuck. |
| **You** upload it (someone else issues it) | MCS certificate, Building Regulations certificate, Warranty insurance, Wiring diagram, System design calculations, G98 notice or G99 papers, Invoice and receipt, Other useful documents, and for now the **Solar estimate, shading and care** page (see below) | Press **Upload** (choose the file or **Take a photo**; a photo becomes a PDF page), **Replace** or **+ Upload another**. |
| **The product library** | Product manuals and warranties | Press **Add to the library** for a model that’s missing. |

Some of the app’s documents use the words and numbers in the handover settings: until those are set, that document isn’t made ([Set up the handover pack (once)](/admin/help/handover/set-up)). The electrician’s records come from their sign-off on the site app ([Sign-offs and the automatic labour PO](/admin/help/site/sign-offs-and-labour-pos)).

> **Watch out:** The app doesn’t make the **Solar estimate, shading and care** item (HX-HO-10) yet. Once the estimate and shade factor are on the job, it stays **Missing** and says: “MIS 3002’s estimate wording has to be copied into the app first. Until then, upload your own estimate page.” Upload one on every solar job, or the pack will have a gap. You can see this item in the second picture in [The Handover tab](/admin/help/handover/the-handover-tab).

For uploading, the helpers on particular items, and marking an item Not needed, see [Upload and manage handover documents](/admin/help/handover/upload-and-manage-documents).
