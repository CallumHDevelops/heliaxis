From Ready to book, a job’s **Handover** tab lists everything the customer’s pack needs, as the compliance system sets it out, with each item’s state and what’s needed next. You can also open it from the **Handover pack** card on the Overview.

## At the top

- **Handover pack**, a headline such as “2 of 21 complete · 15 waiting · 4 missing · 4 not needed”, a progress bar, the **Target**, and the pack line once it’s built (version, pages, **Download** · **Print version**).
- The **Target**: before switch-on it reads “the morning after the electrician signs off · MCS Customer Duty: within 30 days of completion”. Once the job is commissioned it gives the dates, such as “Wed 21 Oct, the morning after switching on · MCS Customer Duty: within 30 days, by Thu 19 Nov”.
- Buttons: **Send to customer** (**Send again** once sent), **Build handover pack** (**Build again**) and **+ Add a document**.
- **Can be sent once these are in** lists what must come from site first.

## Before the install day

This list is for Ready to book to Install booked (the tab starts at Ready to book): design figures on the job, the estimate page and maintenance schedule, manuals in the library, wiring diagram and design calculations uploaded, G99 papers, warranty and covering letter drafted, maintenance plan. For now the estimate page is one you upload (see [Who makes each handover document](/admin/help/handover/who-makes-each-document)).

## The items

The items come in the customer’s order: **Cover letter**, **Certificates**, **Warranties and guarantees**, **Using and looking after your system**, **Design**, **Test and inspection records**, **Grid connection**, **Manufacturer information**, **Invoices**, **Other**.

Each shows its title, its code (HX-HO-03 and so on), the customer’s one-line description, the technologies it’s for and its state: **Complete**, **Missing**, **Waiting**, **Not needed** or **Out of date**. **Not for this job (n)** lists the ones that don’t apply.

On a phone each item is a two-line card. Tap it for its actions.

![The rest of the handover items by group, each with its state and buttons.](/help/handover/s23b-handover-tab-items.jpg "The rest of the items by group, each with its state (Complete, Waiting, Missing, Not needed) and what to do.")

The tab refreshes every 30 seconds while it’s open.

> **Once switched on:** The Handover tab and the Overview card need **both** site-commissioning.sql and handover.sql, run in that order. See [What needs switching on](/admin/help/projects/what-needs-switching-on).

To fill the gaps, see [Upload and manage handover documents](/admin/help/handover/upload-and-manage-documents). To send it, see [Send the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/send-the-pack).
