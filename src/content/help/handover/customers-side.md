Once you’ve sent the pack ([Send the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/send-the-pack)), the customer’s portal shows **Your handover pack** (“Your certificates, warranties and guides, all in one place.”), with a download of everything and a print version. Items still to come show “On its way”.

They also get **Sign it** cards:

- the completion sign-off (“Sign to say the work’s finished”);
- where it’s needed, the G98 form.

![The customer’s portal on a phone: Your handover pack, Download everything, Print version and the Sign it cards.](/help/handover/o3-customer-portal-handover.jpg "The customer’s portal on a phone: Your handover pack, Download everything, Print version, and the Sign it cards.")

> **Good to know:** The documents in the portal are the digital versions, with the cream page and dark bands. The print version leaves those out to save ink. See [Digital and print versions of documents](/admin/help/projects/print-versions).

> **Once switched on:** The customer’s handover pack in their portal needs handover.sql.
