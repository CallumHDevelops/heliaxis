Once the pack has gone, the Handover tab shows **Next: the final invoice**, with the job value, the deposit paid and the balance.

![The Handover tab after sending: Sent by email, the pack line with Download and Print version, and Next: the final invoice.](/help/handover/s25a-handover-sent-final-invoice.jpg "After sending: Sent by email, the pack line with Download · Print version, and Next: the final invoice.")

## Upload the invoice

Press **Upload the final invoice**. It goes into their portal and the pack.

## Record a payment

1. Press **Record a payment**.
2. Fill in **Amount £** and the date.
3. Choose **Bank transfer**, **Card** or **Other**.
4. Add a **Reference (optional)** if there is one.
5. Tick **This is the final payment** only for the last one. It ticks “Final invoice paid” on the Complete checklist.
6. Press **Record payment**.

The receipt in the pack lists exactly these payments.

![The Final invoice sheet with Upload the final invoice and Record a payment.](/help/handover/s25b-final-invoice-sheet.jpg "The Final invoice sheet: upload it, then record a payment (amount, date, method, final payment).")

> **Watch out:** There’s no card payment link for the final invoice. They pay as the invoice says.

> **Admins only:** Only admins can **Remove** a payment.

When the final invoice is paid, the scaffold is down and the review request has gone, the job can move to Complete: see [Finish a job](/admin/help/projects/finish-a-job).
