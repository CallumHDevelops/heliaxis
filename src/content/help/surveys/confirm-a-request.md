When online booking is set to **Ask me to confirm**, a time a customer picks doesn’t book straight away. It becomes a request. The surveyor and the job’s owner (or everyone on the CRM, if neither is set) get a notification ("Survey request: …", "Tap to confirm"), the job’s next step becomes "Confirm …’s survey request", and the request appears at the top of the Diary under **Waiting for you**.

## What each request shows

- The customer (tap the name to open the job) and their postcode. **Possible duplicate** means their email or mobile matches another customer.
- The time they asked for, the survey type and how long it takes. With more than one surveyor, who would go.
- Once Outlook is connected: "Instant booking would have accepted this ✓", or "✗ Outlook couldn’t be read".
- How they’ll be told: by WhatsApp, by email, or "No WhatsApp or email for them: call to tell them".
- When it lapses if nobody confirms it.

If it’s still waiting 2 hours after it came in, the same people get one more notification: "Still waiting: …’s survey request" (not during quiet hours).

## Confirm it

1. On the Diary, find the request under **Waiting for you**.
2. Tap **Confirm**.

The app checks the time still fits first. If something has gone in the diary since, it asks whether to confirm anyway. Once it’s confirmed, the job moves to **Survey booked** and the app sends the customer their confirmation by itself. If the request says to call them, use the **Call** button to tell them.

## Turn it down

1. Tap **Offer another time**, then **OK** to confirm.
2. A card appears: "Send … another time", with a message that includes their booking link.
3. Tap **Copy message** and paste it into their WhatsApp chat or an email, or tap **Open the job to send it**. You can also tap **Call …**.
4. Tap **Done**.

> **Watch out:** The app doesn’t send the "another time" message for you. Copy it and send it yourself.

## Requests that lapse

An unconfirmed request lapses after 48 hours, or at the cut-off time on the working day before the survey, whichever comes first (but never less than 2 hours after it was made). Admins can change the 48 hours in the booking settings (**Unconfirmed requests lapse after**).

When a request lapses, the timeline says it wasn’t confirmed in time, the next step becomes "Call … to arrange the survey (request not confirmed)", and the surveyor and the job’s owner get "Survey request lapsed: …".

> **Good to know:** To stop requests altogether, an admin can switch the booking settings to **Book instantly**. Customers’ times then book straight away, as long as Outlook can be read.
