Most of what the survey needs is photos. Each photo question shows **Needed** until it has one.

## Take the photos in one go

1. Tap **📷 Take the photos (… needed)** (on the Outside screen, under **⋯** **More**, or on the Finish screen).
2. Each step shows what to photograph and a tip, for example "Stand back to get the whole front and the roof in".
3. Tap **📷 Take photo**, or **🖼 From gallery** for a picture you already have (such as a screenshot).
4. Tap **Next: …** for the next photo. Until you’ve taken this one, the button says **Skip**, which leaves it for later. **← Back** goes back.
5. Tap **Done** at the end, or **✕ Close** at any time.

The header says how many are left, for example "Photo 3 of 14 · 9 to go". Once the needed ones are done, the rest are optional.

You can also take one photo at a time: **📷 Take photo** in the bottom bar takes the next one needed on the screen you’re on, or tap **📷 Take photo** under any photo question.

## Drawing on a photo

Three photos need a drawing:

- **Roof layout (aerial, panel area drawn on)**: a photo or a screenshot of the aerial view, with the panel area and any no-go areas drawn on.
- **DC route, roof to inverter**.
- **AC route, inverter to consumer unit**.

To draw, tap **✎ Draw** next to the photo (or tap the photo, then **✎ Draw on photo**):

- **✎ Pen**, **➚ Arrow** or **T Text**, in **Yellow** or **Red**. One finger draws; two fingers zoom and move.
- **T Text** offers ready labels: **Panels here**, **No panels**, **Inverter here**, **Battery here**, **Cable route**, **Scaffold here**. Or type your own and tap **Add label**.
- **↶ Undo**, **⤢ Fit** and **Clear all**.
- Tap **Save**, or **✕ Cancel** to leave without saving.

The original photo is kept: the drawing sits on top of it.

> **Good to know:** Drawings never stop you finishing. Do them now or later in the van. A finished survey with drawings still to do says **Finished, drawings to do**, and whoever finished it gets a task to do them.

## Photos and signal

Photos are kept on the phone and upload by themselves when there’s signal. A photo’s thumbnail shows **Uploading**, **Couldn’t upload** or **✎ Drawn**.

If one couldn’t upload, tap it, then **↻ Try again**, or **Remove** and take it again. To delete an uploaded photo, tap it and tap **Delete** (you need signal for that).

> **Watch out:** If the survey says "Sign in again to upload your photos", tap **Sign in**. Photos wait on the phone until you do.
