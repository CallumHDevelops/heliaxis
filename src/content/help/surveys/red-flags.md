The survey picks out problems from the answers by itself. You don’t add red flags by hand.

## What raises a red flag

| Red flag | When |
|---|---|
| Asbestos suspected / Asbestos confirmed | The answer to **Asbestos suspected?** (an asbestos cement roof starts as Suspected) |
| Looped supply / Looped supply? (not sure) | **Looped supply? (two cables into the cut-out)** is Yes or Not sure |
| 60A main fuse | **Main fuse rating** is 60A |
| Main fuse rating unknown | **Main fuse rating** is Can’t see |
| No loft access | **Loft access** is No access |
| Structural engineer needed / Roof not suitable | The structural verdict for a roof area is Needs a structural engineer or Not suitable for panels |
| Roof covering in poor condition | **Condition of the covering** is Poor |
| Battery spot not allowed | **Battery location** is somewhere batteries can’t go, such as the loft |
| Scaffold licence needed | **Scaffold on the pavement or road?** is Yes |
| Neighbour’s consent needed | **Scaffold over a neighbour’s land?** is Yes |
| TT earthing | **Earthing** is TT (earth rod) |
| No WiFi at the inverter | **WiFi at the inverter** is No WiFi |
| … not checked: reason | A section was marked **Couldn’t check this section** |

With several roof areas, the flag names the roof area.

## Where you see them

- On the survey’s **Finish** screen, under **Red flags**.
- In the survey lists, as "⚑ 2 red flags" after the status.
- On the job’s **Surveys** tab.
- At the top of the survey report.
- In the timeline line when the survey is completed, and in the notification to the job’s owner.

## Warnings and stops

As you answer, some choices show a warning (⚠) with advice, for example "Don’t drill until it’s been tested for asbestos (HSG264)" or "60A main fuse: the DNO may need to upgrade it (free for homes). Ask when you apply." Warnings never stop you finishing.

A stop sign (⛔) means the answer can’t be used, for example "Batteries can’t go here (PAS 63100). Pick another spot." Change it, or you can’t finish the survey.

> **Good to know:** Red flags don’t stop you finishing either. They’re there so the office sees them before the design and the quote.
