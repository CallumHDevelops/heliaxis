On a phone, tap **More** at the bottom of the screen, then **Surveys and diary**.

The page has four tabs along the top: **Diary**, **To do**, **Drafts** and **Done**. Admins also see **⚙**, which opens the survey booking settings. You book surveys on the Diary. The other three tabs list the surveys themselves: see [Surveys to do, started and finished](/admin/help/surveys/survey-lists).

![The Diary on a computer, with a request waiting and two days of appointments](/help/surveys/diary.jpg "The Diary: requests waiting at the top, then each day with its drives")

## The buttons at the top

- **+ Add to my diary**: site visits, meetings and personal time, so survey times leave room for them. See [Add something to your diary](/admin/help/surveys/add-to-my-diary).
- **Find a time**: the times that fit for a customer, and booking one. See [Book a survey with Find a time](/admin/help/surveys/find-a-time).
- **Refresh**: loads the Diary again. Once Outlook is connected the button says **Refresh (read Outlook again)** and reads Outlook again straight away.

## Messages you may see

- **Outlook isn’t connected yet**: the Diary only knows the surveys in the CRM and what you’ve added. Customers can still request a time, and you confirm it here.
- **Couldn’t read …’s Outlook**: times are worked out without it, and nothing is booked instantly until it can be read.
- **Online booking is switched off**: customers can’t book yet. You can still find times and book them here.

## Waiting for you

Survey times customers have asked for online, each with **Confirm** and **Offer another time**. This part only shows when something is waiting. See [Confirm or turn down a survey request](/admin/help/surveys/confirm-a-request).

## Copy these dates into Outlook

A one-off list that appears once Outlook is connected: upcoming surveys, installs and diary entries that were set before then. See [Outlook and the Microsoft 365 calendar](/admin/help/surveys/outlook-calendar).

## Next 3 weeks

One card per day. The heading gives the date (today’s says **Today**), the surveyor’s hours or **No surveys**, and how many surveys are booked. With more than one surveyor, each has their own run of days under their name.

Inside a day, in order:

- **From home** (or the time they leave, if a leave-home time is set): their home postcode and the drive to the first appointment.
- Each appointment with its time and place. Outlook appointments say **(Outlook)** and entries you added say **(your diary)**. A customer’s request shows **waiting for you**. A time a customer is holding on the booking page shows **held online**.
- The drive between appointments, with the buffer and the coffee break, for example "→ 25 min · buffer 15 · coffee 15". **+ rush hour** means the drive falls in rush hour.
- **Home**: the drive back, with "home by" if a home-by time is set.
- **Free for jobs nearby**: a gap long enough for another survey.
- Why customers can’t book that day, for example "Bank holiday", "Blocked all day: …", "4 surveys already" or "Too soon for customers to book".

Tap a survey or install to open the job. Tap one of your own diary entries to change or delete it.

Notes under an appointment point out problems: an Outlook appointment with no location (add a postcode in Outlook so the drive can be worked out), a postcode with no exact location, or an appointment moved in Outlook. An all-day Outlook appointment marked Free doesn’t block the day. Mark days off as Away in Outlook instead.

> **Good to know:** Drive times in the Diary are the app’s own estimate. They err on the long side and are longer in rush hour.

> **Once switched on:** The Diary appears once survey-booking.sql has been run.
