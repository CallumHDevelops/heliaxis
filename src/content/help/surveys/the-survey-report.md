Open the report from:

- **Surveys and diary › Done**: tap **Report** on the row.
- The survey itself: **See the report** on a finished survey, or **⋯** **More** › **Survey report** at any time.
- The job’s **Surveys** tab: tap a finished survey (**Report →**).

## What’s on it

- The customer, address, date, surveyor, job reference and status.
- Four chips: **DNO route**, **Planning**, **Structural** and how much is **Done**, with a line explaining the DNO route and planning.
- **Red flags**, if there are any.
- **Still to do**: anything still needed, drawings to do, and what the customer will send.
- **For the ENA application**: the main electric cut-out and meter photos, each with **⬇ Download**. See [Survey photos for the ENA application](/admin/help/surveys/ena-photos).
- Every screen’s answers and photos, with the drawings shown on the photos. Unanswered questions say **Needed, not filled** or **Not filled**.
- **Finish**: the suggested system, your notes, and the customer’s sign-off with their signature.

## Print it or save a PDF

Tap **🖨 Print or save as PDF** and choose your printer, or **Save as PDF**. The report is laid out for A4.

**← Survey** goes back to the survey, and **Open the job** opens the job.

> **Good to know:** All of this is for solar surveys. A finished heat pump survey’s report (from the job’s **Surveys** tab) only shows its SimpliHeat link, the SimpliHeat report, notes and photos: see [Heat pump surveys (SimpliHeat)](/admin/help/surveys/heat-pump-surveys).
