## The screens

The survey follows a visit, one screen at a time:

1. **Chat with the customer**: the property, what they want, requests and things to avoid.
2. **Outside**: photos of the house, scaffold and access.
3. **Roof**: each roof area.
4. **Loft**: access and the roof structure.
5. **Electrics**: cut-out, fuse, meter, consumer unit, earthing.
6. **Kit spots**: where the inverter and battery go.
7. **Cable routes**: the DC and AC routes, drawn on photos.
8. **Finish**: see [Finish a survey](/admin/help/surveys/finish-a-survey).

For a battery-only survey, the Roof and Loft screens are left out.

## Moving around

- **Next →** and **← Back** along the bottom.
- The bar at the top shows where you are (for example **3/8 Roof**) and how much is done as a percentage. Tap it to see every section with how many things each still needs, and jump to one.
- Under the bar, a line says how saving is going: **Saved ✓**, **Saved on this phone: uploads when you have signal**, or how many photos are waiting.
- **⋯** opens **More**: take the photos, **✍ Customer sign-off**, **Open the job**, **Survey report** and **Cancel this survey**.

## Questions

- **Needed** next to a question means you can’t finish without it. A **✓** means it’s done.
- Some questions only appear when they matter, for example the landlord’s name only if the customer doesn’t own the property.
- **▾ More detail** opens optional questions.
- **Customer will send** under **Yearly electricity use** and **Supplier and tariff** marks them done when the customer will send the details later. When you finish, the app makes a task to get them.
- **Couldn’t check this section** (Scaffold and access, Loft, Electrics, Kit spots): pick a reason such as **No access** or **Not safe to check**, or type one. Its questions are skipped and a red flag says why. **Undo: check it now** brings them back.

## Roof areas

On the Roof and Loft screens, each roof area has its own tab. Tap **+ Add roof area** for another face (up to 6) and give it a name. To remove one (any but the first), use **Remove …** at the bottom of its Roof questions. Its answers go; its photos stay on the job.

## Measuring with the phone

- **Direction the roof faces**: tap the nearest direction, or tap **🧭 Compass**, stand facing the slope with the phone flat, top edge towards the roof, then tap **Use …**.
- **Roof pitch** (on the Loft screen): tap **📐 Measure with the phone**, lay the phone along a rafter with the long edge down the slope, then tap **Use …°** (or **Stop**).

If the phone can’t measure, it says so: pick the nearest value instead.

## What the customer said

Tap **💬 Customer said…** at any time, type it and tap **Save**. It’s added with the time under **Requests from the customer**.

> **Good to know:** A warning (⚠) under an answer is advice, for example "TT earthing: check and test the earth rod before installing." A stop sign (⛔) means that answer can’t be used, for example a battery spot that isn’t allowed: change it before you finish.
