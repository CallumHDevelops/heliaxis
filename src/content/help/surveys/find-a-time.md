Find a time looks at each surveyor’s day (surveys, installs, Outlook and diary entries), works out the drive either side, and shows the times that fit. Times that add little driving are marked **Nearby**.

## Open it

- On the Diary, tap **Find a time**, or
- On the customer’s job, tap **Find a time** under the survey date (or the main button once a survey is booked). It opens with the customer picked and their times already found.

## Book a time

1. Under **Customer**, search by name, mobile or postcode, then tap the customer. Lost and Complete jobs aren’t listed.
2. Under **Survey**, pick **Solar**, **Heat pump** or **Solar + heat pump**. The app usually picks it from the job’s products.
3. Check the **Postcode**. If the job has none, or the survey is somewhere else, type it and tap **Find times**.
4. Tap a time. Each one shows who would go and the extra driving, for example "Nearby · +5 min driving" or "no extra driving".
5. Tap **Book it: …** (the button shows the day and time you picked).

The Diary then shows "Booked ✓" with the customer, the time and the surveyor.

> **Good to know:** If the customer already has a survey, the sheet says "Booking a new time moves their survey from …". Their current time doesn’t count as busy while you look.

## When there are no times

- Open **Why some days have no times** to see each day’s reason, for example "Bank holiday", "4 surveys already" or "Too far from home (about 70 min)".
- "Too far from home for the usual rules" or "No times fit in the next 3 weeks" means nothing fits the normal rules. Use **Book anyway…** if you still want to go.
- A job with no solar, battery or heat pump on it doesn’t need a survey. Use **+ Add to my diary** for a home visit, or pick **Solar** or **Heat pump** if they want a survey too.

## Book anyway

1. Pick the customer first.
2. Tap **Book anyway…**.
3. Set the **Day** and the **Arrive at** time. With more than one surveyor, pick **Who**.
4. Tap **Book … survey anyway** (for example **Book solar survey anyway**).

This ignores the usual hours, distance and daily limits. It’s still refused if it overlaps something in the diary. If the surveyor’s diary is blocked all day, the app asks you to confirm first. The timeline records that it was booked outside the usual rules, and why.

## What happens next

- The job moves to **Survey booked** and gets the survey date.
- The timeline says who booked it, for example "Survey booked by Esther".
- Once Outlook is connected, the survey is copied into the surveyor’s Outlook.
- The app sends the customer a booking confirmation by itself.
- A postcode you typed in Find a time becomes the job’s postcode.

To give the customer the choice instead, tap **Send booking link**: see [Send a customer their booking link](/admin/help/surveys/send-a-booking-link). To move or cancel a survey, see [Move or cancel a booked survey](/admin/help/surveys/move-or-cancel-a-survey).

> **Once switched on:** Find a time works once survey-booking.sql has been run. Until then, the job’s button adds the survey date by hand.
