The heat pump survey itself is done in SimpliHeat. The app keeps a record of it on the job, so everyone can find the link and the report, and the job moves on when it’s done.

## Start one

- On the job’s **Surveys** tab, tap **Add heat pump survey (SimpliHeat)**, or
- On **Surveys and diary › To do**, tap **+ New survey**, pick the customer and job, then **Heat pump (SimpliHeat)** and **Start survey**, or
- From Home’s **Today** list, tap **Start survey** for a heat pump survey.

## Fill it in

1. Do the survey in SimpliHeat.
2. Paste the SimpliHeat link into **SimpliHeat link** and tap **Save link**. If the job already has a SimpliHeat link, tap **Use the job’s SimpliHeat link** instead. **Open in SimpliHeat ↗** opens it.
3. Tap **📄 Upload SimpliHeat report (PDF)** and pick the PDF.
4. Add any **Notes** (they save when you tap away, or tap **Save notes**).
5. Add **Photos** worth keeping with the job: the outside unit spot, the cylinder, the boiler.
6. Tap **✓ Mark complete**.

**✓ Mark complete** only works once the link is saved or the report is uploaded. The job then moves to **Surveyed**.

> **Good to know:** The link starts with https://. Saving a SimpliHeat link here also puts it on the job, if the job doesn’t have one yet.

> **Watch out:** Saving the link, the notes and uploading the report need signal. Photos are kept on the phone and upload when there’s signal.

## Solar and heat pump together

A job booked for **Solar + heat pump** gets both a solar survey and a heat pump survey. If a customer tells you at a solar survey that they’re interested in a heat pump, finishing the solar survey adds a task to book a SimpliHeat heat pump survey for them.
