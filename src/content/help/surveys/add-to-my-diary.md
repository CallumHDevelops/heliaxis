Anything you add counts as busy, and where it is counts for the drive to and from the customers either side. Customers can’t book over it, and Find a time leaves room to get there and back.

You can also start one from Home: tap **+ Add to my diary** under **Today**.

## Add an entry

1. On the Diary, tap **+ Add to my diary**.
2. Under **What**, pick **Site visit**, **Install**, **Meeting**, **Personal** or **Other**.
3. Type a **Title**, for example "Roof check for Jones".
4. With more than one surveyor, pick **Whose diary**.
5. Under **When**, pick the **Day**, then the **Start** and **End**. The − and + buttons move a time by 15 minutes. Moving the start keeps the same length.
6. For whole days (a holiday, training), tick **All day** and pick the **First day** and **Last day** instead.
7. Under **Where**, pick one:
   - **At an address**: type the **Postcode** (the **Address (optional)** too if you like). The postcode is needed so the drive can be worked out.
   - **At home**: no driving before or after.
   - **Online / phone**: counts as wherever you were before it.
8. If it’s about a customer, search under **Job (optional)** and tap the job. Its postcode fills in if you haven’t typed one.
9. Tap **Add to my diary**.

The Diary shows it with **(your diary)** after the title.

## Change or delete an entry

1. On the Diary, tap the entry.
2. Change what you need and tap **Save**.
3. To remove it, tap **Delete**, then **Yes, delete it** (or **Keep it**).

> **Good to know:** Once Microsoft 365 is connected, each entry is copied into Outlook too, and deleting it removes it from Outlook within a minute. If the copy fails, the entry shows "Couldn’t copy it to Outlook" with the reason when you open it.

> **Once switched on:** Diary entries need survey-booking.sql to have been run.
