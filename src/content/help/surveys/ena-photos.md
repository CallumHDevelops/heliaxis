The DNO application on ENA (Connect Direct) needs two photos, and the solar survey takes both on the **Electrics** screen:

- **Main electric cut-out (DNO)**: a close-up where the fuse rating and the seal can be read, plus a wide shot with the meter.
- **Meter, with the serial number and MPAN visible**.

Both are Needed, so a solar survey can’t be finished without them (unless the whole Electrics section was marked **Couldn’t check this section**).

## Download them

From the survey report:

1. Open the survey report (**Surveys and diary › Done › Report**).
2. Under **For the ENA application**, tap **⬇ Download** under each photo.

Or from the job: on the job’s Overview, open **Paperwork** (on a job page with no Paperwork card yet, it’s a card of its own on the Overview). The same photos are under **For the ENA application**, each with **⬇ Download**. They always come from the job’s latest finished solar survey. See [Paperwork and deadlines](/admin/help/projects/paperwork-and-deadlines).

**⬇ Download** saves the original photo, without any drawing on it, named after the job (for example HX-0042-main-cut-out.jpg).

> **Good to know:** If the job has no finished solar survey yet, the card says where the photos are taken, with **Open the survey →** or **Start solar survey**.

> **Watch out:** If a photo says "Can’t download right now. Reload the page to try again.", reload the page and tap **⬇ Download** again.
