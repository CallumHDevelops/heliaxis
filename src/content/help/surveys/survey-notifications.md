Tap a notification to open what it’s about: the job, the Diary (for a request) or the survey. Most booking notifications go to the surveyor and the job’s owner. If neither is set (or the surveyor’s Outlook email doesn’t match anyone’s sign-in), they go to everyone on the CRM. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications) for switching them on.

## Bookings

| Notification | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Survey request: name, time ("Tap to confirm") | A customer asked for a time and bookings are set to **Ask me to confirm** | Confirm it or offer another time: see [Confirm or turn down a survey request](/admin/help/surveys/confirm-a-request) |
| Still waiting: name’s survey request | The request is still there 2 hours later (not sent during quiet hours) | The same |
| Survey request lapsed: name | Nobody confirmed it in time | Call them to arrange a time. The job’s next step says so |
| 📅 Survey booked: name, area | A customer booked online and it went straight in | Nothing: the customer gets their confirmation by itself |
| Survey moved: time | A customer moved their survey on their link and it went straight in (with **Ask me to confirm**, you get "Survey request" instead) | Nothing, unless the new time doesn’t suit |
| Survey cancelled: name (time) | A customer cancelled on their link | Call to rebook. The job’s next step is "Call to rebook the survey" |
| Call name to arrange a survey | Someone on the website form was too far for online booking, or found no times, and tapped **Ask us to call me** | Ring them. The new job’s next step says the same |
| Call name: their online booking didn’t go through | Their time went to someone else while they were filling in the form | Ring them to book a time |
| Possible duplicate of …: name ("Check before you call them") | A website booking used the email or mobile of a customer already in the CRM | Check both jobs before you call. To join them, see [Merge a customer who’s in twice](/admin/help/customers/merge-duplicates) |

## Surveys on site

| Notification | Who gets it | When |
|---|---|---|
| name’s survey uploaded ✓ | The person who finished it | Everything from **✓ Finish on site** has reached the office |
| Survey done: name | The job’s owner, if someone else did the survey | The same, with how many red flags |
| name: survey not finished | The person who finished it | Finishing failed when it reached the office, for example a photo didn’t upload. Open the survey to see what’s still needed |
| Was name’s survey done? | The job’s owner (or everyone on the CRM if it has none) | 3 hours after the survey should have finished, the job is still at **Survey booked** and no survey was finished in the app. Mark the job as Surveyed, or set a new survey date |

See [Finish a survey](/admin/help/surveys/finish-a-survey).

> **Good to know:** Booking a survey yourself in Find a time doesn’t send you a notification: the Diary shows "Booked ✓" instead.
