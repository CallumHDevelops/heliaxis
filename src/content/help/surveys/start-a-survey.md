The solar survey runs on your phone, in the app or the browser. It’s built for a roof: one screen at a time in the order of a visit, big buttons, and the screen kept on while you work.

## Before you go

Open the app with signal on the morning (or the day before). Surveys booked for today and tomorrow are copied to the phone whenever the app is open with signal, so they open later with no signal at all. A survey copied to the phone says **Ready offline ✓** at the top.

## Ways to start it

- **Home**: under **Today**, tap **Start survey** next to the customer. **Directions** opens your maps app. See [Today’s surveys, installs and diary](/admin/help/dashboard/today).
- **Surveys and diary › To do**: tap **Start** on the row (or **Continue** if it’s already started).
- **The job**: on the **Surveys** tab, tap **Start solar survey**. On the day of the survey, **Start survey** is also the job’s main button.
- **+ New survey** on the To do tab, for a survey that isn’t booked: see [Surveys to do, started and finished](/admin/help/surveys/survey-lists).

A job only has one open survey of each kind, so starting it again opens the same one.

## The top of the survey

- The customer’s name, address, **Solar survey**, the date and the job reference.
- **📍 Directions** and **📞 Call**.
- **← Surveys** goes back to the list.

The first screen, **Chat with the customer**, already has the address and what the survey is for, filled in from the job. Check them with the customer.

Next: [Find your way round the solar survey](/admin/help/surveys/fill-in-the-survey).

> **Good to know:** Everything you enter is kept on the phone first and uploads when there’s signal. You don’t need to press Save.

> **Watch out:** If you sign out with changes that haven’t uploaded, those surveys stay on the phone until they’ve gone up. Get signal and let them upload before you hand the phone to someone else.

> **Once switched on:** Surveys need surveys.sql to have been run.
