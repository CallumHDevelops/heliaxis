Customers can book a survey in two places:

- **Their own booking link**, sent from the job. It knows who they are, fills in their details and books against their job.
- **The website form** at heliaxis.co.uk/book-a-survey. Anyone can use it. Each booking there makes a new customer and opportunity in the CRM. If the email or mobile matches an existing customer, the app adds "Possible duplicate of …" to the new job’s timeline and sends a notification, so you can check before you call.

## The steps

1. **What’s the survey for?**: **Solar panels (and battery)**, **Heat pump** or **Both**. On their own link it’s usually picked already.
2. **Your postcode**, then **Find times**.
3. They pick a day, then a morning or afternoon time. **Soonest** jumps to the first time available, and "Good day: we’re nearby" marks times when a surveyor is already close by. Only times that fit the surveyor’s day and the drive are offered.
4. **Continue**. The time is held for them while they fill in their details (10 minutes unless an admin changes it). On the website form they tick the "I’m not a robot" box first, if that check is switched on.
5. **Is this right?** (on their link) or **Your details**: name, mobile, email, house number and street, town (optional), postcode, a tick for "OK to message me on WhatsApp about my survey", and anything we should know about parking, access or pets.
6. **Book my survey** when bookings are instant, or **Send my request** when you confirm them.

They then see "You’re booked: …" with **Add to my calendar**, or "Thanks! We’ve got your request for …". A booking that goes straight in sends the surveyor and the job’s owner a notification ("📅 Survey booked: …"). A request comes to the Diary under **Waiting for you**, and you get a notification: see [Confirm or turn down a survey request](/admin/help/surveys/confirm-a-request).

## Changing or cancelling

On their link, a booked survey shows "Your survey: …" with **Change time** and **Cancel**. They can do this online until a set number of hours before the survey (24 unless an admin changes it in the booking settings). After that, the page asks them to call.

- **Change time** (only while online booking is switched on): when bookings are instant it moves straight away, and the surveyor and the job’s owner get "Survey moved: …". Otherwise the new time comes to you to confirm.
- **Cancel**: the job keeps its stage, the survey date is cleared, the next step becomes "Call to rebook the survey", and the surveyor and the job’s owner get a notification.

## When they can’t book online

- **"You’re a little further than we can book online"**: on the website form they can tap **Ask us to call me**. That makes a new customer with the next step "Call to arrange survey (outside online booking area)". On their own link, they’re asked to call or WhatsApp.
- **"We don’t have any times online in the next 3 weeks"**: the same choices.
- **"Online booking is coming soon"**: online booking is switched off in the booking settings.
- **"This link has expired"**: the link is wrong, or the job has moved past Survey booked. The page offers **WhatsApp us** and a call button.

> **Good to know:** Customers only see times, and once they’re booked, the surveyor’s first name. They never see the rest of the diary, other customers or the drive times.
