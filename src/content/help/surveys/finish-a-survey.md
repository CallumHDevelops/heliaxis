## The Finish screen

The last screen pulls everything together:

- **Still needed**: everything that stops you finishing. Tap one to go to it. **📷 Take the photos** works through the missing photos.
- **Drawings to do**: photos still to draw on. They don’t stop you finishing.
- **Red flags**: see [Red flags and warnings](/admin/help/surveys/red-flags).
- **What it means**: the **DNO route** (G98 or G99, from the inverter size), **Planning** and **Structural**, worked out from your answers.
- **Suggested system**: number of panels, system size in kWp, inverter size, battery size (when there’s a battery) and the inverter make and model. The inverter size gives the DNO route.
- **Your notes**.
- **Customer sign-off**.
- **Not filled**: things worth doing that never stop you finishing. **Skip the rest** hides the list.

## The customer’s sign-off

1. Tap **✍ Customer sign-off**.
2. Check the **Customer’s name** and tap **Next: sign**.
3. The customer reads "I agree the kit locations and cable routes we discussed." and signs with their finger.
4. Tap **Save signature**.

To redo it, tap **Sign again**. **Remove the signature** clears it.

## Finish

1. Tap **✓ Finish on site** (or **Finish** in the bottom bar). It only works once nothing is still needed and no answer has a stop sign (⛔).
2. If the customer hasn’t signed, the app asks whether to finish without their signature.

This works with no signal. The survey says **Finished, uploading** until its answers and photos have gone up, then completes by itself: keep the app open, or open it again when you have signal. **Undo finish to change something** takes you back.

If finishing fails when it reaches the server (for example, a photo didn’t upload), you get a notification and the survey says why and what’s still needed. Fix it and tap **✓ Finish on site** again.

## What happens when it completes

- The job moves to **Surveyed**, and the timeline says who completed it, how many photos and any red flags.
- You get a notification that the survey has uploaded. The job’s owner (if it isn’t you) gets "Survey done: …".
- If you changed the site address in the survey, the job’s address is updated. If the office changed the job’s address while the survey was open, theirs is kept and the timeline asks you to check which is right.
- The app adds tasks when needed: finish the drawings, get the electricity bill or the supplier and tariff the customer will send, and "Book a SimpliHeat heat pump survey for …" if they said they’re interested in a heat pump and the job has no heat pump survey yet.

Then open the report: see [The survey report](/admin/help/surveys/the-survey-report).

## Reopen or cancel

- **Reopen** (on a finished survey) lets you change it. Only the person who finished it, or an admin, can reopen it. If the customer signed, they’ll need to sign again.
- **Cancel this survey** (under **⋯** **More**) keeps it on the job as cancelled. **Bring it back** reopens a cancelled survey.

Both are noted on the job’s timeline.
