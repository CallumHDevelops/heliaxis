Each job has its own booking link. The customer opens it, picks a time that fits the surveyor’s day and the drive, and the survey books itself, or comes to you to confirm, depending on the booking settings. They can use the same link later to change or cancel. See [What the customer sees when they book](/admin/help/surveys/customer-booking-page).

## From the job

1. Open the customer’s job (see [A customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/customer-page)).
2. Tap **Send booking link**. It’s the main button once they’ve been contacted, and it also sits under the survey date.
3. Tap **WhatsApp it** to open the customer’s WhatsApp chat in the app with the message ready to send, or **Copy link** to paste it somewhere else. If they have no mobile number yet, the app asks you to add it first.

The message reads like this: "Hi Jane, you can pick a time for your free survey here: … It takes about an hour."

## From Find a time

1. On the Diary, tap **Find a time** and pick the customer.
2. Tap **Send booking link**.
3. Tap **Copy message** or **Copy link**, and paste it into their WhatsApp chat or an email. Or tap **Open the job to send it**.

Either way, the job’s timeline notes "Booking link created".

## When there’s no link

- Booking links are only for jobs at **Lead**, **Contacted** or **Survey booked**. Once the job moves on, the link shows "This link has expired".
- A job with only infrared, an EV charger, LED lighting or Other on it doesn’t need a survey, so there’s no link. Use **+ Add to my diary** for a home visit, or add **Solar PV** or **Heat pump** to the job if they want a survey.

> **Good to know:** If someone set the survey date on the job by hand, the customer’s link shows that date, but they have to call to change it. To move or cancel a survey yourself, see [Move or cancel a booked survey](/admin/help/surveys/move-or-cancel-a-survey).

> **Once switched on:** Booking links work once survey-booking.sql has been run.
