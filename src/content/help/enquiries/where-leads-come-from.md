You don’t need to add website, Meta or WhatsApp leads yourself. They arrive on Enquiries by themselves.

## The ways in

| How they got in touch | Shows on Enquiries as |
| --- | --- |
| A form on the website (quote, contact or survey booking) | **Website** (or **Website booking**) |
| A Facebook or Instagram lead form | **Meta**, with the ad they came from |
| The website, after clicking a Meta ad | **Meta**, with the campaign from the ad link |
| A WhatsApp message to the business number | **WhatsApp** |
| A WhatsApp chat started from a Meta ad | **Meta** ("WhatsApp ad") |
| Added by someone on the team in the CRM | **Other** ("Added by staff") |
| Brought in with Import | Meta or WhatsApp, as above |

For the exact wording each source shows, see [Lead sources and ad names](/admin/help/enquiries/lead-sources).

## What the app does when a lead arrives

For a website form, a Meta lead form or a new WhatsApp number, the app:

1. Adds them to Enquiries at **New**.
2. Makes an opportunity for them in the CRM at **Lead**, with a line on its timeline saying where they came from (for example "New enquiry via Meta ad “Video 4”").
3. Gives the opportunity to the next person on the rota, if one is set in **CRM settings** under **Share new leads between (in turn)**. That person gets a "New lead for you" notification. See [Choose who gets new leads](/admin/help/admin/new-leads).
4. Notifies everyone with Enquiries access: "New enquiry: …" for the website, "New Meta lead: …" for a lead form and "New WhatsApp lead: …" for a new WhatsApp number.

Also:

- **Website forms** email the details to the office inbox as well (hello@heliaxis.co.uk, unless an admin has set another address). If they ticked the WhatsApp box on the form, that permission is recorded.
- **Meta lead forms** can start a first WhatsApp 8–13 minutes later (the template for the ad they came from), when first messages are switched on to go by themselves and the lead agreed to WhatsApp. The notification then says when: "WhatsApp first message (“…”) goes at 10:42 today." See [First messages by video](/admin/help/whatsapp/first-messages-by-video).
- **A first WhatsApp message** from a new number makes a new lead named from their WhatsApp profile. A number already in the CRM goes on that customer’s chat instead.
- **A survey booked online** also lands on Enquiries, as **Website booking**, with its job already made. The booking itself is handled in Surveys and diary. See [Confirm or turn down a survey request](/admin/help/surveys/confirm-a-request) and [What the customer sees when they book](/admin/help/surveys/customer-booking-page).

> **Good to know:** If nobody gets in touch in time, the lead’s owner (or the whole CRM team, when it has no owner) gets "… hasn’t been contacted". The time is set in **CRM settings** under **Alert if a new lead hasn’t been contacted within**. See [Contact a new lead](/admin/help/enquiries/contact-a-lead).

## Leads you add or import

- Someone who phoned or was referred: add them with **+ New** in the CRM. See [Add a new customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer).
- Older Meta leads: see [Import older Meta leads](/admin/help/enquiries/import-meta-leads). Imported people keep their original dates, and nobody is notified or messaged.

## If something looks wrong

- **Meta leads:** live leads must be **On** for the Facebook Page. An admin can check on **Import**: see [Meta lead ads coming in](/admin/help/enquiries/meta-lead-ads).
- **Someone already in the CRM** (same phone or email) who fills in a form again may be added as a second enquiry. Search for them, and merge if they’re in twice: see [Merge a customer who’s in twice](/admin/help/customers/merge-duplicates).
- Press **Refresh** at the top of Enquiries to load the newest leads.
