The quicker a new lead hears from you, the better. Reports measure it as **Typical first reply**, and the app chases the team when a lead waits too long.

## Get in touch from Enquiries

1. Find the lead. The **New** card shows leads still at New (one that has had the automatic WhatsApp intro shows as Contacted instead: see below).
2. Press **View** on their row.
3. Pick how to get in touch:
   - **WhatsApp** opens their chat in the WhatsApp inbox, where you can write to them or send a template.
   - **Call** rings their number from a phone.
   - **Reply by email** opens a new email to them in your own email app.

**WhatsApp** and **Call** only show when they gave a phone number.

## What counts as getting in touch

The app treats a lead as contacted the first time someone on the team:

- sends them a WhatsApp from the app (or types one in the WhatsApp Business app on the same number),
- emails them from their job in the CRM, or from the mailbox connected to the CRM,
- logs a call, site visit or email on their job,
- starts a survey for them.

Then their job moves from **Lead** to **Contacted**, their status on Enquiries goes to **Contacted**, and the new-lead and "hasn’t been contacted" notifications about them clear for everyone.

> **Watch out:** Pressing **Call** or **Reply by email** on Enquiries doesn’t record anything. After a phone call, log it on their job so the app knows: see [Add a note, log a call or a site visit](/admin/help/customers/notes-calls-visits). An email from your own mailbox only counts if it’s the mailbox connected to the CRM.

> **Good to know:** The automatic WhatsApp intro doesn’t count as getting in touch. It changes the status on Enquiries to **Contacted**, but the job stays at **Lead** and the "hasn’t been contacted" alert still goes until a person gets in touch.

## The "hasn’t been contacted" alert

If a new lead is still at **Lead** with nobody in touch after the time set in **CRM settings** (**Alert if a new lead hasn’t been contacted within (minutes, 0 = off)**), the app sends "⏱ … hasn’t been contacted". It goes to the job’s owner, or to everyone with CRM access when it has no owner. Tapping it opens their job. See [Choose who gets new leads](/admin/help/admin/new-leads).

The alert only goes once per job, and only for jobs made in the last 3 days. It doesn’t go for imported leads, surveys booked online, a job started from a WhatsApp chat, or a second job for an existing customer.
