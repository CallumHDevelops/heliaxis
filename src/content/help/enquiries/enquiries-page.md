**CRM › Enquiries** lists everyone who has got in touch, newest first. You need Enquiries access to open it. On a phone it’s under **More** (or it’s a tab at the bottom, if you have Enquiries but not CRM).

![The Enquiries page](/help/enquiries/enquiries-page.jpg "Status cards, the From buttons, search and the list")

## At the top

Under the **Enquiries** heading it says how many leads there are. When you filter, it says how many are showing, for example "12 of 340 leads (New, from Meta)", with **Show all** to clear the filters.

The buttons on the right:

| Button | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp inbox** | Opens the WhatsApp inbox | Everyone on this page |
| **Smart replies** | Opens Smart replies & chasers | Everyone on this page |
| **Import** | Brings in older Meta leads and WhatsApp chats | Admins |
| **Select** | Tick people to delete or merge them (it turns into **Done**) | Admins |
| **Refresh** | Loads the list again | Everyone on this page |

If notifications aren’t on for the device you’re using, a banner offers **Turn on** so new enquiries and WhatsApp messages reach you straight away. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).

## Status cards and From buttons

Five cards, **New**, **Contacted**, **Quoted**, **Won** and **Lost**, show how many leads are at each status. Below them, **From** has **All**, **Meta**, **Website**, **WhatsApp** and, when there are any, **Other**. Click any of them to filter the list. See [Find the leads you need](/admin/help/enquiries/find-leads) and [Enquiry statuses](/admin/help/enquiries/statuses).

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Their name, customer reference (such as HX-0042) and email. Meta leads also show a small **Meta** tag with the ad they came from |
| **Location** | Their town, found from the postcode, with the postcode beneath |
| **Sector** | Residential, Commercial or what the form said |
| **Interested in** | Icons for what they asked about. Point at one on a computer to see its name |
| **Received** | When they got in touch: "Just now", "5m ago", "3h ago", "Yesterday", "3 days ago", then the date |
| **Status** | A drop-down to change their status |

Press **View** at the end of a row to open their details.

## A lead’s details

The panel shows their name, then their reference, sector, organisation, where they came from ("via Meta ad · Video 4") and when. Below that:

- A map, when their postcode can be found.
- **Email**, **Phone**, **Postcode** and **Source**. If their ad has been renamed since, the old name shows under the source ("was Video 1").
- **Interested in**: Solar PV Installation, Battery Storage, Infrared Heating, LED Lighting, Consultation & Advisory and Funding & Grants, with what they picked lit up.
- **Message**: what they wrote, or "No message".

Then the buttons:

- **Open in CRM**: opens their job in the CRM. Needs CRM access. See [Turn an enquiry into an opportunity](/admin/help/enquiries/open-in-crm).
- **Reply by email**, **Call** and **WhatsApp** (the last two only when they gave a phone number). See [Contact a new lead](/admin/help/enquiries/contact-a-lead).
- **Delete customer**: admins only. See [Delete a customer](/admin/help/customers/delete-customers).

Close the panel with **×** or by clicking outside it.

> **Good to know:** The filters and the open lead are kept in the page’s address, so a refresh keeps them and you can bookmark a filtered view. What you type in the search isn’t kept.

> **Admins only:** **Select** puts a tick box by each name, with **Select all (12)**, how many are selected, **Delete selected (2)** and **Merge 2 selected** at the bottom. **Merge 2 selected** only works when exactly two people are ticked. See [Delete a customer](/admin/help/customers/delete-customers) and [Merge a customer who’s in twice](/admin/help/customers/merge-duplicates).

> **Once switched on:** Customer references (HX-0042) need customer-refs.sql. Until it has been run, the list has no references and the search box says "Search name, email or phone".
