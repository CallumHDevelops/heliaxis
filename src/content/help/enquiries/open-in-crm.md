Website, Meta and WhatsApp leads get an opportunity in the CRM by themselves when they arrive, at **Lead**. So turning an enquiry into a customer is usually just opening the job that’s already there.

## Open a lead’s job

1. On **Enquiries**, press **View** on their row.
2. Press **Open in CRM**. It says **Opening…** for a moment.
3. Their job opens in the CRM.

Which job opens:

- their open job (one that isn’t Complete or Lost; the newest, if they have more than one), or
- if every job is Complete or Lost, their latest job, or
- if they have no job at all, a new opportunity the app makes there and then.

**Open in CRM** only shows for people with CRM access.

## When the app makes a new opportunity

If the lead has no job yet, **Open in CRM** makes one:

- at **Lead**,
- named after them and their postcode (for example "Jane Evans · NP20 4AB"),
- with you as its owner,
- with the products they asked about ticked (Solar PV, Battery, Infrared heating, LED lighting).

It isn’t shared out on the rota. Like any new lead at **Lead**, if nobody is in touch within the time set in **CRM settings**, the "… hasn’t been contacted" alert goes to you as its owner.

Change any of it on the job’s page. From there you work them through the sales stages: see [Sales stages and moving a customer along](/admin/help/customers/sales-stages).

> **Good to know:** The CRM home also tells you about enquiries without a job ("2 enquiries aren’t in the CRM yet") with **Review**, where you can press **Create opportunity** for each. See [Add a new customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer).

## A second job for the same customer

**Open in CRM** never makes a second job for someone who already has one. To start another job for them (say, a battery a year after their panels), use **+ New** in the CRM and pick them: see [Add a new customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer).

## Someone who isn’t on Enquiries

People who phoned, were referred or walked in aren’t on Enquiries until you add them. Add them with **+ New** in the CRM. They then show on Enquiries as **Added by staff**.

> **Watch out:** If **Open in CRM** shows an error instead of opening the job, ask an admin: the CRM may not be fully set up yet.
