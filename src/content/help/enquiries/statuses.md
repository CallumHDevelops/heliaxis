Every lead on Enquiries has one of five statuses. It’s a summary of where the customer is up to, and it follows their job in the CRM by itself.

## What each status means

| Status | Their job in the CRM is at |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Lead: nobody has been in touch yet |
| **Contacted** | Contacted, Survey booked or Surveyed |
| **Quoted** | Quote sent |
| **Won** | Won or any stage after it, up to Complete |
| **Lost** | Lost, on every job they have |

When a customer has more than one job, the furthest-on job that isn’t Lost sets the status (a Complete job counts as **Won**). They only show as **Lost** when every job is lost. See [Sales stages and moving a customer along](/admin/help/customers/sales-stages) for the stages themselves.

## How it changes by itself

- When someone on the team first gets in touch, the job moves from Lead to Contacted and the status goes to **Contacted**. See [Contact a new lead](/admin/help/enquiries/contact-a-lead) for what counts.
- When the automatic WhatsApp intro goes to a new lead, the status changes to **Contacted** too. Their job stays at Lead until someone on the team gets in touch.
- Each time the job moves stage in the CRM, the status catches up.

Where people start:

- Website, Meta and WhatsApp leads start at **New**.
- Someone added by hand in the CRM starts at **Contacted**, or further on if their first job starts further on (a project added at Won, for example).
- Imported Meta leads start at **New**. Imported WhatsApp chats with replies from you in them start at **Contacted**.

## Change it by hand

Pick a status from the drop-down in their row. It saves straight away.

> **Watch out:** Changing the status here doesn’t move their job in the CRM. The next time the job moves stage, the status is set from the job again. To move a customer on properly, change the stage on their job.

Use the drop-down for people whose status is plainly wrong, for example someone you’ve spoken to who has no job in the CRM.

## Where the statuses show

- The status cards at the top of Enquiries count them. Click a card to see only those leads: see [Find the leads you need](/admin/help/enquiries/find-leads).
- The dashboard’s New Meta leads card lists Meta leads still at **New**. A lead drops off it as soon as it moves on.
