## Filter by status

Click a status card (**New**, **Contacted**, **Quoted**, **Won** or **Lost**) to see only the leads at that status. Click the same card again to see everyone. One card works at a time.

## Filter by where they came from

Under the cards, **From** has a button for each kind of lead, each with a count:

- **All**: everyone.
- **Meta**: Facebook and Instagram lead forms, website visits from a Meta ad link, and WhatsApp chats started from a Meta ad.
- **Website**: the website’s forms, survey bookings and Google ad clicks.
- **WhatsApp**: people who messaged the business number, and imported WhatsApp chats.
- **Other**: people added in the CRM and anything else. This button only shows when there’s someone in it.

Click one to see only those leads; click it again, or **All**, to see everyone. For what each source means, see [Lead sources and ad names](/admin/help/enquiries/lead-sources).

## Use both together

The two work together. The status cards count only the source you’ve picked, and the From buttons count only the status you’ve picked. So **Meta** and then **New** shows the new Meta leads, and the **New** card tells you how many there are.

> **Good to know:** **See all new Meta leads →** on the dashboard’s New Meta leads card opens Enquiries with exactly this filter.

## Search

Type in the search box to narrow the list. It finds:

- names, company names and email addresses,
- postcodes, with or without the space,
- phone numbers (at least 3 digits; 07700 900123 also finds +44 7700 900123),
- customer references: HX-0042, hx42 and 0042 all find HX-0042, and a job’s reference such as HX-0042-2 finds its customer.

Each word can match a different thing, in any order, so "rob evans" finds Rob Evans. The search works inside any filter you’ve picked.

## Clear the filters

- **Show all** (beside the count at the top) clears the status, the source and the search.
- When nothing matches, the list says so ("No New Meta leads at the moment.") with **Show all leads**, **Clear search** or **Clear filters**.

> **Good to know:** If you change someone’s status while a card is picked, they stay on screen so you can see what you did. They drop off when you pick another card.

The filters stay if you refresh the page. What you type in the search doesn’t.
