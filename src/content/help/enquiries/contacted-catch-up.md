When WhatsApp Business app chats are imported, anyone you’d already messaged moves to **Contacted** by itself. Chats imported before the app did this may have left some of those customers at **Lead**. The catch-up fixes them in one go.

> **Admins only:** Only admins see the catch-up, and only when there’s someone to move.

## Where it shows

A banner on the CRM home (**CRM › Opportunities**) and on **Enquiries › Import**, for example:

"**41 customers** you’ve already messaged on WhatsApp are still at Lead."

If there’s nobody to move, there’s no banner.

## Move them

1. Press **Move them all to Contacted** (or **Move them to Contacted** when it’s one person).
2. It counts up as it goes: "Moving them to Contacted… 15 of 41".
3. When it’s finished it says "Done: **41 customers** moved to Contacted. Each job’s timeline says when you first messaged them." Press **OK**.

Press **Not now** to hide the banner until you next open the page.

## Who it moves

- Jobs still at **Lead** that nobody has contacted, whose chat has a message from you sent after the job was made.
- Customers at **New** with no job, whose chat has a message from you sent after they came in.

A message from you means one typed by a person: imported from the phone, typed in the WhatsApp Business app, or sent by someone on the team. An old conversation never marks a newer enquiry as contacted, and nobody is moved back or past **Contacted**.

Each moved job gets a line on its timeline with the date you first messaged them, and counts that date as their first contact.

> **Good to know:** Nothing is sent to the customer or to Meta. If some couldn’t be moved, it says how many: try again later. If it stops partway with an error, press **Try again**.

To import WhatsApp chats in the first place, see [Import WhatsApp Business app chats](/admin/help/whatsapp/import-chats).
