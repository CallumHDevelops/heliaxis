CRM reports counts leads and sales. Visitor figures for the website are elsewhere, under **CMS**, and need Analytics access:

- **CMS › Website analytics**: visitors, page views and where visitors came from. See [Website analytics](/admin/help/website/website-analytics).
- **CMS › Heatmap**: where people click on each page. The menu item shows with Analytics access, but only admins can load the clicks. See [The click heatmap](/admin/help/website/heatmap).

To see which visitors became leads, and from which ad, use **By source and ad campaign** on CRM reports: see [Sources in the report](/admin/help/reports/sources).
