**CRM reports** shows how the opportunities made in a period have done: where they came from, how far they got, who’s working them and what was won. You need CRM access to open it.

To open it:

- on a computer, **CRM › CRM reports**, or the **Reports** button at the top of the CRM page (also on **CRM settings**),
- on a phone, **More** › **CRM reports**.

<!-- shot: reports/reports-page.jpg | The period buttons and the figures at the top | The top of CRM reports -->

## Pick a period

The buttons at the top right are **30 days**, **90 days** (the one it opens on) and **1 year**. The line under **Reports** says which is showing: "Opportunities created in the last 90 days."

Everything on the page counts opportunities made in that period, and where each has got to now. See [What each figure means](/admin/help/reports/figures-explained).

## The figures at the top

| Figure | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Leads** | Opportunities made in the period |
| **Won (3)** | The value of those that were won, and how many (in brackets) |
| **Win rate (won ÷ won + lost)** | Of the ones that have been decided, the share that were won |
| **Typical first reply · 40% within 15 min** | How long a new lead usually waits for someone to get in touch, and the share contacted within 15 minutes |
| **Ad spend on Meta** | What Meta charged for your ads in the period. Only once ad spend is connected |
| **Cost per lead · Meta leads only (12)** | Meta spend ÷ leads from Meta (how many in brackets). Only once ad spend is connected |

## Below the figures

- **By source and ad campaign**: leads, surveys, sales and £ won for each source. Once ad spend is connected, each Meta campaign and ad with its spend and cost per lead, survey and sale. See [Sources in the report](/admin/help/reports/sources) and [Meta ad spend and cost per lead](/admin/help/reports/meta-ad-spend).
- **Pipeline funnel**: how many of the period’s opportunities have reached each stage.
- **By person**: the same figures for each owner.
- **Export**: admins only. See [Export customers and jobs](/admin/help/reports/export).

> **Good to know:** If the page says "Could not load the report", refresh it. Once ad spend is connected, a period can take a little longer to load when the app has to ask Meta for fresh figures.
