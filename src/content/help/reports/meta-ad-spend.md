Once an admin has connected your Meta ad account, CRM reports puts what Meta charged beside the leads, surveys and sales each campaign and ad brought in. You can see which ads bring customers, not just form-fillers.

![The Meta part of By source and ad campaign](/help/reports/ad-spend-table.jpg "Campaigns with their ads beneath, spend and cost per lead, survey and sale")

## At the top of the page

- **Ad spend on Meta**: everything Meta charged in the period.
- **Cost per lead · Meta leads only (12)**: that spend ÷ the leads from Meta in the CRM (how many in brackets).

## The table

**By source and ad campaign** then reads "Meta campaigns with their ads beneath, then your other sources. Sorted by £ won. Return is £ won for each £1 spent."

The rows, top to bottom:

1. **All Meta ads**: every Meta lead and all the account’s spend.
2. Each campaign, with its ads beneath it. Campaigns and ads are sorted by £ won, then leads, then spend.
3. **Other Meta leads and spend**: Meta leads that couldn’t be put with one ad, and spend Meta gives no ad for. Each row says why underneath.
4. **Other sources**: everything that isn’t from a Meta ad, one row per source. See [Sources in the report](/admin/help/reports/sources).

Every ad’s spend is in exactly one row, so the rows add up to **All Meta ads**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Leads**, **Surveys**, **Won**, **Win rate**, **£ won** | As in the rest of the report: see [What each figure means](/admin/help/reports/figures-explained) |
| **Spend** | What Meta charged for that ad or campaign in the period |
| **Cost per lead** | Spend ÷ leads in the CRM |
| **Cost per survey** | Spend ÷ surveys |
| **Cost per sale** | Spend ÷ sales won |
| **Return** | £ won for each £1 spent, such as "£4.20 per £1" |

A "—" means there’s nothing to divide by yet (no spend, or no surveys or sales). Amounts under £100 show pence; bigger ones are rounded to the pound.

> **Good to know:** Point at a **Leads** number on a computer to see how many leads Meta itself counted for that row.

## The small notes under a name

| Note | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "was Video 4" | The ad or campaign has been renamed. Its older leads are in this row. See [Renamed ads](/admin/help/reports/renamed-ads) |
| "2 ads with this name" (or "2 ads have been called …") | More than one ad has had the name these leads came in with, so the leads and those ads share one row |
| "Leads that don’t say which ad: …" | Leads with a name several ads have had, kept in a row of their own with no spend, so no ad gets them by guesswork |
| "Not in Meta’s figures for this period: renamed or deleted?" | Leads whose ad name doesn’t match any ad Meta reported for the period |
| **Website enquiries**: "Ad links that didn’t say which ad" | Website leads from a campaign’s link that didn’t name the ad. Counted in the campaign’s total |
| **Ads Meta no longer lists**: "Deleted or archived: Meta gives only their total" | Spend on ads that have since been deleted or archived |
| **WhatsApp ad chats**: "Which ad wasn’t recorded" | Chats started from an ad, where which ad wasn’t recorded |

Other notes say why a lead couldn’t be put with an ad, such as "Meta didn’t say which ad" or "The ad link didn’t say which campaign".

## How fresh the figures are

Above the table, a line says when the figures came from Meta, for example "Updated from Meta: today’s figures and ad names at 10:58, earlier days at 10:12." The app keeps Meta’s figures for a while rather than asking Meta every time: today’s for 15 minutes, earlier days for an hour, and the ad names for 10 minutes.

Press **Refresh from Meta** to ask for today’s figures and the ad names straight away. If Meta was asked less than a minute ago, or is busy, it says so and shows the latest figures it has.

Under the table, a note gives the exact dates Meta’s spend covers, and compares Meta’s own lead count with the CRM’s: "Meta counts leads its own way and on its own dates, so a small gap is normal. A big one can mean leads aren’t reaching the CRM." If you see a big gap, see [Meta lead ads coming in](/admin/help/enquiries/meta-lead-ads) and [Why the report’s numbers differ from Enquiries and Meta](/admin/help/reports/numbers-dont-match).

> **Watch out:** Recent leads may still become sales, so cost per sale comes down as they do. Judge a new ad on cost per lead and per survey first.

No spend showing, or a warning about Meta’s figures? See [When ad spend isn’t showing in reports](/admin/help/reports/set-up-ad-spend).
