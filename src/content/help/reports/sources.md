**By source and ad campaign** has a row for each source, with its leads, surveys, sales and £ won. The rows use the short names the CRM keeps for each source, which are different from the plain words on Enquiries.

## What the names mean

| In the report | What it is | On Enquiries it shows as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **quote-form** | The website’s quote form | Website |
| **cms-form** | A contact form on one of the website’s pages | Website |
| **website-booking** | A survey booked on the website | Website booking |
| **website** | Older website enquiries | Website |
| **google-ads: Brand** | A Google ad click, then a website form. After the colon is the campaign | Google ad · Brand |
| **meta: Video 4** | A Facebook or Instagram lead form on the ad called Video 4 when the lead came in | Meta ad · Video 4 |
| **meta-lead-ad** | A Meta lead form where Meta didn’t say which ad | Meta ad |
| **meta-organic** | A Meta lead form that wasn’t on an ad | Meta (organic) |
| **meta-ad: Solar Oct / Video 4** | A Meta ad click to the website, then a form. After the colon are the campaign and ad from the ad’s link | Meta ad (website) |
| **meta-ad** | The same, when the link didn’t say which campaign | Meta ad (website) |
| **whatsapp** | Messaged the business number | WhatsApp |
| **whatsapp-ad** | A WhatsApp chat started from a Meta ad | Meta ad (WhatsApp) |
| **whatsapp-import** | A chat brought in with Import | WhatsApp (imported) |
| **crm** | Added by someone on the team in the CRM | Added by staff |
| **unknown** | A job whose customer has no source | |

Anything else, such as **newsletter: October**, is a website visit from a tagged link.

> **Good to know:** Website visitors are only labelled with the ad or link that brought them if they accepted cookies on the website. Otherwise their enquiry is **quote-form**, **cms-form** or **website-booking**. See [Lead sources and ad names](/admin/help/enquiries/lead-sources).

## Before ad spend is connected

The table shows one row per source, sorted by £ won. The line above it says "“meta-ad: …” rows are your Meta campaigns and ads (from the ad link tracking)."

> **Watch out:** In this table, **meta: …** rows carry the ad’s name from when each lead came in. If an ad was renamed, its leads can be split over two rows: one under the old name, one under the new. Connect ad spend to see them together under the ad’s name now. See [Show ad spend in reports](/admin/help/admin/ad-spend).

## Once ad spend is connected

The Meta rows (all but **meta-organic**) move into the Meta part of the table, grouped by campaign with each ad’s spend. Everything else, **meta-organic** included, is listed below under **Other sources**. See [Meta ad spend and cost per lead in the report](/admin/help/reports/meta-ad-spend).
