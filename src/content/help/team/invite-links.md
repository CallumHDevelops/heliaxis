Each subcontractor firm has its own portal, where they keep their details, agreement, team and documents. They get in with a personal link, or by signing in with their email and a one-time code.

The buttons for this are along the top of the firm’s page (**Team › Subcontractors**, then click the firm). They’re hidden once a firm is terminated.

## Email them their link

- **Email invite** shows if they’ve never been sent one.
- **Email a reminder** shows once they have. The email’s subject is "Reminder: complete your Heliaxis subcontractor onboarding" with their reference.

Either way, it says "Link emailed to …" when it’s gone. The link inside works for 14 days.

## Send the link yourself

Use this if the email isn’t getting through, or you’d rather WhatsApp it.

1. Press **New link to copy**.
2. Confirm "Generate a new link? Any link already sent will stop working."
3. The page shows **New portal link (the previous one no longer works)**. Press **Copy** and send it to them.

> **Watch out:** Every new link cancels the one before: emailed, copied, sent with **Send everyone a fresh link**, or the one in the email they get when you countersign their agreement. If they say a link doesn’t work, it’s usually an older one: send a fresh one, or ask them to sign in with a code (below).

## Signing in without a link

After the 14 days, or any time, they can go to the subcontractor portal (subcontractor.heliaxis.co.uk), type the email address you added them with, press **Email me a sign-in code**, and type the 6-digit code from the email. The code lasts 10 minutes. It only goes to the email address on their record, so if they type a different one, nothing arrives. An old link takes them to the same page, with "That link has expired or been replaced. Sign in with your email below instead."

A terminated firm can’t sign in at all. A suspended one can, but sees "Your account is suspended. Please contact Heliaxis." at the top.

## Sign them out of every device

If a phone or laptop with their portal on it is lost, press **Sign out everywhere** and confirm. Every device they were signed in on is signed out. They can sign back in with an email code.

Everything you do here is listed in the **Activity** card on their page, for example "Invite emailed", "Reminder emailed", "New portal link generated" and "Signed out everywhere".
