**Site contacts** on a firm’s page lists the people who go to site for them. When the firm is put on a job, its job packs and labour POs go to these people’s mobiles and emails. It’s the same list the office picks from when putting trades on a job, so the details are kept in one place.

## Add someone

- **Add their main contact** adds the firm’s contact from their own details. You won’t usually need it: the main contact is added by itself the first time the firm is put on a job.
- To add one of their team, pick them from **Add someone from their team…** and press **Add**. Their name, mobile and email come from what the firm entered.

Until someone is added, it says "Nobody yet. Their main contact is added the first time they’re put on a job."

## Change someone’s details

1. Press **Edit** beside the person.
2. Change their **Mobile** or **Email**. If the mobile doesn’t look right, it says what’s wrong and **Save** stays greyed out.
3. Press **Save**.

## WhatsApp job messages

Tick **Happy to get job messages on WhatsApp** for someone once they’ve said yes. Their job packs, POs and review messages then go on WhatsApp as well as email. It shows when it was recorded. Untick it if they change their mind.

What can stop a WhatsApp going is in [Job messages to trades on WhatsApp](/admin/help/site/whatsapp-job-messages). To put the firm on a job, see [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade).

> **Once switched on:** **Site contacts** only appears once site-commissioning.sql has been run.
