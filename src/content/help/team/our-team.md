Heliaxis’s own operatives (the people we employ, as opposed to subcontractors) are kept in RAMS, not in Heliaxis. RAMS holds the one list of everyone who goes to site, because crews and RAMS documents name them. **Our team** is a shortcut that opens RAMS on that list, filtered to Heliaxis’s own team.

## Open it

- On a computer: **Team › Our team**. If it’s the only thing you can open in the **Team** menu, the top menu shows a plain **Our team** link instead.
- On a phone: tap **More**, then **Our team**.
- On the dashboard, under **Apps**, the **Our team** card: "Heliaxis’s own operatives — cards, qualifications and expiry dates (kept in RAMS)."

It opens in RAMS. Adding someone, or updating their cards and qualifications, is done there.

## Signing in

Once one-tap sign-in is set up, it opens already signed in. The first time, you confirm your email with a 6-digit code: see [Open RAMS and the social media studio](/admin/help/getting-started/open-rams-and-social). It only works if you have an approved RAMS account under the same email as your Heliaxis login. Otherwise you see the RAMS sign-in page.

## Who sees it

You need the **Our team** tick on your account (or to be an admin). The **RAMS** tick is separate: either one lets RAMS open signed in. See [Who sees what](/admin/help/team/who-sees-what).

For subcontractor firms’ own people, see [Their team, jobs and RAMS](/admin/help/team/team-jobs-and-rams).
