An NTP agreement appoints someone at a subcontractor firm as Heliaxis’s Nominated Technical Person for one or more technologies. Each agreement lasts 12 months. It sits on top of the firm’s Framework Agreement, so it can only be sent once that has been countersigned (see [Countersign the Framework Agreement](/admin/help/team/countersign-the-agreement)).

## Send one

1. On the firm’s page, under **NTP agreements**, press **+ Send NTP agreement**. Or, in the **Team** card, press **Make NTP…** beside the person.
2. Under **Technologies**, tick one or more: **Solar PV** (MIS 3002), **Battery energy storage systems (BESS)** (MIS 3012), **Air source heat pumps (ASHP)** (MIS 3005-D and MIS 3005-I).
3. Choose the **NTP (from their team)**, or **Someone else — type below** and fill in **NTP full name**.
4. Fill in **NTP’s email**. They sign from a personal link sent there. Leave it blank and they sign in the firm’s portal instead.
5. Fill in **Minimum days per month**, and **Fee (optional)** (blank means the Framework rates).
6. Describe the supervision: **Geographical spread**, **Installations per month**, **Typical duration**, **Installers to supervise**, and **Other supervision requirements**.
7. Press **Send for signature**.

## Follow it through

Each agreement is listed with the NTP’s name, the technologies, its reference (like HLX-NTP-2026-001), its dates and its status:

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Awaiting signature** | Sent, not signed yet. | **Resend link** (emails a new link; the old one stops working) or **Cancel**. |
| **Awaiting countersign** | The NTP has signed. | **Countersign**: check **Name** and **Title**, draw your signature, press **Countersign NTP agreement**. |
| **Active** | In force. | **Cancel**, which ends it at once. Tell your Certification Body. |
| **Expired** | Lapsed without a renewal. | |
| **Renewed** | Replaced by its renewal. | |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled. | |

**View / print ↗** opens the agreement to read or print.

When the NTP signs, the office is emailed straight away ("Signed: NTP agreement … needs countersigning"), and it’s in the office’s daily summary email 2 and 7 days later if it’s still waiting. Unsigned NTP agreements are chased on day 3, 7 and 14. See [Automatic reminders to subcontractors](/admin/help/team/automatic-reminders).

Once you countersign, it runs for 12 months from that day (a renewal starts the day after the one it replaces ends). The firm, and the NTP if they have their own email, are emailed that it’s in force.

## Renewals

You don’t need to remember. 30 days before an agreement ends, the app sends a renewal to sign by itself: to the NTP, or to the firm if the NTP has left their team. This only happens while the firm is **Active**. If an agreement ends with no signed renewal, it’s marked **Expired** and the office is emailed: "Notify your Certification Body if no replacement is in place."

> **Once switched on:** NTP agreements need ntp.sql, an older file that isn’t on the From Claude card.
