Each subcontractor firm has its own portal at subcontractor.heliaxis.co.uk. It’s separate from Heliaxis: the firm never sees your pages, and you manage them from **Team › Subcontractors**. This article is what they see, so you can talk them through it on the phone.

## Getting in

- **With their link**: the button in their invite or reminder email takes them straight in. Each link works for 14 days, and a newer one replaces it.
- **With a code**: on the **Subcontractor portal** page they type the email address you added them with, press **Email me a sign-in code**, then type the 6-digit code and press **Sign in**. The code lasts 10 minutes. **Send a new code** sends another.

The sign-in page also gives them the office phone number and hello@heliaxis.co.uk if they’re stuck. To send a link yourself, see [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links).

## The top of their portal

Their reference and company name, a **Sign out** button, and one line saying where they are:

| They see | When |
| --- | --- |
| "Confirm your details, sign the agreement, add your team and upload your documents." | They haven’t signed yet. |
| "You signed on … Heliaxis will countersign once your documents have been checked." | Waiting for you to [countersign](/admin/help/team/countersign-the-agreement). |
| "Agreement live since …", then what still needs doing, or "Your documents are up to date." | Countersigned. |
| "Your account is suspended. Please contact Heliaxis." | You’ve suspended them. |

## The steps

Along the top are the steps, each with a ✓ once done. The portal opens on whatever needs them next.

1. **Your details**: their business, tax, CIS, bank and contact details (Schedule B of the agreement). They press **Save and continue**. Once they’ve signed the agreement, these are locked.
2. **Sign agreement**: they read the Subcontractor Framework Agreement (with your rates in it), tick that they’ve read it and can sign for the business, draw a signature and press **Sign agreement**. They can’t sign until their details are complete.
3. **Your team**: everyone they might send to a Heliaxis site. They add each person with **Add to team**, and can remove or restore people.
4. **Documents**: each document type, marked **Required** or **If applicable**, with **+ Add** to upload. Anything you’ve asked for is at the top under "Heliaxis has asked for …", and the step says how many, for example **Documents (1 requested)**. When they’re done they press **Send to Heliaxis for review** (later, **Notify Heliaxis of new uploads**), which emails the office.
5. **Jobs**: jobs booked in RAMS. For each they pick who’s going and press **Confirm crew** (later **Change crew**), or **Can’t do this job** with a reason.
6. **NTP**: only if they have an NTP agreement. It’s where they sign one that hasn’t got its own email link.

They can’t change their rates or the agreement, only their own details before signing, their team and their documents. Anything they upload waits for you: see [Review a subcontractor’s documents](/admin/help/team/review-documents).
