There’s no delete button for a subcontractor: their agreement and documents are records you keep. Instead you suspend them or terminate them, using the buttons at the top of their page.

## Suspend

**Suspend** shows on an **Active** firm. Press it and their status becomes **Suspended**.

While suspended:

- RAMS no longer sees the firm or its team: RAMS only sees **Active** firms.
- They can still sign in to their portal, where the top says "Your account is suspended. Please contact Heliaxis."
- They still get the daily reminder emails. Use **Pause reminders** if you don’t want that.
- They can’t be put on a new job. When putting a trade on a job, they and their people still show in the list, marked "Suspended: can’t be given new jobs", but they can’t be picked. Jobs they’re already on carry on as they are.

## Reinstate

**Reinstate** shows on a suspended firm. Press it to bring them back: they go back to **Active** if their agreement was countersigned, or to **In progress** if not.

## Terminate

Use this when you’ve stopped working with a firm for good.

1. Press **Terminate**.
2. Confirm "Terminate this subcontractor? Their portal link stops working. Records are kept."

Their status becomes **Terminated**, and:

- their portal link stops working and they’re signed out of every device, so they can’t get in at all,
- they can’t be put on a new job: they and their people no longer show when putting a trade on a job,
- they’re never sent reminders, and
- everything on their page stays, for your records.

> **Watch out:** There’s no button to undo **Terminate**. The buttons along the top of a terminated firm’s page all disappear. If you might work with them again, **Suspend** them instead.

To find suspended and terminated firms, choose **Suspended / terminated** in the drop-down on the Subcontractors page.
