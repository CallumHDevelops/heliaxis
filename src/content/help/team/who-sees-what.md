The **Team** menu shows only what your account can open. An admin ticks what each person can open on the **Users** page (see [What each access tick allows](/admin/help/admin/access-ticks)).

| What | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Team › Tasks** | People with the **Tasks** tick. See the Tasks section. |
| **Team › Subcontractors**, every firm’s page and documents | People with the **Subcontractors** tick. Everything on these pages (adding firms, countersigning, approving documents, CIS, NTP, suspending and terminating) is open to anyone with the tick, not just admins. |
| **Team › Our team** | People with the **Our team** tick. It opens RAMS, which has its own accounts: see [Our team](/admin/help/team/our-team). |
| A firm’s **Site contacts** | People with **Subcontractors**, on the firm’s page. People with **CRM** pick from the same contacts when putting trades on a job. |
| The **Subcontractors** count under **Needs you** on the dashboard | People with the **Subcontractors** tick. |
| The office’s emails about subcontractors, including the daily summary | One office address, not everyone with the tick. See [Emails the office gets about subcontractors](/admin/help/team/office-emails). |

Admins can open all of these.

> **Watch out:** Subcontractor pages hold ID documents, bank details and UTR numbers. Only give the **Subcontractors** tick to people who need it.

If you follow a link to something you can’t open, the app takes you to the dashboard with "You don’t have access to that area. Ask an admin to add it to your account."
