Click a firm on **Team › Subcontractors** to open its page. **← All subcontractors** takes you back.

## The top

Their reference, company name, contact, email, phone and trade, with their status on the right. Underneath are the buttons:

| Button | What it does | Read more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email invite** / **Email a reminder** | Emails them their portal link. | [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links) |
| **New link to copy** | Makes a new link for you to send. | [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links) |
| **Sign out everywhere** | Signs them out of the portal on every device. | [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links) |
| **Pause reminders** / **Resume reminders** | Stops or restarts the daily reminder emails. | [Automatic reminders to subcontractors](/admin/help/team/automatic-reminders) |
| **Suspend**, **Reinstate**, **Terminate** | Changes their status. | [Suspend or terminate a subcontractor](/admin/help/team/suspend-or-terminate) |

## The main column

- **Framework Agreement**: who signed and when, and Heliaxis’s countersignature. **View / print ↗** opens the full agreement. See [Countersign the Framework Agreement](/admin/help/team/countersign-the-agreement).
- **Documents**: what’s missing, expired or expiring, then every document by type. See [Review a subcontractor’s documents](/admin/help/team/review-documents) and [Ask a subcontractor for a document](/admin/help/team/request-a-document).
- **NTP agreements**: see [NTP agreements](/admin/help/team/ntp-agreements).
- **Pulled by RAMS**: every document RAMS has taken for a job. See [Their team, jobs and RAMS](/admin/help/team/team-jobs-and-rams).

## The side column

- **Reminders**: what they’d be chased for today. Only shown when something is outstanding, or reminders are paused.
- **CIS verification**: see [Record CIS verification](/admin/help/team/cis-verification).
- **Team** and **Jobs**: the people they’ve added and the RAMS jobs they’re booked on.
- **Site contacts**: who goes to site for them. See [Site contacts](/admin/help/team/site-contacts).
- **Terms**: their rates, and when they were invited and last seen. See [Change a subcontractor’s details or rates](/admin/help/team/change-details-or-rates).
- **Details (Schedule B)**: what they filled in: business name and type, company number, VAT, addresses, UTR, CIS status, notices and accounts emails, contact, bank details, whether they employ staff, and their operatives. It says "Not complete yet." until they’ve finished. Only they can change these, in their portal, and only until they sign the agreement.
- **Notes**: internal notes only Heliaxis sees. Type and press **Save notes**.
- **Activity**: a dated log of the latest 100 things that have happened, and who did it: invites, sign-ins, uploads, reviews, status changes and so on.

> **Watch out:** This page shows bank details, UTR and ID documents. Only people with the **Subcontractors** tick can open it.
