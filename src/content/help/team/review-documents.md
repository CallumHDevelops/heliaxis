Firms upload their documents in their portal. Each one arrives as **To review** until someone at Heliaxis approves it. Firms with documents to review count under **Need attention** on the Subcontractors page, and on the dashboard under **Needs you**. When a firm presses **Send to Heliaxis for review** in their portal, the office is emailed "Documents ready to review: …"; a document you asked for gets its own email when it arrives (see [Emails the office gets about subcontractors](/admin/help/team/office-emails)).

## What’s needed

The **Documents** card starts with a summary: "All required documents are in date and reviewed.", or a list of what’s **Missing**, **Expired** or **Expires** soon, and how many are awaiting your review. Then the documents are listed by type:

| Type | Required? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Photo ID** | Yes | One for each person who’ll go to site. |
| **Trade qualifications** | Yes | Per person, for example 18th Edition or NVQ. |
| **Public liability insurance** | Yes | At least £2,000,000. Needs an expiry date. |
| **Tools & equipment insurance** | Yes | Needs an expiry date. |
| **Employers’ liability insurance** | If they employ staff | At least £5,000,000. Needs an expiry date. |
| **Scheme registrations** | No | NICEIC, NAPIT, MCS and so on. Needs an expiry date. |
| **Cards** | No | Per person: ECS / JIB, CSCS, IPAF, PASMA and so on. Needs an expiry date. |
| **Professional indemnity insurance** | No | Only where it applies, such as design work. |
| **RAMS** | No | Their own risk assessments and method statements. |
| **Other documents** | No | Anything else. |

A document counts as **expiring** from 30 days before its expiry date.

## Approve or reject

1. Click the document’s name to open it on the page.
2. If it’s right, press **Approve** (in the viewer, or beside the document).
3. If not, press **Reject**, type the **Reason for rejecting … (they see this)**, and press **Reject**.

When rejecting, **Email them now and ask for a replacement (chased on day 3, 7 and 14 until they upload one)** starts ticked. Leave it ticked to ask for a new one straight away. If they’ve already sent a newer copy, have already been asked for it, or the person has left their team, the box isn’t offered and the form says why.

Once approved, only the tidy PDF the app made from their upload is kept. Until then, the original upload is kept as well.

## Other things you can do

The **▾** beside each document has:

- **Approve** or **Reject…**, to change your mind, and **Reset to “to review”**.
- **Ask for a replacement…** on a rejected document, if you didn’t ask when rejecting.
- **Rename / move…**: change its **Name**, **Category**, **Team member**, **Expiry date** or **Cover amount**, then **Save**. Use this when they’ve uploaded something under the wrong type or person, or the expiry is wrong.
- **Download**, and **Original upload** (what they actually sent, until it’s approved).
- **Delete…**: removes it for good, after you confirm.

> **Good to know:** Every new upload is turned into one compact PDF by itself: photos are converted and scans are made smaller. Approved documents are what RAMS uses for the firm and its team.
