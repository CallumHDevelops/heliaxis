Adding a subcontractor creates their record and their Framework Agreement, and emails them a link to their own portal. There they fill in their details, sign the agreement and upload their documents. You need the **Subcontractors** tick on your account.

## Add them

1. Go to **Team › Subcontractors** and press **+ Add subcontractor**.
2. Fill in **Company / trading name**, **Contact name** and **Email** (all needed), and **Phone** if you have it.
3. Type their **Trade**, or pick one of the suggestions: Electrician, Roofer, Plumber, Solar PV installer, BESS installer, Labourer or Scaffolder.
4. Under **Rates (Clause 4.1)**, choose:
   - **4.1.1 Standard default rates**: Electrician £27.50 per hour, Plumber £37.50 per hour, Roofer £30.00 per panel. Other trades are to be agreed in the work order.
   - **4.1.2 Agreed bespoke rates**: this strikes out 4.1.1 for them. Fill in a row for each rate: **Trade / description**, **Rate e.g. £250** and **Basis e.g. per day**. Press **+ Add rate** for another, or **×** to remove one.
5. Leave **Email them their onboarding link now** ticked to send the invite straight away.
6. Press **Create**.

![The New subcontractor form with bespoke rates chosen](/help/team/add-subcontractor.jpg "New subcontractor: who they are, their rates under Clause 4.1, and the invite tick")

## What happens next

The page says "Added." and shows their portal link. It’s only shown this once, so press **Copy link** if you want to send it yourself too. **Open record →** opens their page.

They get an email with the subject "Heliaxis subcontractor agreement — " and their company name, headed "Welcome to the Heliaxis subcontractor network", with a **Start onboarding** button. In their portal they:

1. Confirm their company, CIS and payment details (**Your details**).
2. Read and sign the Framework Agreement (**Sign agreement**).
3. Add the people who’ll go to site (**Your team**).
4. Upload photo ID, qualifications, cards and insurance (**Documents**).

The link works for 14 days. After that they sign in to the portal with their email and a one-time code. Their record moves from **Invited** to **In progress** when they save their details, and to **Awaiting countersign** once they’ve signed. Then it’s your turn: [Countersign the Framework Agreement](/admin/help/team/countersign-the-agreement). What they see is in [The subcontractor portal](/admin/help/team/the-subcontractor-portal).

If you unticked **Email them their onboarding link now**, nothing is emailed. Send the link yourself, or press **Email invite** on their page later. The automatic onboarding reminders only start once they’ve been emailed.

If they don’t finish, the app chases them by itself: see [Automatic reminders to subcontractors](/admin/help/team/automatic-reminders).

## If it won’t save

- "Company and contact name are required." Fill both in.
- "Enter a valid email address." Check the email.
- "Add at least one bespoke rate." With **4.1.2** chosen, at least one row needs both a trade and a rate. Rows missing either are left out.
- "Link created but the email failed: …" The firm has been added, but the invite didn’t go. Don’t press **Create** again, or you’ll add them twice. Press **Cancel**, refresh the page, open them from the list, and use **Email a reminder** or **New link to copy** on their page.

> **Good to know:** Their reference is made for you, like HLX-SC-2026-001. To put a firm on a particular job, see [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade).
