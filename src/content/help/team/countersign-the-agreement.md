Once a subcontractor has signed the Subcontractor Framework Agreement in their portal, their status is **Awaiting countersign**. The office is emailed straight away ("Signed: … needs countersigning"). They show under **To countersign** on the Subcontractors page, and on the dashboard under **Needs you**, and the office’s daily summary email reminds you 2 and 7 days after they signed. The agreement only takes effect when Heliaxis countersigns it.

## Before you sign

On their page, the **Framework Agreement** card shows:

- **Subcontractor**: who signed, their title, when, and from which internet address.
- **Heliaxis**: "Not countersigned".
- **Version**: the agreement’s version, and **✓ integrity verified** if nothing has changed since they signed.

Press **View / print ↗** to read the full agreement as they signed it. **Print / save as PDF** prints it or saves a copy.

Check their documents too. If anything required is missing or expired, the countersign box says so: "Heads up: missing …. Clause 3A.1 requires these before they start work." You can still countersign, but they shouldn’t start work until it’s sorted.

## Countersign

1. Under **Countersign for Heliaxis Limited**, check **Name** (it starts as yours) and **Title** (it starts as Director).
2. Draw your signature in the box. **Clear** starts it again.
3. Press **Countersign agreement**.

It says "Countersigned — the agreement is live and a copy has been emailed to them." Their status becomes **Active**. They get an email, "Your Heliaxis subcontractor agreement is live", with a **View your agreement** button so they can download their signed copy and keep their documents up to date. That button is a new portal link, so any older link they have stops working.

Once countersigned, **NTP agreements** can be sent to them (see [NTP agreements](/admin/help/team/ntp-agreements)), and RAMS can see the firm, its team and its approved documents (while it’s **Active**).

## If the record has been altered

If the card says **⚠ signed record has been altered**, the countersign box is replaced by "The signed record has been altered since they signed. Don’t countersign — ask them to re-sign." Do as it says, and tell Claude, as it shouldn’t happen.

> **Good to know:** Their **Terms** (rates and contact details) are locked once they’ve signed. See [Change a subcontractor’s details or rates](/admin/help/team/change-details-or-rates).
