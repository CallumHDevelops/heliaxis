A firm adds the people who’ll go to site in their own portal (the **Your team** step). Heliaxis books the firm onto jobs in RAMS, and the firm then chooses which of their people go. These cards on the firm’s page show all of that.

## Team

**Team** lists the people the firm has added, with each person’s role. Beside each is whether they’re ready for site, worked out from their documents:

| Pill | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | They have a photo ID and a trade qualification on file (not rejected, not expired), and none of their documents has expired. |
| **2 expiring** (or however many) | Ready, but some of their documents run out within 30 days. |
| **Needs 2** (or however many) | Their photo ID, trade qualification or both are missing. Hover over it to see which. |
| **1 expired** (or however many) | Nothing’s missing, but some of their documents have run out. |

If someone is (or is being made) the firm’s NTP, it shows as an **NTP:** pill with the technologies. Once their Framework Agreement is countersigned, **Make NTP…** starts an NTP agreement for that person: see [NTP agreements](/admin/help/team/ntp-agreements).

Only the firm can add or remove their people, in their portal. A removed person shows in **Activity** as "Team member removed".

## Jobs

**Jobs** lists the jobs RAMS has booked the firm onto: the project, its reference, the RAMS document and the start date. The pill shows where it’s got to:

- **Choose your crew**: waiting for the firm to pick who’s going. They’re chased by the daily reminder.
- **Crew confirmed**: with the names they chose.
- **Declined**: with their reason.
- **Cancelled**.

If a message to RAMS about the job didn’t go through, it shows under the job as "RAMS: …".

## Pulled by RAMS

Every time RAMS takes one of the firm’s documents for a job, it’s written here: when, which document, which project and RAMS document, who pulled it (or "automatic"), and the document’s fingerprint. The fingerprint proves exactly which file RAMS was given. Nothing can be removed from this record.

> **Good to know:** RAMS only sees **Active** firms (countersigned and not suspended), with their team and approved documents. It never gets their photo ID, bank details or the agreement itself. For Heliaxis’s own operatives, see [Our team](/admin/help/team/our-team).

> **Once switched on:** The team and jobs need portal-v2.sql, an older file that isn’t on the From Claude card.
