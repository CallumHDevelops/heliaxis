The **Terms** card on a firm’s page shows their rates (**Clause 4.1.1 standard default rates**, or their bespoke rates), and when they were invited and last seen.

## Before they’ve signed

You can change everything you entered when adding them, for example a mistyped email.

1. On the firm’s page, press **Edit** on the **Terms** card.
2. Change **Company / trading name**, **Contact name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Trade** or the rates. The rates work the same as when adding them: see [Add a subcontractor](/admin/help/team/add-a-subcontractor).
3. Press **Save terms**.

Their Framework Agreement picks up the changes, because it isn’t fixed until they sign. If you’ve changed their email, send them their link again: see [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links).

## After they’ve signed

Once they’ve signed the Framework Agreement, the card says "Locked — the agreement is signed." and there’s no **Edit** button. The signed agreement can’t change.

Their **Details (Schedule B)** (business, tax, bank and contact details) are locked too. If they try to change them in their portal, they’re told "Your agreement is signed, so these details are locked. Email Heliaxis to change them." There’s no way to change them from Heliaxis’s side either, so:

- Write the change, and who told you, in **Notes** on their page (only Heliaxis sees it).
- Ask Claude if a signed record really needs correcting.
- The mobiles and emails that job packs and POs go to can still be changed, under **Site contacts**: see [Site contacts](/admin/help/team/site-contacts).
- The rate for a particular job is agreed when you put them on it anyway: see [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade).
