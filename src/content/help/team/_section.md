Everything about the people who do the work. Subcontractor firms are added under **Team › Subcontractors**: they sign the Framework Agreement online, keep their ID, cards, qualifications and insurance up to date, and the app chases them for anything missing. Heliaxis’s own operatives are kept in RAMS, under **Team › Our team**. For putting a trade on a particular job, see the Trades on site section.
