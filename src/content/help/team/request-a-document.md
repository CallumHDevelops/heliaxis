Use this when you need something specific from a firm: a renewed insurance certificate, an IPAF card for one of their team, or anything else. The request shows at the top of the **Documents** step in their portal, and the app chases it until they upload it.

## Ask for it

1. Open the firm (**Team › Subcontractors**, then click it) and, in **Documents**, press **Request a document**.
2. Pick the **Document** type.
3. For a per-person type (photo ID, qualifications, cards), choose who it’s **For**, or leave **Anyone on their team**.
4. Optionally fill in **What exactly (optional)**, for example "IPAF card" or "18th Edition", and **Needed by (optional)**.
5. Add a **Note to them (optional)** if it helps. They see it.
6. Leave **Email them now (they’re reminded on day 3, 7 and 14 until they upload it)** ticked to email them straight away.
7. Press **Send request** (or **Save request** if you unticked the email).

## Keeping track

Open requests are listed under **Requested from them**, with when you asked, whether it was emailed, and the date it’s needed by. The pill says **Requested**, or **Overdue** once that date has passed.

The **▾** beside a request has:

- **Resend email** (or **Email it now** if it was never emailed).
- **Cancel request…**: they won’t be chased for it any more.

When they upload it in their portal, the request is marked as done and the document waits for you to review it. See [Review a subcontractor’s documents](/admin/help/team/review-documents).

> **Good to know:** To ask for a new copy of something you’ve rejected, use **Reject** with the replacement box ticked, or **Ask for a replacement…** on the rejected document. It works the same way.

> **Once switched on:** Document requests need portal-v3.sql, an older file that isn’t on the From Claude card.
