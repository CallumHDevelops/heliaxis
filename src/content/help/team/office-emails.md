The app emails the office whenever a subcontractor firm does something you need to act on, plus a daily summary on days when something needs you. They go to one address: the office address set in Vercel (the **Website emails** group on [The Vercel keys page](/admin/help/admin/vercel-keys)), or callum@heliaxis.co.uk if none is set (a declined job goes to whoever booked it in RAMS, when RAMS says who). They don’t go to everyone with the **Subcontractors** tick, and they don’t appear in the bell.

## As things happen

| Subject | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Signed: … — subcontractor agreement … needs countersigning" | [Countersign the Framework Agreement](/admin/help/team/countersign-the-agreement). |
| "Documents ready to review: …" | The firm pressed **Send to Heliaxis for review** (or **Notify Heliaxis of new uploads**). [Review their documents](/admin/help/team/review-documents). |
| "… uploaded the … you asked for" | A document you requested has arrived and is waiting for review. See [Ask a subcontractor for a document](/admin/help/team/request-a-document). |
| "Signed: NTP agreement … needs countersigning" | The NTP has signed. Countersign it: see [NTP agreements](/admin/help/team/ntp-agreements). |
| "… declined …" | The firm can’t do a job booked in RAMS, with their reason. It goes to whoever booked it in RAMS, or to the office address if RAMS didn’t say. |
| "RAMS not updated: … crew for …" | The firm chose their crew but RAMS didn’t hear about it. Use **Pull again** in RAMS. |

## Once a day

These come from the daily job, early in the morning:

- **"Subcontractors: … need you"**: a list of what needs Heliaxis, such as agreements waiting to be countersigned, firms stuck in onboarding, expired documents, crews not chosen and overdue document requests. See [Automatic reminders to subcontractors](/admin/help/team/automatic-reminders).
- **"NTP agreement … expires in … days"**: a renewal the NTP has signed is waiting for your countersignature before the old one ends.
- **"NTP agreement expired: …"**: an NTP agreement ended with no renewal in force. Tell your Certification Body if no replacement is in place.

> **Good to know:** The firms get their own emails (invites, sign-in codes, reminders, "your agreement is live"). Those go to the email on their record, not to the office.
