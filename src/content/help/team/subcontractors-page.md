**Team › Subcontractors** lists every subcontractor firm, newest first. From here you add a firm, see at a glance who needs something, and open a firm’s own page. You need the **Subcontractors** tick on your account to see it.

> **Good to know:** If **Subcontractors** is the only thing you can open in the **Team** menu, the top menu shows it as a plain **Subcontractors** link instead. On a phone it’s under **More**.

## At the top

- **Automatic reminders last ran …**: when the daily reminder emails last went, and how many. If it says they haven’t run, or need setting up, see [Automatic reminders to subcontractors](/admin/help/team/automatic-reminders).
- **+ Add subcontractor**: see [Add a subcontractor](/admin/help/team/add-a-subcontractor).
- **Send everyone a fresh link**: see below.

## The counts

Four boxes count the firms. Press one to show just those firms, and press it again to show everyone.

| Box | Which firms |
| --- | --- |
| **Onboarding** | **Invited**, or **In progress** (they’ve saved their details but not signed yet). |
| **To countersign** | They’ve signed the Framework Agreement and are waiting for Heliaxis to countersign. |
| **Active** | Countersigned and working. |
| **Need attention** | Anything to do: waiting for countersigning, documents to review, expired or expiring documents, missing documents on an active firm, or CIS not verified on an active firm or one waiting to be countersigned. |

The drop-down beside the search box does the same, and also has **Suspended / terminated**. Search by name, email, reference or trade in **Search name, email, ref…**.

## The list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Subcontractor** | The firm, with its contact and reference (like HLX-SC-2026-001). Click to open its page. |
| **Trade** | The trade you gave when adding them. |
| **Status** | **Invited**, **In progress**, **Awaiting countersign**, **Active**, **Suspended** or **Terminated**. Once CIS is verified it adds **CIS ✓** with the rate (gross, 20% or 30%). An **Active** or **Awaiting countersign** firm that isn’t verified shows **CIS not verified**. |
| **Documents** | How many are on file, and pills for any **to review**, **expired**, **expiring** (within 30 days) or **missing** (not shown while a firm is still **Invited**). Hover over **missing** to see what. |
| **Last seen** | When they last opened their portal. |

The dashboard’s **Needs you** strip also shows **Subcontractors** when firms are waiting to be countersigned or have documents to review.

## Send everyone a fresh link

This emails every firm except terminated ones a new personal link straight into their portal, valid for 14 days. Any older links they have stop working. Press it to see the email first.

> **Watch out:** The email is an apology: it says sign-in codes weren’t working and that it’s fixed. Only use it after a sign-in problem. For one firm, use **Email a reminder** on their page instead: see [Invite links, reminders and signing in](/admin/help/team/invite-links).

## Convert existing documents to PDF

If this card shows, some documents were uploaded before the app turned every upload into one compact PDF. Press **Convert them now** to do the same for those. **Stop after this batch** pauses it, and **Carry on** starts again. Documents RAMS has already pulled are left exactly as they are.
