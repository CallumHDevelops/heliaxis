Before a subcontractor is paid, your accountants verify them with HMRC, which tells you what rate of CIS to deduct. Record what HMRC said on the firm’s page. Their labour POs then use that rate (see [The money on a labour PO](/admin/help/site/labour-po-money)).

Firms that are **Active** or **Awaiting countersign** and not verified show **CIS not verified** on the Subcontractors page, and count under **Need attention**.

## Record it

1. Open the firm and find the **CIS verification** card. It shows what they told you, for example "They told us: Net payment status", with their UTR and company number.
2. Tick **Verified with HMRC by our accountants**.
3. Choose the **Rate HMRC gave**:
   - **Gross — 0% deduction**
   - **Net — 20% deduction**
   - **Higher rate — 30% deduction (unmatched)**
4. Type the **Verification number**. They look like V1234567890, with letters on the end for the higher rate.
5. Set **Date verified (blank = today)** if it wasn’t today. It can’t be in the future.
6. Press **Save**.

The card now says **Verified**, with the rate, the number, the date and who recorded it. The Subcontractors list shows **CIS ✓** with the rate.

> **Watch out:** If HMRC’s rate doesn’t match what they told you, the card warns you, for example "They told us Gross payment status, but HMRC verified them at net (20%). Deduct at HMRC’s rate." Always go by HMRC.

## Change or remove it

- **Edit details** opens the same form to correct it.
- Unticking the box asks "Remove the CIS verification? It will show as not verified until it is ticked again." Confirm to remove it.

> **Good to know:** When you put a trade on a job, the **CIS** line shows this verification, and **Change for this job** lets you change it for that job only. See [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade).

> **Once switched on:** CIS verification needs portal-v3.sql, an older file that isn’t on the From Claude card.
