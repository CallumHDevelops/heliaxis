A chat belongs to a WhatsApp number. It links to whoever has that mobile in the CRM, however it was typed (07700 900123, +44 7700 900123). There’s nothing to link by hand. The **Customer** card says so while you edit it: "Any WhatsApp chat on the mobile number links to this customer by itself."

## The chat isn’t on their page

1. On their **Customer** card, press **✎ Edit**.
2. Check **Mobile** is the number they WhatsApp from. Correct it or add it.
3. Press **Save**.

The chat then shows on their page, and the card’s **WhatsApp** line says "Linked · 12 messages". See [Change a customer’s details](/admin/help/customers/edit-customer-details).

If they use two numbers, the main **Mobile** is the one their chat follows. After a merge, an extra number shows under **Other numbers**, where **Use for WhatsApp** makes it the main one.

## A chat that says "Not in the CRM"

In the WhatsApp panel’s **Chats** list, a chat whose number has no job shows **Not in the CRM** and **Create opportunity**. Only people with both Enquiries and CRM access see these: CRM staff without Enquiries never see chats with no job, and Enquiries staff without CRM see the chat but not the label or button.

1. Press **Create opportunity**.
2. The app makes a new opportunity at Lead for them, with you as owner, and opens it.

On **CRM › Enquiries**, **Open in CRM** on a lead opens their job, or makes one if they have none. See [Turn an enquiry into an opportunity](/admin/help/enquiries/open-in-crm).

## The same person twice

If two customers share a number, or one person came in twice, merge them. Their WhatsApp messages move to the kept customer, and a WhatsApp from the other number lands in their chat. See [Merge a customer who’s in twice](/admin/help/customers/merge-duplicates).

> **Good to know:** In the WhatsApp panel, one person who came in more than once on the same number shows as one chat.

> **Good to know:** A trade’s number on a site contact (with no customer on it) never becomes a chat: their replies go on the job’s Site history. See [Add someone you can WhatsApp](/admin/help/whatsapp/who-you-can-message).
