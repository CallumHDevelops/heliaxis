Nobody is added to WhatsApp separately: you message people through the record they already have, so their mobile goes there.

| Who | Where their mobile goes | How they get WhatsApps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A customer | **Mobile** when you add them, or their **Customer** card (**✎ Edit**) | Their chat in the CRM. See [Start a WhatsApp chat with someone new](/admin/help/whatsapp/start-a-chat) |
| Someone who messages the business number | Nothing to do | They become a new customer by themselves, with an opportunity at Lead |
| A trade (scaffolder, roofer, electrician) | **Mobile** on their site contact, with **Happy to get job messages on WhatsApp** ticked | Job messages from the site app, not a chat |

## Customers

Add them with their mobile ([Add a customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer)), or add the mobile later on the **Customer** card ([Change a customer’s details](/admin/help/customers/edit-customer-details)). Any chat on that number links to them by itself. The card’s **WhatsApp** line then says "Linked · 12 messages", or "No chat yet" until there is one.

## Trades

A trade gets their job pack, PO and review messages by email. They also get them on WhatsApp once **Happy to get job messages on WhatsApp** is ticked for them, either on **+ New person** when you add them to a job, or on **Team › Subcontractors ›** their firm **› Site contacts**, and once that message’s template is approved on **WhatsApp templates** (until then it goes by email only). Their card on the job then shows "WhatsApp OK". See [Site contacts](/admin/help/team/site-contacts), [Add a trade to a job](/admin/help/site/add-a-trade) and [Job messages to trades on WhatsApp](/admin/help/site/whatsapp-job-messages).

When a trade replies on WhatsApp, the message doesn’t go into the WhatsApp inbox. It goes on their job’s Site history, and the job’s owner gets "WhatsApp from Dave (roofer)" (everyone with Enquiries, if the job has no owner). If they reply STOP, job messages to them go by email only. If the trade’s number is also a customer’s, it stays in that customer’s chat.

## A new number messages you

The app adds them as a new customer (named from their WhatsApp profile, or their number), with an opportunity at Lead (given to the next person, if new leads are shared out on **CRM settings**). Everyone with Enquiries gets "New WhatsApp lead: …". Their message counts as agreeing to WhatsApp. Check their name and add their email and postcode on the **Customer** card.

> **Good to know:** If they’re already in the CRM under a mobile typed another way (07… instead of +44…), the chat finds them and doesn’t make a new customer.
