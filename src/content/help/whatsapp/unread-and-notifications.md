## Unread counts

- **WhatsApp inbox** (or **WhatsApp chats**) in the menu, **WhatsApp** on the phone’s bottom bar, the tablet’s **WhatsApp** button and the panel’s **Chats** tab all show how many **people** have unread messages. Someone who sent five messages counts once.
- Each chat in a list shows its own number of unread messages.
- CRM staff without Enquiries only count the chats they can see.

## When a chat counts as read

Only when it’s really on your screen: the chat is showing in the tab you’re on, and, in the WhatsApp panel, that window is the one you’re using. Then:

- the unread number goes, for everyone;
- the customer sees blue ticks;
- that chat’s notifications are marked read in everyone’s bell and taken off Android phones. A browser notification closes when the chat is opened in that browser, or the next time its bell loads.

Opening a customer’s page marks their messages read only when their chat is showing in the WhatsApp panel (on a wide screen it opens on **This job**). Replying always clears the chat’s notifications, whether you reply here or in the WhatsApp Business app.

## The notifications

Everyone with Enquiries access gets these:

| Notification | When |
| --- | --- |
| New WhatsApp lead: Jane | A number that isn’t in the CRM messages for the first time |
| WhatsApp from Jane | Every other message |
| … with "Smart reply “Battery storage” sends in ~4 min — tap to check, edit or cancel." | A smart reply is queued. It has **Send now** and **Cancel reply** buttons |
| Jane Smith is waiting for a reply | Their message has had no answer for 30 minutes (**Remind me after** on **Smart replies & chasers**). Once per message, only within 24 hours of it, and not in quiet hours |
| WhatsApp message not delivered | WhatsApp couldn’t deliver one of ours |
| Chased Jane | The last chaser has gone and nothing more is scheduled |
| Couldn’t chase Jane / WhatsApp reply to Jane failed | An automatic message couldn’t go |

A STOP or START from the customer is added to the notification. See [WhatsApp permission and STOP](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-permission).

Only the person who scheduled a message hears about it: "Jane replied — your message for 07:00 tomorrow is still scheduled" (with **Cancel message** and **Edit**, then "Your message to Jane went at 07:00" once it goes), "Jane replied STOP: your message for 07:00 tomorrow won’t go" or "Your WhatsApp to Jane Smith didn’t go". See [Send a WhatsApp later](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-later).

A trade’s WhatsApp goes to the job’s owner instead: "WhatsApp from Dave (roofer)". If the job has no owner, everyone with Enquiries gets "WhatsApp from Dave (trade)".

To get these on your phone or computer, see [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).

## Live updates

Nothing needs reloading:

- An open chat checks for new messages and ticks every few seconds. If you’re at the bottom, new messages scroll into view; if you’ve scrolled up to read, it leaves you where you are.
- The WhatsApp inbox updates every 12 seconds, and the WhatsApp panel and WhatsApp chats every 15 seconds.
- They pause while the tab is hidden and catch up as soon as you come back.

**Refresh** (or **↻** in a chat) reloads straight away.
