There’s no "new chat" button: a chat belongs to a person in the CRM, so you start from them.

1. Make sure they’re in the CRM with their mobile.
   - Someone new: add them with their **Mobile**. See [Add a new customer by hand](/admin/help/customers/add-a-customer).
   - Already in, but no mobile: on their **Customer** card, press **Add their mobile** (or **✎ Edit**), fill in **Mobile** and press **Save**.
2. Open their page and press the green WhatsApp button (just above the yellow **+**). On a computer or tablet the chat opens in the WhatsApp panel; on a phone it opens full screen. (With Enquiries access you can also open the lead on **CRM › Enquiries** and press **WhatsApp**.) See [The + and WhatsApp buttons on a customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/plus-and-whatsapp-buttons).
3. They haven’t messaged the CRM’s number yet, so the chat says "Start the conversation with an approved template. Once they reply you can chat freely for 24 hours."
4. Pick a template, check the blanks and press **Send template**. See [Send a template in a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-a-template).
5. Once they reply, you can type freely for 24 hours.

Type the mobile however you like (07700 900123, +44 7700 900123): the app works out the WhatsApp number.

> **Watch out:** Only message people who’ve agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Until someone has WhatsApp permission, an empty chat says "This lead hasn’t opted in to WhatsApp. Only message them if they’ve agreed to it (e.g. on the phone)." See [WhatsApp permission and STOP](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-permission).

> **Good to know:** Sending a WhatsApp counts as getting in touch: a customer at Lead moves to Contacted.

## When they message you first

You don’t need to do anything. A number the CRM doesn’t know becomes a new customer by itself (source WhatsApp, or WhatsApp ad if they tapped an ad), with an opportunity at Lead (handed to the next person, if new leads are shared out on **CRM settings**). Everyone with Enquiries gets "New WhatsApp lead: …", and you can reply freely for 24 hours.

## A trade, not a customer?

Trades get their job messages from the site app, not from a chat. See [Add someone you can WhatsApp](/admin/help/whatsapp/who-you-can-message).

If WhatsApp isn’t connected yet, the chat says sending is off until the Meta keys are added.
