**CRM › WhatsApp chats** is the WhatsApp page for people with CRM access but not Enquiries. It says "Chats with customers who have a job in the CRM." The same list is the **Chats** tab of the WhatsApp panel.

## The filters

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs reply** | They wrote last, and nothing is queued or scheduled to answer them |
| **Unread** | Chats with unread messages |
| **All active** | Anyone with a message in the last 14 days, anything unread, or a 24-hour window still open |
| **Waiting on them** | You wrote last |
| **All chats** | Everyone with WhatsApp history, imported chats too, newest first. **Show older chats** loads more |

It opens on **Needs reply** while anyone needs a reply, otherwise on **All active**. Each filter shows a count, except **All chats**.

## Search

**Search every chat: name, number or HX-ref** looks through every chat, not just the active ones. Postcodes work too, and so do several words in any order ("rob evans"). Names that start with what you typed come first. Picking a filter clears the search.

## Each row

- Their initials, name, the job’s stage, a reference (the job’s, else the customer’s, else their postcode) and how long ago.
- The last message ("You: " in front if it was ours; a picture shows as "📷 Photo", a voice note as "🎤 Voice note") and the unread count.
- **Free reply · 18h left** while you can type freely, or **Template only**.
- **Reply queued**, **Scheduled 07:00** or **Chasing** when something is waiting to go.
- **Open job ↗** opens their latest open job.

In the active lists, people with unread messages come first, then those needing a reply, then the most recent. Rows come 15 at a time: **Show more (12)** (with how many are left) adds the next ones. Under **All active**, **Older chats, imported ones too: All chats →** switches to **All chats**.

Tap a row and the chat opens full screen. **←** brings you back to all chats. The list updates itself every 15 seconds while it’s on screen.

> **Good to know:** A customer whose jobs are all Complete or Lost still shows here, but **Open job ↗** only appears while they have an open one.
