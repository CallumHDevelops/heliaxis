Once 24 hours have passed since a customer last messaged, WhatsApp only lets you send a **template**: a message Meta has approved in advance. **CRM › WhatsApp templates** is where they’re sent to Meta, checked and chosen for the automatic messages. On a phone it’s **Templates** under **More**.

Anyone with Enquiries access can look. Only admins can add, delete or change templates; everyone else sees "Only admins can add, delete or change them."

![The WhatsApp templates page on the Ready-made tab](/help/whatsapp/templates-ready-made.jpg "Ready-made templates: one tap sends each to Meta")

## The tabs

- **Your templates** (with a count): every template on the WhatsApp account. Each card shows its name, its status (such as **Approved ✓** or **Waiting for Meta**), its type and language, and a preview with each blank named. "Used for:" lists the automatic messages that send it. Admins get **Use for…** (on approved ones), **Say what fills the blanks** or **Change what fills the blanks** (on ones with blanks) and **Delete**.
- **Ready-made** (with a count): Heliaxis’s own templates, worded to pass Meta’s rules. Each card says when it’s used and what it costs. Admins press **Send to Meta for approval**; once it’s approved, **Use for “…”** where it has an automatic job. Until then others see "Not sent to Meta yet. An admin can send it with one tap."
- **+ New template** (admins only): write your own. See [Add a WhatsApp template](/admin/help/whatsapp/add-a-template).

On a first visit, while the account only has Meta’s sample, the page opens on **Ready-made**. At the bottom, **Utility or Marketing? What each costs** explains the two types.

While anything is waiting for Meta, the page checks again every 20 seconds by itself.

## The ready-made templates

| Template | Type | What it’s for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome a new enquiry | Utility | First message to a new lead who said yes to WhatsApp |
| Get back in touch with an older lead | Marketing | Leads who went quiet weeks or months ago |
| Quote ready | Utility | Their quote is ready, with the link |
| Sorry we missed you | Utility | After you rang and they didn’t pick up |
| Survey booked / reminder | Utility | Survey confirmation and reminders |
| Install booked / reminder | Utility | Install confirmation and reminders |
| Google review request | Utility | A few days after a job is marked Complete |
| Trade: your job pack, PO raised, scaffold strike photos, clear to start, don’t use the scaffold yet, return to site required, green light | Utility | The site app’s messages to trades |
| Handover pack ready | Utility | When the customer’s handover pack goes into their portal |

See [Templates the app sends by itself](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates-the-app-uses) and [Template statuses, rejections and changes](/admin/help/whatsapp/template-approval).

## If the page can’t see your templates

It says "Heliaxis can’t see your templates yet." with the reason, and **WhatsApp setup guide**. When it’s the connection, admins also get **Check the WhatsApp connection →**.

> **Good to know:** **← WhatsApp** at the top goes to the WhatsApp inbox, and **Smart replies & chasers** to the smart replies page.
