When a customer says "call me tomorrow after 4", you can set it up without leaving their chat.

1. In the chat, press **+ Add task** (at the top of a full chat, or above the chat in the WhatsApp panel).
2. Pick **Call-back reminder** (at a set time), **Task** (who and which day) or **Note** (on the job’s timeline).
3. The app fills it in from their latest message ("Tomorrow’s fine" becomes tomorrow). Check the words, the day or time and who it’s for.
4. Save it.

To start from one particular message with words in it, press the **⋯** beside it (on a computer it appears when you point at the message; on a phone it’s always there) and pick **⏰ Make a reminder from this** or **✓ Make a task from this**.

Nothing is saved until you save. A line in the chat then says what was set (such as "Reminder set: … — you’ll get a notification"), with **Open job** and **OK**.

The sheet says which job it goes on. A task or note goes on their open job, or failing that their last one. A call-back reminder needs an open job, because a closed job’s reminder never goes off. If there isn’t one, saving opens a new CRM record for them (the sheet says "This also opens a CRM record for Jane.").

**+ Add task** and the **⋯** only show for people with CRM access.

For the full details, including how a call-back reminder differs from a task and who gets told, see [Add a task from a job or a WhatsApp chat](/admin/help/tasks/from-a-job-or-chat).
