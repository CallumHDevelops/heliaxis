**CRM › WhatsApp inbox** lists every WhatsApp chat, newest first. You need Enquiries access. CRM staff without it use [The WhatsApp chats page](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-chats-page) instead.

## At the top

- **← Enquiries** goes back to the Enquiries page.
- **Smart replies** opens **Smart replies & chasers**.
- **Refresh** reloads the list now. You rarely need it: the list updates itself every 12 seconds while it’s on screen.
- A **Turn on notifications** banner shows until notifications are on for this device.
- The search box (**Search name or number**, or **Search name, number or HX-0042** once customers have references). Type several words in any order, such as "rob evans".
- Three filters, each with a count:
  - **All**.
  - **Needs reply**: they wrote last, and no smart reply, first message or scheduled reply is waiting to answer them.
  - **Waiting on them**: you wrote last.

## Each row

- Their initial, name, reference (HX-0042) and how long ago the last message was.
- The last message. "You: " in front means it was from us. "Opted in — not messaged yet" is someone who agreed to WhatsApp (on a Meta form, say) but hasn’t had a message.
- **⏱ reply queued**: a smart reply or a first message is about to go.
- **🕐 scheduled 07:00**: someone has used Send later. See [Send a WhatsApp later](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-later).
- **↻ chasing**: a chaser is waiting. See [Chasers and chase hours](/admin/help/whatsapp/chasers).
- A number: how many messages are unread. Unread rows are in bold.
- **24h window closed · template only**: only an approved template can go now. See [The 24-hour window](/admin/help/whatsapp/24-hour-window).

Tap a row to open the chat. See [Read and reply to a WhatsApp](/admin/help/whatsapp/read-and-reply).

When there are no chats, or nothing matches the search or filter, it says "No WhatsApp conversations yet. Leads who message you, or opt in on a Meta form, show up here."

> **Good to know:** The inbox shows the newest 300 chats, and its search looks through those. To find an older or imported chat, use the search box in the WhatsApp panel, which looks through every chat.

> **Good to know:** If WhatsApp isn’t connected, the page says "WhatsApp isn’t connected yet — conversations will appear here once the Meta keys are added (see WHATSAPP_SETUP.md)." An admin needs to finish the connection.
