Every WhatsApp with a customer lives in the CRM: the WhatsApp inbox (or WhatsApp chats), the WhatsApp panel on the right of the screen, and the green button on a customer’s page. Start with who sees which page, then how to read and reply, the 24-hour rule and templates, and the automatic parts: smart replies, chasers, Send later and the late-night rule. If a message doesn’t go, see [When a WhatsApp doesn’t go](/admin/help/whatsapp/message-didnt-send).
