## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not sent yet** | A ready-made template nobody has sent to Meta |
| **Waiting for Meta** | Usually a few minutes, sometimes up to a day. The page updates by itself |
| **Waiting for Meta (appeal)** | Meta is looking at it again |
| **Approved ✓** | Ready to send |
| **Rejected** | Meta said no. The reason shows underneath |
| **Paused** | Meta paused it because people blocked or reported it. It restarts by itself after a few hours. If it keeps happening, change the wording |
| **Disabled** | Meta turned it off after pausing it too often. Make a new one with different wording |
| **Being deleted** | It’s on its way out |
| **Over Meta’s limit** | The account has as many templates as Meta allows. Delete ones you don’t use |

"People are blocking or reporting this one" on a card is an early warning: change the wording before Meta pauses it.

## When Meta rejects one

The card puts Meta’s reason in plain words, for example:

- "Meta says the format is wrong: usually a blank at the very start or end, two blanks together, or examples that don’t look real."
- "Meta thinks it’s selling something. Send it again as Marketing, or take out anything that sounds like an offer."
- "Meta says the wording doesn’t fit the category (e.g. sales wording in a Utility template). Try it as Marketing."

Then: "You can change it and ask again in WhatsApp Manager, or delete it and make a new one with another name."

## Changing a template

The words of a template can’t be changed in the app. Either change it in **WhatsApp Manager** (the link on a rejected card), or delete it and make a new one with another name.

> **Admins only:** **Delete** asks first: "Delete “quote_ready” (English (UK))? Nobody can send it any more, and Meta won’t let you reuse the name for about 4 weeks."

## Say what fills the blanks

What you can change in the app is what goes in each blank, so the boxes fill themselves when someone sends it and automatic messages know what to put in.

1. On the card, press **Say what fills the blanks** (or **Change what fills the blanks**).
2. For each **Blank 1**, **Blank 2**…, pick **Customer’s first name**, **Your first name**, **What they asked about**, **Quote / portal link**, **When (“tomorrow”, “in 3 days”)**, **Date and time** or **Google review link**.
3. For anything else, pick **Something else (typed in when sending)** and say what it is (for example "Their postcode").
4. Press **Save**.

> **Admins only:** Only admins see these buttons.

A template nobody has described is sent with its first blank as the customer’s first name.

## Cards with a note

- "Meta’s sample. It’s never offered when sending." is Meta’s hello_world template.
- "Heliaxis can’t send this one: it has a picture or a button that needs filling in. Send it from WhatsApp Manager."
