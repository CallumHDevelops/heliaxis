WhatsApp’s own rule: for 24 hours after a customer’s last message to the CRM’s WhatsApp number, you can send anything you like, free. After that, or if they’ve never messaged that number, only a **template** can go: a message Meta has approved in advance. Free chat opens again the moment they reply.

## How to tell

- The chat’s top line says "19h left" (or "35m left") while it’s open, and "window closed" after.
- The panel and WhatsApp chats say **Free reply · 18h left** or **Template only**.
- The WhatsApp inbox says **24h window closed · template only**.
- The reply box turns into a template picker, with one of these:
  - "It’s been over 24 hours since they last messaged, so WhatsApp only allows an approved template. Free chat reopens as soon as they reply."
  - "Start the conversation with an approved template. Once they reply you can chat freely for 24 hours."
  - For an imported chat: "This chat was imported from the WhatsApp Business app. They haven’t messaged the CRM’s WhatsApp number yet, so start with an approved template…"

Then see [Send a template in a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-a-template).

## What it costs

- Inside the 24 hours: free, and no template needed.
- A **Utility** template: about 1.6p a message, and free if they’ve messaged you in the last 24 hours.
- A **Marketing** template: about 4–5p a message, every time.

See [WhatsApp templates](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates) for which is which.

## What else the window decides

- **Documents** from a job only go inside the 24 hours ("Documents can only be sent within 24h of their last message — send it by email, or put it in their portal.").
- **Send later:** a typed message has to go before the window closes. A template can go any time. See [Send a WhatsApp later](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-later).
- **Chasers** go as a free message inside the window, otherwise as a paid template. See [Chasers and chase hours](/admin/help/whatsapp/chasers).
- **Reminders** (survey, install, review requests) go as a free message inside the window, otherwise as their template. See [Templates the app sends by itself](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates-the-app-uses).

> **Good to know:** Only a message from the customer to the CRM’s WhatsApp number opens the window. Imported history from the WhatsApp Business app doesn’t, and nor do our own messages.
