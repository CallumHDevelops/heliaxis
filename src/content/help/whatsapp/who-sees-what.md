There are two WhatsApp pages. Which one you get depends on your access, which an admin sets on **Users** (see [What you can see depends on your access](/admin/help/getting-started/your-access)).

| Your access | In the menu | Which chats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries (with or without CRM) | **CRM › WhatsApp inbox** | Every WhatsApp chat |
| CRM, without Enquiries | **CRM › WhatsApp chats** | Only chats whose number belongs to a customer with a job in the CRM (an opportunity at any stage, open or closed) |
| Neither | Nothing | No WhatsApp |

Admins can open everything, so they get the inbox.

On a phone, **WhatsApp** sits on the bottom bar instead, with the number of people who have unread messages. It opens the same page.

On a computer, anyone with Enquiries or CRM also gets the WhatsApp panel on the right of most pages. See [The WhatsApp panel](/admin/help/whatsapp/the-whatsapp-panel).

## Only with Enquiries access

- Chats with numbers that have no job in the CRM.
- **Smart replies & chasers** and **WhatsApp templates** in the CRM menu. Only admins can add, delete or change templates.
- The **Smart replies on** / **Smart replies off** button on a chat.
- A notification for each new WhatsApp message. Everyone with Enquiries gets them.

With both Enquiries and CRM, a chat whose number has no job also shows **Not in the CRM** and **Create opportunity** in the WhatsApp panel. See [Link a chat to a customer](/admin/help/whatsapp/link-a-chat-to-a-customer).

CRM staff without Enquiries can still read and reply to their customers’ chats, send templates and add tasks from a chat. If no template has been approved yet, their chat says "Ask whoever looks after Enquiries to add one in WhatsApp templates."

## Can’t find a chat?

- **CRM staff without Enquiries:** the customer needs a job in the CRM on the same mobile number. Check the number on their **Customer** card. See [Link a chat to a customer](/admin/help/whatsapp/link-a-chat-to-a-customer).
- **A trade’s reply** to a job pack or PO doesn’t appear in either page. It goes on the job’s Site history and the job’s owner gets a notification (everyone with Enquiries, if the job has no owner). See [Job messages to trades on WhatsApp](/admin/help/site/whatsapp-job-messages).
- **An older chat:** the WhatsApp inbox lists the newest 300 chats. The search box in the WhatsApp panel, and **All chats** there, look through every chat, imported ones too.

> **Admins only:** Importing old chats from the WhatsApp Business app is on **CRM › Enquiries › Import**. See [Import WhatsApp Business app chats](/admin/help/whatsapp/import-chats).
