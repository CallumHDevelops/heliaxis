When someone fills in a Meta lead form, gives a mobile number and says yes to WhatsApp, the app writes their first WhatsApp for them. You know which video (ad) they watched, so each ad can have its own template. Any ad without one gets the general first message.

> **Admins only:** Anyone with Enquiries can look at **First messages**. Only admins can change it.

## Switch on sending by itself

1. Go to **CRM › WhatsApp templates** and open **First messages**.
2. Tick **Send it by itself, 8–13 minutes after they come in**. It says "First messages now go by themselves ✓".
3. To change the timing, type new numbers in **Between … and … minutes after they come in, at random** and press **Save**. Each lead gets a random time in that range, so it doesn’t look automatic.

Someone who enquires in quiet hours gets it in the morning instead, while **Hold the first message to new leads who enquire in quiet hours until the morning** is ticked on **Smart replies & chasers** (it is to start with).

If the box is ticked and greyed out, it says "Switched on in Vercel (WHATSAPP_AUTO_FIRST_MESSAGE is “true”), so it can’t be switched off here."

## Choose a template for each video

Under **By video** are the ads that have brought leads in the last 90 days, under their names now, busiest first. Each shows what it used to be called ("was Video 4") and how many leads it’s brought. The same ad name in two ad sets is one row.

1. Pick a template in the box under the ad. It says "Its leads get “…” ✓", and the template’s words show underneath, with what fills each blank in square brackets.
2. To go back to the general first message, pick **The general first message**.

Only templates that can be a first message are offered: approved, in English (UK), and only asking for the customer’s first name or what they asked about.

> **Good to know:** Renaming an ad in Ads Manager doesn’t lose its choice. The app matches a lead to its ad by Meta’s own number for it, or by any name the ad has had.

## Write a new one for a video

1. Press **Write one for this video** beside the ad.
2. The **+ New template** form opens, already filled in from the ready-made welcome, with the title "First message" and the ad’s name, and its three quick replies.
3. Change the words so they follow on from that video, then press **Send to Meta for approval**.
4. Once Meta has approved it, come back to **First messages** and pick it for the ad.

See [Add a WhatsApp template](/admin/help/whatsapp/add-a-template) for the rest of the form.

## What happens when a lead comes in

- The team’s notification says when it goes: "WhatsApp first message (“…”) goes at 10:42 today."
- In their chat it waits as **⏱ First message “…” sends in …**, showing the message as they’ll see it, with **Send now** and **Cancel**.
- It goes as their ad’s template, else the general first message. Their enquiry then moves to **Contacted**.
- While it’s waiting they’re not on the dashboard’s **New Meta leads** card: it’s in hand.

With **Send it by itself** off, nothing goes by itself. Their ad’s template is the one their chat offers first, ready for someone to check and press Send.

> **Watch out:** If a template stops being approved, its ad says “…” isn’t approved or can’t be filled in now, so these leads’ first message won’t go. Choose another.

Leads who start a chat from a WhatsApp ad message you first, so they don’t get one. For the general first message, see [Templates the app sends by itself](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates-the-app-uses).
