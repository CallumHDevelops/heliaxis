CRM staff without Enquiries can do all of this for customers with a job, from **WhatsApp chats** or the WhatsApp panel.

## Reply

1. Open the chat: tap a row in the WhatsApp inbox or WhatsApp chats, a chat head or row in the WhatsApp panel, or the green WhatsApp button on a customer’s page.
2. Check the top: their name, reference, number, and how long you have left to type freely ("19h left"), or "window closed". In the WhatsApp panel the bar says **Free reply · 18h left** or **Template only**.
3. Type in the box at the bottom (**Message**, or **Type a reply…** in the panel).
4. Check **Chase if no reply:** just above the box. **Auto** chases a message with a "?" in it. See [Chasers and chase hours](/admin/help/whatsapp/chasers).
5. Press **Send**, or Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on a Mac).

Enter on its own starts a new line, so a half-written message never goes by accident.

Your message appears in the chat straight away, with ✓ once it’s sent and ✓✓ once it’s delivered. See [What you see in a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/whats-in-a-chat).

- After 20:30, **Send** says **at 07:00** under it and the message waits until the morning. See [The late-night rule](/admin/help/whatsapp/late-night-rule).
- The clock beside **Send** schedules it for later. See [Send a WhatsApp later](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-later).

## No reply box?

If it’s been more than 24 hours since they last messaged, or they never have, the box is replaced by a template picker. See [The 24-hour window](/admin/help/whatsapp/24-hour-window) and [Send a template in a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-a-template).

## Your unsent reply is kept

What you type is saved for you, for that chat, in this browser. Close the chat and it’s there when you come back. If a send fails, the message stays in the box so you can try again. See [When a WhatsApp doesn’t go](/admin/help/whatsapp/message-didnt-send).

## Extras in the WhatsApp panel

With CRM access, a chat with a job has these above the reply box:

- **⚡ Saved replies**: pick one to add it to your message. They’re set up on **CRM settings** under **Saved replies**, and can use {first_name} and {portal_link}. See [Add a saved reply](/admin/help/admin/saved-replies).
- **📎 Send a document** (on the **This job** tab): pick one of the job’s documents or one from the library, and press **Send document**. Documents only go inside the 24 hours, and they go straight away, even late at night.

## What sending also does

- Any smart reply or chaser still waiting in that chat is cancelled.
- A customer at Lead moves to Contacted. See [Sales stages and moving a customer along](/admin/help/customers/sales-stages).
- That chat’s notifications, and "… is waiting for a reply", clear for everyone.

> **Watch out:** "This lead hasn’t opted in to WhatsApp. Only message them if they’ve agreed to it (e.g. on the phone)." shows above an empty chat for someone without WhatsApp permission. See [WhatsApp permission and STOP](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-permission).

> **Good to know:** One message can be up to 4,096 characters.

To set a call-back reminder or a task from the chat, see [Make a task or call-back reminder from a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/task-from-a-chat).
