> **Admins only:** Only admins can send templates to Meta. Everyone else with Enquiries can see them.

## Send a ready-made one

1. Go to **CRM › WhatsApp templates** and open the **Ready-made** tab.
2. Find the template. Its card says when it’s used, its type and what it costs.
3. Press **Send to Meta for approval**.

It says "Approved straight away ✓", or "Sent to Meta ✓ It usually takes a few minutes, and this page updates by itself." If Meta changes its type, it adds "Meta has made it Marketing."

## Write your own

1. Open the **+ New template** tab.
2. **What’s it for?**: a few words, such as "Survey follow-up". **Name (made for you; customers never see it)** fills itself in from that (lowercase letters, numbers and _). Change it if you like.
3. **Type**: pick **Utility** (about something the customer has already asked for or booked; no offers or selling) or **Marketing** (anything else, such as getting back in touch with older leads; only for people who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp).
4. **Message**: it starts "Hi {first_name}, ". Add blanks with the buttons above the box rather than typing them: **+ Customer’s first name**, **+ Your first name**, **+ What they asked about** and **+ Quote / portal link**. They show as {first_name}, {your_name}, {asked_about} and {quote_link}, and are filled in when it’s sent. Up to 1,024 characters.
5. **Examples for Meta (they check these)**: one per blank, already filled in. Make them look real.
6. **Quick replies (optional, up to 3)**: **+ Add a quick reply** adds a button the customer can tap, up to 25 characters. **×** removes one.
7. Check **How it looks**.
8. Press **Send to Meta for approval**.

It moves to **Your templates**, showing "“survey_follow_up” sent to Meta ✓ …" and **Waiting for Meta**. It goes in English (UK). Meta may move it to Marketing if it reads as selling.

## What Meta won’t accept

The form lists anything wrong and won’t send until it’s fixed. The usual ones:

- A message that starts or ends with a blank, or two blanks side by side. Put words around them.
- Too many blanks for a short message.
- A name that’s already used, or kept for a ready-made template ("Send that one from the Ready-made tab.").
- Typing {{1}} yourself: use the buttons instead.
- An empty or repeated quick reply.
