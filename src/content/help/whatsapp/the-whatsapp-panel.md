Anyone with CRM or Enquiries access gets the WhatsApp panel on a computer or tablet, so you can answer chats without leaving the page you’re on. Phones don’t have it: use **WhatsApp** on the bottom bar.

<!-- shot: whatsapp/panel-chats.jpg | The WhatsApp panel: filters, search and chats | The WhatsApp panel docked open on the CRM Pipeline, on the Chats tab -->

## How it shows

| Screen | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Wide screen (1280 pixels or more) | The panel docked open on the right, or folded to a strip of chat heads |
| Smaller laptop (1024 to 1279 pixels) | The strip; the panel opens over the page |
| Tablet | A **WhatsApp** button in the header, with the unread count; it opens the panel over the page |
| Phone | No panel |

It starts open on the CRM **Pipeline** (Home view) and on job pages, and folded to the strip on most other pages. It’s hidden on the WhatsApp inbox and WhatsApp chats pages, where the chat already fills the screen. Once you open or fold it, it stays that way on that kind of page, for you, in that browser.

## The strip

- The WhatsApp logo and **«** open the panel.
- Up to six chat heads (initials): this job’s customer, the chat that’s open, then people with unread messages (with a count), then people waiting for a reply. Click one to open their chat.
- At the bottom, how many people have unread messages.

## The panel

Along the top:

- **This job · Jane** (only on a job page) and **Chats**, with the number of people with unread messages.
- **Inbox ↗** (with Enquiries access) or **All chats ↗** opens the full WhatsApp page.
- **»** folds it away. When it’s over the page, **✕** closes it.

**Chats** is the same list as [The WhatsApp chats page](/admin/help/whatsapp/whatsapp-chats-page): **Needs reply**, **Unread**, **All active**, **Waiting on them**, **All chats** and the search box. Click a row to open the chat in the panel (Ctrl-click opens it in a new tab). A chat with no job shows **Not in the CRM** and **Create opportunity**: see [Link a chat to a customer](/admin/help/whatsapp/link-a-chat-to-a-customer).

An open chat has a bar with **← All chats**, their name and stage, **Open job**, **Free reply · 18h left** or **Template only**, their reference and **Full chat ↗**. The reply box says "Replying to Jane · +44…". See [Read and reply to a WhatsApp](/admin/help/whatsapp/read-and-reply).

After you send, the panel says "Sent ✓ · Next needing reply: Bob Jones (1h) →" (or "Scheduled for 07:00 tomorrow ✓" when it waits). Click the name to go straight to that chat.

## This job

On a customer’s or project’s page, **This job** shows that customer’s chat. With CRM access you also get **⚡ Saved replies** and **📎 Send a document** above the reply box (a chat on the **Chats** tab whose customer has an open job gets **⚡ Saved replies** too). On a wide screen the job’s **Tasks** card stays in the panel too, on either tab.

- No mobile yet: "Add their mobile to message them on WhatsApp." with **Add mobile**, which opens the **Customer** card for editing.
- Open someone else’s chat while you’re on a job and it says "You’re on Jane Smith’s job. This chat is with Bob Jones." with **Back to Jane’s chat**.

> **Good to know:** **Esc** takes you from a chat back to the list, and closes the panel when it’s open over the page.

For the green WhatsApp button on a customer’s page, see [The + and WhatsApp buttons on a customer’s page](/admin/help/customers/plus-and-whatsapp-buttons).

> **Good to know:** A chat only counts as read while it’s on screen in the window you’re using. Leaving the panel open in a window you’re not looking at doesn’t mark messages read.
