When the 24 hours are up (or they’ve never messaged), the chat offers a template instead of the reply box. See [The 24-hour window](/admin/help/whatsapp/24-hour-window).

1. Open the chat.
2. Pick a template from the list. Each shows its name and type, for example "Quote ready · Utility". The first one offered is the template chosen for **First message to new Meta leads** (or the default set in Vercel), when there is one.
3. Check the preview: it’s the message as the customer will see it.
4. Check each box under it. Each is labelled with what goes there, such as **Customer’s first name** or **Quote / portal link**. The app fills in what it knows from the CRM; anything it can’t fill stays empty with a hint and an example. Change any of them if you need to. (A quote or portal link is only filled in for people with CRM access, once the customer has one.)
5. If you want a chaser, pick a time in **Chase if no reply:**. A template is only chased if you choose a time: **Auto** is off for templates.
6. Press **Send template**.

**Send template** waits until every box has something in it. Late at night it says **Send template at 07:00** (see [The late-night rule](/admin/help/whatsapp/late-night-rule)), and the clock beside it schedules it (see [Send a WhatsApp later](/admin/help/whatsapp/send-later)).

The template appears in the chat as the customer saw it. If it has quick-reply buttons, they get those too.

## Which templates you can pick

Only approved ones Heliaxis can send, in English (UK) once there is one. Meta’s sample template, the site app’s trade templates and templates with a picture or a button that needs filling in are never offered in a customer’s chat.

No approved templates yet? The chat says "No approved templates yet." With Enquiries access it adds **Send one to Meta from WhatsApp templates →**; otherwise "Ask whoever looks after Enquiries to add one in WhatsApp templates." See [Add a WhatsApp template](/admin/help/whatsapp/add-a-template).

## A template instead of a late typed message

If you schedule a typed message for after their window closes, the app offers **Send a template at 07:00 tomorrow instead**. The box then says "A template to go at 07:00 tomorrow: …" with **Back to typing**, and the button says **Send template at 07:00**.

> **Good to know:** Sometimes a job opens a **WhatsApp Jane Smith** sheet instead of the chat (for example on a phone, when a button has already written the message for you). It works the same way, and says "outside 24 hours: an approved template is needed (about 2–5p)" when the window has closed.
