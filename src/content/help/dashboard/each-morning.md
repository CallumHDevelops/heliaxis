The dashboard is built to be the first thing you open each day. Work down it from the top. You’ll only see the parts your account has.

1. **Open the dashboard.** Tap **Home** on a phone, or **Dashboard** on a computer. If you’ve had the page open since yesterday, reload it so the numbers are fresh.
2. **Clear Needs you.** Each row is something waiting: unread WhatsApp chats, follow-ups due, drafts from Metis to send, overdue tasks, new enquiries, subcontractor paperwork and (admins) sign-ups. Tap a row to go straight there. See [What’s on the dashboard](/admin/help/dashboard/whats-on-the-dashboard).
3. **Contact new Meta leads.** In **New Meta leads**, use **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Open** on each one. Once their enquiry moves on from New, they leave the card. See [New Meta leads on the dashboard](/admin/help/dashboard/new-meta-leads).
4. **Check Today.** See who’s being surveyed and installed today, and your own diary. Surveyors can press **Start survey** and **Directions** from here. See [Today’s surveys, installs and diary](/admin/help/dashboard/today).
5. **Work through My day.** Deal with each follow-up (tap it to open the job), then your tasks. Tap a task’s circle when it’s done. See [Your follow-ups and tasks in My day](/admin/help/dashboard/my-day).
6. **Admins: glance at From Claude.** If a setup job is waiting, open it and follow the steps. See [From Claude setup to-dos](/admin/help/dashboard/from-claude).

> **Good to know:** If you have tasks due, the app also sends a morning round-up of what’s due today and overdue (once switched on). See [Task notifications and reminders](/admin/help/tasks/task-notifications).

> **Good to know:** Turn on notifications on your phone so new messages, leads and tasks reach you during the day without checking. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).
