The **New Meta leads** card shows leads from Meta (Facebook and Instagram lead forms, Meta ad links and WhatsApp ad chats) whose enquiry is still at **New**. It’s near the top of the dashboard for anyone with Enquiries or CRM.

![The New Meta leads card](/help/dashboard/meta-leads.jpg "New Meta leads: count, today, and Open, WhatsApp and Call")

## What it shows

- Beside the heading: how many new Meta leads are waiting, and how many of them came in today ("2 today").
- The newest six, each with:
  - their name and HX reference,
  - the ad they came from, by the ad’s current name (or **Organic**, or **WhatsApp ad**). If the ad has been renamed since, point at the name on a computer to see what it was called,
  - their postcode,
  - how long ago they came in.

A lead drops off the card as soon as their enquiry is no longer at **New**.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does | Shows for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Opens their open job (with CRM). With no open job, or without CRM, it opens their details on Enquiries. With CRM but not Enquiries, it opens their latest job even if it’s closed. | Everyone, except CRM-only staff when the lead has no job at all |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens their chat in the WhatsApp inbox. | Enquiries, when they gave a phone number |
| **Call** | Starts a call to their number (on a phone). | When they gave a phone number |

**See all new Meta leads →** at the bottom opens Enquiries showing only Meta leads that are New, where you can work through the whole list. That link is for people with Enquiries.

## When there are none

The card says "No new Meta leads." and when the last one came in ("The last one came in 3 days ago."), or "None have come in from Meta yet."

> **Once switched on:** HX references show once customer-refs.sql has been run.

For working through leads on Enquiries, see [Contact a new lead](/admin/help/enquiries/contact-a-lead) and [Meta lead ads coming in](/admin/help/enquiries/meta-lead-ads).
