The **New Meta leads** card shows the leads from Meta (Facebook and Instagram lead forms, Meta ad links and WhatsApp ad chats) that someone needs to get to now. It’s near the top of the dashboard for anyone with Enquiries or CRM.

![The New Meta leads card](/help/dashboard/meta-leads.jpg "New Meta leads: count, today, and Open, WhatsApp and Call")

## Who’s on it

A lead is on the card for one of three reasons, shown in a tag under their name:

| Tag | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Free chat ends in 4h** | They messaged us, and their free 24-hour window closes within 6 hours. Message them while it’s still free. |
| **Not contacted** | Their enquiry is still at **New**. |
| **Quiet for 2 days** | We’ve been in touch (they’re at **Contacted**, with no survey booked), they came in within the last 30 days, and nothing has been said either way on WhatsApp for a day or more. |

Nobody with a message lined up is on the card: one someone scheduled with **Send later**, the automatic first message, a smart reply or a chaser waiting to go. That’s already in hand. A message that failed doesn’t count, as it didn’t go.

They’re listed most urgent first: free chat ending soonest, then the newest not contacted, then the ones that went quiet most recently.

> **Good to know:** Leads who only spoke to us by phone or email aren’t judged as quiet: the card only knows about WhatsApp.

## What each row shows

- Beside the heading: how many leads are on the card, and how many of them came in today ("2 today").
- The top six, each with:
  - their name and HX reference,
  - why they’re there (the tag),
  - the ad they came from, by the ad’s current name (or **Organic**, or **WhatsApp ad**). If the ad has been renamed since, point at the name on a computer to see what it was called,
  - their postcode,
  - how long ago they came in.

A lead drops off the card as soon as a message is lined up for them, they book a survey, or they move on past **Contacted**.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does | Shows for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Opens their open job (with CRM). With no open job, or without CRM, it opens their details on Enquiries. With CRM but not Enquiries, it opens their latest job even if it’s closed. | Everyone, except CRM-only staff when the lead has no job at all |
| **WhatsApp** | Opens their chat in the WhatsApp inbox. | Enquiries, when they gave a phone number |
| **Call** | Starts a call to their number (on a phone). | When they gave a phone number |

**See all 12 not contacted →** (**See the 1 not contacted →** when there’s one) at the bottom opens Enquiries showing only Meta leads that are New, where you can work through the whole list. It shows while any are not contacted, for people with Enquiries.

## When there are none

The card says "Nobody to get to: every Meta lead has been contacted or has a message lined up." and when the last one came in ("The last one came in 3 days ago."), or "None have come in from Meta yet."

> **Once switched on:** HX references show once customer-refs.sql has been run.
