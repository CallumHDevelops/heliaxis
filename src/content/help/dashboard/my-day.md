**My day** sits on the dashboard under **Today**. It shows two things, and disappears when there’s nothing in it.

## Follow-ups (CRM)

Jobs whose next step is due today or is overdue, if the job is yours or nobody looks after it yet. Up to six show, the earliest due first. Each has a ⏰, the next step (for example a call-back you set from a WhatsApp chat), the job’s name, and either the time it’s due today or **Overdue** if it was due on an earlier day.

Tap one to open the customer’s page, then deal with it or move its next step on. See [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).

## Your tasks (Tasks)

Your open tasks: ones assigned to you that aren’t done. Up to six show, the soonest deadline first and those with no deadline last. Each shows:

- a circle to tick it off,
- the task, with **High priority** under it if it’s high,
- its deadline: **Today**, **Yesterday**, "3d overdue", a date, or **No date**.

To finish a task, tap its circle. It goes from the list straight away. If it can’t be saved (no signal, say), it comes back.

Tap a task’s name to open the Tasks page, or **All tasks →** for the full list. See [The Tasks page](/admin/help/tasks/tasks-page).

> **Good to know:** My day only lists tasks assigned to you. Tasks you made for other people, and ones with nobody on them, are on the Tasks page. If you have CRM, **My work** on the CRM home shows your tasks and next steps too. See [My work: your tasks and next steps](/admin/help/customers/my-work).
