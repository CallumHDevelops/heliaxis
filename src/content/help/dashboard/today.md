**Today** is on the dashboard for people with CRM. It lists, in time order:

- every survey booked for today,
- every install happening today (on each working day of a longer install),
- your own diary entries for today.

With nothing on, it says "No surveys, installs or diary entries today."

## Each line

- **The time**: a survey’s start and finish ("09:00–10:30"), an install’s start time on its first day and **All day** after that, or a diary entry’s times (or **All day**).
- **A tag**: **Survey**, **Visit**, **Install** or **Diary**.
- **The customer’s name** (or the diary entry’s title), then what it is and the postcode. For example "Solar survey · NP20 4AB", "Heat pump survey", "Home visit", "Install, day 2 of 3", or for a diary entry its kind and where ("At home", "Online / phone" or a postcode).

Tap the line to open the customer’s job. A diary entry that isn’t for a job opens **Surveys and diary**.

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start survey** | Opens the survey. Today’s and tomorrow’s surveys are copied to your phone while it has a connection, so they open with no signal too. |
| **Continue** | Carries on with a survey you’ve already started. |
| **Report** | Opens the report of a finished survey. |
| **Directions** | Opens Google Maps with directions to the address. |

A **Visit** has no survey button: it’s a home visit with no survey form. That’s a job for products other than solar, battery or heat pump (infrared, EV or LED, say) with no survey type set and no survey started.

## Add to your diary

**+ Add to my diary** under the list opens the diary on **Surveys and diary** with the **Add to my diary** form already open, so you can add a site visit, an install, a meeting or something personal. See [Add to my diary](/admin/help/surveys/add-to-my-diary).

For doing the survey itself, see [Start a survey](/admin/help/surveys/start-a-survey).

> **Once switched on:** Today needs surveys.sql. Your diary entries and **+ Add to my diary** need survey-booking.sql too.
