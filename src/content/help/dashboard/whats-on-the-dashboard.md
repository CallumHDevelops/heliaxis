The dashboard is the page you land on after signing in. Get back to it from anywhere with **Dashboard** in the top menu, **Home** on a phone, or the Heliaxis logo. It only shows what your account can open, so a colleague’s may look different. Each part below appears only for the people it says.

![The dashboard on a computer](/help/dashboard/dashboard.jpg "The dashboard: search, Needs you and New Meta leads")

## At the top

- Today’s date and **Hi** with your first name.
- **View live site ↗** opens the public website in a new tab.
- The search box (Enquiries or CRM): find a customer or project. See [Search for a customer or project](/admin/help/getting-started/search).

## Needs you

A box with one row for each area where something is waiting, and a big number. Rows with nothing waiting are left out, and with nothing anywhere the box doesn’t show at all. Tap a row to go there.

| Row | The number is | Shows for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | Chats with unread messages | Enquiries |
| **CRM** | Follow-ups due today or earlier, on open jobs (everyone’s) | CRM |
| **Drafts** | Drafts from Metis waiting to be sent | CRM |
| **Tasks** | Open tasks past their deadline (everyone’s) | Tasks |
| **Enquiries** | New enquiries | Enquiries |
| **Subcontractors** | Agreements to countersign and documents to review | Subcontractors |
| **Users** | Sign-ups awaiting approval | Admins |

## New Meta leads

The newest leads from Meta that are still at New, with buttons to open, WhatsApp or call them. For Enquiries and CRM. See [New Meta leads on the dashboard](/admin/help/dashboard/new-meta-leads).

## Talk to Metis

For CRM: a bar that opens Metis, the assistant. "Just finished a meeting? Say what happened — notes, tasks and drafts done for you." See [The Metis page](/admin/help/metis/the-metis-page).

## From Claude

Setup to-dos left by Claude, for admins (and anyone an admin passes one to). See [From Claude setup to-dos](/admin/help/dashboard/from-claude).

## Today and My day

- **Today** (CRM, once surveys are switched on): today’s surveys and installs, and your own diary entries, with **Start survey** and **Directions**. See [Today’s surveys, installs and diary](/admin/help/dashboard/today).
- **My day**: your follow-ups due today and your open tasks, which you can tick off here. See [Your follow-ups and tasks in My day](/admin/help/dashboard/my-day).

## The areas you can open

- **Workspace**: a card for each area you can open, with a short description and **Open →**. A card can carry a count, such as "3 overdue" on Tasks or "2 new" on Enquiries.
- **Apps**: cards for the separate apps you can open (Our team, Social media studio, RAMS), marked **Open app ↗**.
- **On your phone**: the **Android app** card. See [Install the Android app](/admin/help/getting-started/android-app).
- **Admin** (admins only): **Users & access**, to approve sign-ups and choose what each person can open.

On a phone, the cards shrink to a grid of icons with their names, and a count shows as a dot.

> **Good to know:** If you’re sent here with "You don’t have access to that area. Ask an admin to add it to your account.", you tried to open a page your account can’t. See [What you can see depends on your access](/admin/help/getting-started/your-access).
