1. On the Tasks page, press **+ New task**. A **New task** box opens (from the bottom of the screen on a phone).
2. In **What needs doing?**, type the task. This is the only thing you have to fill in.
3. If it helps, add detail in **Notes, links, context (optional)**.
4. Under **Assign to**, pick who it’s for:
   - **Unassigned** if nobody’s on it yet,
   - a person from the list (you’re marked **(me)**),
   - **Someone else…** for someone without an account, then type **Their name**.
5. Under **Deadline**, pick a date, or tap **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Friday** or **Next week**. Tap the same one again to clear it.
6. Under **Priority**, tap **Low**, **Normal** or **High**. New tasks start at **Normal**.
7. Press **Add task**.

The task appears on the list straight away. **Cancel**, the **×** or Esc closes the box without saving.

## The deadline buttons

- **Today** and **Tomorrow** are what they say.
- **Friday** is the coming Friday (next week’s Friday if today is a Friday).
- **Next week** is a week from today.

## Who hears about it

- Give a task to someone else and they get a notification: "New task from" your name, with the task and its deadline. Tapping it opens the task.
- A task you give yourself doesn’t send a notification.
- Someone added with **Someone else…** has no account, so they aren’t told. Let them know yourself.
- On the morning it’s due, the person it’s for gets it in their round-up. A task that’s unassigned, or for **Someone else…**, goes in the round-up of whoever made it. See [Task notifications and reminders](/admin/help/tasks/task-notifications).

> **Watch out:** **Assign to** lists everyone who can sign in. A person without Tasks access can’t open the Tasks page, so check they have it before giving them a task here. See [What you can see depends on your access](/admin/help/getting-started/your-access).

> **Good to know:** A task about a customer is better added on their job, so it’s linked to them. See [Add a task from a job or a WhatsApp chat](/admin/help/tasks/from-a-job-or-chat). With CRM you can also add one from **+ Task** in **My work** on the CRM home, linked to a customer or, if you have Tasks access, not linked to one. See [My work: your tasks and next steps](/admin/help/customers/my-work).
