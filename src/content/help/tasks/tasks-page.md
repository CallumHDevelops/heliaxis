Open it from **Team › Tasks** on a computer (just **Tasks** in the top menu if it’s the only page in **Team** for you). On a phone it’s under **More › Tasks**, or **Tasks** on the bottom bar if you have Tasks but neither CRM nor Enquiries. From the dashboard, **All tasks →** under **My day** goes there too.

It’s a shared list: you see everyone’s tasks, not just your own.

![The Tasks page on a computer](/help/tasks/tasks-page.jpg "Tasks: counts, tabs, filters and the list")

## At the top

- **Tasks**, with "Create a task, assign it, give it a deadline — then tick it off." and a line saying whether your changes are saved (**All changes saved**, **Syncing…**, or how many are waiting).
- **+ New task**: see [Add a task](/admin/help/tasks/add-a-task).
- A row of counts: **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Next 7 days**, **Open** and **Done**. Tap **Overdue**, **Open** or **Done** to show just those.

Admins (and anyone an admin has passed a setup job to) may also see the **From Claude** card above. See [From Claude setup to-dos](/admin/help/dashboard/from-claude).

## Narrow the list

- Tabs: **Open**, **Overdue**, **Done** and **All**, each with how many it holds. **Open** is where the page starts.
- The person filter: **Everyone**, **Assigned to me**, any one person, or **Unassigned**.
- **Search…**: finds words in a task’s title or notes, or in a name typed in with **Someone else…**.

## The order

Open tasks come first, soonest deadline at the top (so overdue ones lead), then those with no deadline. Tasks due the same day go **High**, then **Normal**, then **Low**. Done tasks go to the bottom, the most recently finished first.

## Each task

- A circle on the left: tap it to tick the task off (see [Tick off a task](/admin/help/tasks/tick-off-a-task)).
- A **High** or **Low** tag, then the task’s title and any notes.
- Who it’s for, or **Unassigned**.
- The deadline: **No deadline**, "2 days overdue", **Due today**, **Tomorrow**, or the date.
- For people with CRM, a task that belongs to a customer’s job also shows **Job:** with the job’s HX reference and the customer’s name. Tap it to open the job.
- On the right: the status (**To do**, **In progress** or **Done**), **Edit** and **Delete**. See [Change, reassign or delete a task](/admin/help/tasks/change-a-task).

Opening a task from a notification scrolls the page to it and highlights it.

With no tasks at all the page says "No tasks yet — add the first one above." If the tab and filters you’ve picked match nothing, it says "Nothing here."

## Other places your tasks show

- **My day** on the dashboard: your open tasks, with a circle to tick each off. See [Your follow-ups and tasks in My day](/admin/help/dashboard/my-day).
- **My work** on the CRM home (CRM): your tasks and next steps by day. See [My work: your tasks and next steps](/admin/help/customers/my-work).
- The **Tasks** card on a customer’s job (CRM): that job’s tasks. See [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).

> **Once switched on:** Tasks needs tasks.sql. Until it has been run, the page says the tasks table isn’t set up yet.
