## Change a task

1. On the Tasks page, find the task and press **Edit** on its line.
2. In the **Edit task** box, change what you need: the title, the notes, **Assign to**, **Deadline** or **Priority**.
3. Press **Save changes**.

- **Reassigning** to someone else sends them a "New task from" notification, just like a new task.
- **Moving the deadline** means the task can be in the morning round-up again on its new date.
- To change only where a task is up to, use the status on its line instead (see [Tick off a task](/admin/help/tasks/tick-off-a-task)).

## Delete a task

1. Press **Delete** on the task’s line (or **Delete** at the bottom of the **Edit task** box).
2. Confirm when it asks "Delete “…”? This can’t be undone."

The task is gone for everyone.

> **Watch out:** Anyone with Tasks access can change or delete any task on the list, not just their own. If a task is finished, tick it off rather than deleting it, so there’s a record it was done.

> **Good to know:** A task on a customer’s job can also be moved to another day or given to someone else from the job’s **Tasks** card (**Snooze the task**, then **Whose task**). That doesn’t send the new person a notification, so tell them, or reassign it here on the Tasks page instead. See [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).
