A task about a customer is best added where their job is, so it’s linked to them. It’s still an ordinary task: it shows on the Tasks page with **Job:** beside it, on the job, and in the person’s **My day**. Anyone with CRM can add one, even without Tasks access.

## From the customer’s job

On the customer’s page (or the project’s page), press **+ Task** on the **Tasks** card, type **What needs doing?**, pick a day and who it’s for, and save. See [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks) and [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).

## From a WhatsApp chat

1. In the customer’s chat, press **+ Add task**.
2. Pick what you want:
   - **Call-back reminder** (at a set time),
   - **Task** (who and which day),
   - **Note** (on the job’s timeline).
3. The app fills it in from their latest message ("Tomorrow’s fine" becomes tomorrow). Check the words, the day or time, and who it’s for, and change anything that’s wrong.
4. Save it.

To start from a particular message instead, tap the **⋯** beside it and pick **⏰ Make a reminder from this** or **✓ Make a task from this**.

Nothing is saved until you save. Afterwards a line in the chat says what was set and who’ll hear about it, with **Open job** and **OK**.

For a **Task**, you’ll see **New task** with **What needs doing?**, a few ready-made ideas (**Send quote**, **Book survey** and so on), **Who** (you, unless you change it), **Due** with **Today**, **Tomorrow** and **Next week**, **High priority**, and **Notes**. Press **Add task**. If you picked someone else, they get a notification.

## A call-back reminder isn’t a task

A **Call-back reminder** sets the job’s next step at a time, replacing any next step the job already had (the sheet warns you). It shows under **My day** as a follow-up for whoever looks after the job, and the app sends them a "Follow up: …" notification when it’s due (at the time you picked, even in the evening). If someone else looks after the job, they’re also told straight away ("Call-back from" your name). If nobody looks after the job yet, everyone on the CRM is told when it’s due.

> **Good to know:** The sheet says which job it goes on: the job you opened the chat from, or their latest open job. With no open job, a task or note goes on their latest job, and a call-back reminder opens a new CRM record for them (a closed job’s reminder would never go off). If they have no job at all, saving opens one.

> **Good to know:** **+ Add task** and the **⋯** beside messages only show for people with CRM. See also [Make a task or call-back reminder from a chat](/admin/help/whatsapp/task-from-a-chat).
