Tasks is one to-do list for the whole team. Anyone with Tasks access sees every task on it, can add one for themselves or someone else, and ticks it off when it’s done. The app adds tasks of its own too, for paperwork deadlines and survey follow-ups, and reminds people in the morning about what’s due.
