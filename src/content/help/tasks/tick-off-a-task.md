## On the Tasks page

- Tap the circle at the start of the task’s line. It’s marked **Done** straight away: on the **Open** and **Overdue** tabs it leaves the list, and you’ll find it under **Done** (and at the bottom of **All**, with a ✓ in its circle).
- Ticked the wrong one? Find it under **Done** (or **All**) and tap its circle again. It goes back to **To do**.

You can also use the status on the right of each line:

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **To do** | Not started. Every new task starts here. |
| **In progress** | Someone’s on it. It still counts as open. |
| **Done** | Finished. |

## On the dashboard

Under **My day**, tap the circle beside one of your tasks. It leaves the list straight away. If it couldn’t be saved (no signal, say), it comes back so you can try again. See [Your follow-ups and tasks in My day](/admin/help/dashboard/my-day).

## Who hears about it

When you tick off a task someone else made, on the Tasks page or the dashboard, they get a notification: your name and "completed a task", with its title. A task that’s on a customer’s job doesn’t send it when it’s ticked off on the job’s **Tasks** card or in **My work** on the CRM home.

> **Good to know:** Some tasks the app made tick themselves off once the thing is done, for example "Finish the drawings for …’s survey" when the survey’s drawings are finished. See [Tasks the app makes by itself](/admin/help/tasks/automatic-tasks).

> **Good to know:** Tasks on a customer’s job can also be ticked on the job itself. See [Tasks and next steps on a job](/admin/help/projects/tasks-and-next-steps).
