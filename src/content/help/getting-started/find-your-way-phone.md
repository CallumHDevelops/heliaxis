On a phone the top menu is replaced by a bar along the bottom of the screen, like most apps. The bar at the top keeps the Heliaxis logo (back to the dashboard), the bell and your initials.

![The bottom bar with the More sheet open on a phone](/help/getting-started/phone-more.jpg "On a phone: the bottom bar and the More sheet")

## The bottom bar

What’s on it depends on what you can open. From left to right:

| Button | Opens | Shows for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | The dashboard | Everyone |
| **CRM** | The CRM pipeline | CRM |
| **Enquiries** | Enquiries | Enquiries, without CRM (in place of **CRM**) |
| **Tasks** | Tasks | Tasks only, without CRM or Enquiries (in place of **CRM**) |
| **WhatsApp** | The WhatsApp inbox, or WhatsApp chats if you have CRM but not Enquiries | Enquiries or CRM |
| **RAMS** | RAMS | RAMS |
| **More** | Everything else you can open | Everyone |

A red number on **WhatsApp** is how many people have messages waiting to be read.

## More

Tap **More** to open a sheet of tiles for every other page you can open: the pages from the top menu that aren’t already on the bottom bar. With more than six tiles they’re grouped under the same headings as the computer menu (**CRM**, **Operations**, **Finance**, **Team**, **CMS**, **Apps**).

Some tiles use a shorter name: **Smart replies** (Smart replies & chasers), **Templates** (WhatsApp templates), **Orders** (Purchase orders), **Design** (Design studio) and **Social media** (Social media studio).

The last tile is always **Help**, which opens the Help articles about the page you’re on. See [Find your way around Help](/admin/help/getting-started/using-help).

While the sheet is open, **More** turns into **Close**. Tap a tile, tap **Close**, or tap the dimmed page behind the sheet to shut it.

## Your account, the bell and search

- Tap your initials at the top right for **Appearance**, **Help**, **Android app**, **Users** (admins only) and **Sign out**. See [Find your way around on a computer](/admin/help/getting-started/find-your-way-computer) for what each does.
- Tap the bell for your notifications. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).
- There’s no Search button on a phone. Use the search box at the top of the dashboard instead. See [Search for a customer or project](/admin/help/getting-started/search).

> **Good to know:** On a phone the dashboard’s areas show as a grid of small app icons, without their descriptions. The Website CMS works best on a tablet or computer.
