The app tells you when something needs you: a new task for you, a follow-up that’s due, new WhatsApp messages and new enquiries, and news on your jobs such as a quote accepted, a deposit paid or a survey done. Each one goes in the bell, and also to your phone or computer if you’ve turned notifications on there.

![The notifications panel open under the bell](/help/getting-started/bell.jpg "The bell: unread notifications, Mark all read and Turn on")

## The bell

The bell is at the top right of every page, on a computer and on a phone.

- A red number is how many you haven’t read yet.
- A small dot (and no number) means notifications aren’t turned on for the device you’re using.
- Tap the bell to open **Notifications**. Unread ones have a dot beside them.
- Tap one to go straight to the page it’s about. That marks it read.
- **Mark all read** clears the number.
- With nothing there, it says "Nothing yet — you’re all caught up."

The list checks for new ones every minute while the app is open, and as soon as you come back to it. When anyone reads a WhatsApp chat, that chat’s notifications go for everyone, and once a new lead has been contacted, the "new lead" notifications about them go too.

## Get notifications on this device

Each phone and computer is turned on separately.

1. Tap the bell.
2. Under "Get a notification on this device for new WhatsApp messages, enquiries and tasks.", tap **Turn on**.
3. Tap **Allow** when your browser or phone asks.

The bottom of the panel then says "Notifications are on for this device." with **Turn off** beside it.

People with Enquiries also see a **Turn on notifications** banner at the top of Enquiries and the WhatsApp inbox until it’s on for that device.

## On a phone

- **Android app:** notifications come through the app by themselves. The panel says "Notifications come through the Heliaxis app." To change them, go to your phone’s **Settings → Apps → Heliaxis → Notifications**. See [Install the Android app](/admin/help/getting-started/android-app).
- **iPhone:** they only work from the Heliaxis app on your Home Screen, not from a Safari tab. See [Add Heliaxis to an iPhone Home Screen](/admin/help/getting-started/iphone). The unread number also shows on the app’s icon.

## If they don’t arrive

| What the panel says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Notifications are blocked — allow them for this site in your browser or phone settings." | Allow notifications for heliaxis.co.uk in your browser’s or phone’s settings, then try **Turn on** again. |
| "Notifications are off for the Heliaxis app…" | Turn them on in **Settings → Apps → Heliaxis → Notifications**. |
| "This browser can’t show notifications — install the app or use Chrome." | Install the Android app, add the iPhone Home Screen app, or use Chrome. |

> **Once switched on:** The bell needs notifications.sql, and notifications to the Android app need native-push.sql. Until an admin has also added the notification keys (it’s a **From Claude** to-do on the dashboard), notifications only appear in the bell. For admins, see [Set up phone notifications](/admin/help/admin/phone-notifications-setup).
