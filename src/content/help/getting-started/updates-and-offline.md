## "Heliaxis has been updated."

When a new version of the app is ready, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen saying **Heliaxis has been updated.** Press **Refresh** to load it. Finish what you’re typing first: the page reloads.

On the Android app there’s a second kind of update, for the app itself. See [Install the Android app](/admin/help/getting-started/android-app).

## "Offline"

When your phone or computer loses its connection, a bar says **Offline — changes will sync when you reconnect**. It goes when you’re back online.

These pages keep working with no signal, as long as you’ve opened them before on that device:

- the dashboard (as it was when you last loaded it),
- **Tasks**: you can add, change and tick off tasks, and they’re sent when you’re back online (see [Use Tasks with no signal](/admin/help/tasks/tasks-offline)),
- **Get the app**.

Surveys can also be done with no signal: today’s and tomorrow’s are copied to the phone while it has a connection. See [Start a survey](/admin/help/surveys/start-a-survey).

Open any other page with no signal and you’ll see **You’re offline**: "This page needs a connection." It has **Open Tasks**, **Dashboard** and **Try again**.

> **Good to know:** Signing out clears the copies kept on the device. If survey changes or photos are still waiting to upload, the app warns you before signing out. Task changes that haven’t been sent yet are cleared without a warning, so wait for **All changes saved** on the Tasks page first.
