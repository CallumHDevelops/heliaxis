The staff app comes in a light and a dark version. You choose under **Appearance**:

- **Light**: always light.
- **Dark**: always dark.
- **Match phone**: follows your phone’s (or computer’s) own light or dark setting. This is where everyone starts.

## Change it

1. Tap your initials at the top right.
2. Under **Appearance**, tap **Light**, **Dark** or **Match phone**.

The colours change straight away, and the menu stays open so you can see the difference. The same three buttons are also at the top of the **Get the app** page.

## Where it applies

- Your choice is kept on the device you made it on.
- It also follows you to your other devices: change it on your phone and your laptop picks it up next time you open a page there.
- The sign-in pages follow the same choice.
- The CMS page editor (**Manage CMS**) and its previews always stay light.
- The public website isn’t affected.

> **Once switched on:** Following you between devices needs theme.sql. Until it has been run, each device keeps its own choice.
