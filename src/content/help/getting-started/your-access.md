Each account is either a **Member** or an **Admin**. Admins can open everything. Members only get the areas an admin has ticked for them on the **Users** page. The menus, the dashboard and the phone’s bottom bar only show what you can open, so if something’s missing for you, it’s usually access rather than a fault.

## What each kind of access opens

These are the names as they appear on the Users page.

| Access | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Website CMS** | **Manage CMS** and **Blog** |
| **Brand guidelines** | Logos, colours, fonts and usage rules (from its card on the dashboard) |
| **Design studio** | **Design studio** |
| **Tasks** | **Tasks** |
| **Enquiries** | **Enquiries**, **WhatsApp inbox**, **Smart replies & chasers** and **WhatsApp templates** |
| **CRM** | **Pipeline**, **Surveys and diary**, **Projects**, **CRM reports**, **CRM settings** and **Metis**; **WhatsApp chats** if you don’t have Enquiries; and **Purchase orders**, once switched on |
| **Analytics** | **Website analytics** and **Heatmap** |
| **Subcontractors** | **Subcontractors** |
| **Our team** | **Our team** (in RAMS) |
| **Social media studio** | **Social media studio** |
| **RAMS** | **RAMS** |

Everyone who’s approved can open the dashboard and **Get the app**. The search box needs **Enquiries** or **CRM**.

Admins also get **Users** (in the menu under their initials), the **Users & access** card on the dashboard, and the **From Claude** setup to-dos.

## If you can’t open something

If you follow a link to an area you can’t open, the app takes you back to the dashboard with: "You don’t have access to that area. Ask an admin to add it to your account."

If you have no access at all yet, the dashboard says: "No portals have been added to your account yet. Ask an admin to give you access."

Ask an admin to tick what you need.

## Changing someone’s access

> **Admins only:**
> 1. Click your initials › **Users**.
> 2. Find the person (there’s a **Search name or email…** box).
> 3. Tick or untick the boxes under **Portal access**.
> 4. Press **Save**.
>
> For a new sign-up, tick their boxes first, then press **Approve** (it shows how many you’ve ticked, for example **Approve with 3 portals**), or **Reject**. Setting **Role** to **Admin** (then **Save**) gives them everything. You can’t change your own **Status** or **Role**.
>
> See [Add a new person (approve a sign-up)](/admin/help/admin/approve-a-new-user), [Change someone’s access or stop them signing in](/admin/help/admin/change-or-remove-access) and [What each access tick allows](/admin/help/admin/access-ticks).

> **Good to know:** **Social media studio** and **RAMS** are separate apps. Ticking them shows the link on the person’s dashboard. See [Open RAMS and the social media studio](/admin/help/getting-started/open-rams-and-social).

> **Once switched on:** Ticking access per person needs user-portals.sql. Until it has been run, members keep the old access: Website CMS and Enquiries.
