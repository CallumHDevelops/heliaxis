Help has a short guide to each part of the app: adding a customer, WhatsApp templates, reports and the rest. Everyone who can sign in can read all of it.

## Open Help

| On | Where |
|---|---|
| A computer | Click your initials, top right, then **Help**. |
| A phone | Tap **More** on the bar along the bottom, then **Help**. Or tap your initials, then **Help**. |

Help opens on the articles about the page you were on, under **Help for this page**. If there isn't one for that page yet, it says so, and you can search or pick a section instead.

## Search

1. Type in the box at the top of Help. On a computer, press **/** to jump to it.
2. The results change as you type, with the words that matched highlighted.
3. Click a result to read it, or press **Enter** to open the first one.

Every word you type must be in the article, but the start of a word is enough: "temp" finds "templates". Small words like "how" and "the" are left out, so "how do I add a customer" works.

If no article has all your words, Help lists the ones that have some of them. To start again, press **Esc** in the box or click **Show all sections**.

> **Good to know:** What you searched for stays in the page's address. Copy the address and send it to a colleague, and they see the same results.

## Read an article

Under an article's title you'll see:

- where it is in the menu on a computer, for example "CRM › WhatsApp templates", when it's about a page;
- who can use it: **Open to everyone**, **Admins only**, or the access it needs, such as **Needs CRM access**. If your account doesn't have that access, it says "you don't have it";
- **Open this page**, which takes you to the page the article is about. It's only there when you can open that page.

A long article lists its parts under **On this page**: down the side on a computer, above the article on a phone. At the bottom, **Previous** and **Next** take you through the rest of the section.

> **Good to know:** If an article needs access you don't have, ask an admin to give it to you.

## Ask Metis

If you have CRM access, you can ask Metis in your own words instead. Click **Ask Metis** on the Help page, under your search results, or at the end of an article. From your search results, Metis opens with what you searched for already in the box. Nothing is sent until you send it. Metis answers from these articles and gives you the link to the one it used.

> **Watch out:** For how-to questions Metis goes by these articles. If its answer doesn't match what you see on the screen, open the article it links to and follow that.
