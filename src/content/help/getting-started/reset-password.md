## Reset it yourself

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email**.
3. Press **Email me a reset link**.
4. The page says "If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way. Check your inbox (and spam)."
5. Open the email on the same phone or computer, and in the same browser, that you asked from, and tap the link. It opens **Set a new password**.
6. Type your **New password** and the same again in **Confirm new password**. It needs at least 10 characters.
7. Press **Save password**.

You’re signed in and taken to the dashboard.

> **Watch out:** The link you email yourself only works in the browser you asked for it in. Opened on another device or in another browser, it says the link has expired. On an iPhone, links in emails open in Safari, so ask for the link in Safari rather than in the Heliaxis app on your Home Screen, then sign in to the Home Screen app with your new password.

There’s no separate "change password" page: if you know your password and want a new one, follow the same steps.

## If it doesn’t work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That link has expired or was already used. Request a new one." | Each link works once, and only in the browser you asked from. Start again from **Forgot your password?** on the device you’ll open the email on, or ask an admin for a link. |
| "Your reset link has expired. Request a new one." | The same: ask for a fresh link. |
| "The passwords don’t match." | Type the same password in both boxes. |
| "Use at least 10 characters." | Make the password longer. |
| "Too many emails have been sent in the last hour…" | Wait an hour and try again, or ask an admin to send you a link. |
| "The reset email could not be sent (…)" | Ask an admin to send you a link. |
| No email arrives | Check your spam folder, then ask an admin. |

## Ask an admin

> **Admins only:** On the **Users** page (your initials at the top right › **Users**), each person has a **Password** line with two buttons:
>
> - **Email reset link** emails them a link to set a new password (it asks you to confirm first).
> - **Copy link** makes a new link and copies it, for you to send by WhatsApp or text. Don’t open it yourself: it signs in as them.
>
> Either link works once, for an hour, and on any device. Making a new one cancels any earlier link. See [Reset someone’s password](/admin/help/admin/reset-someones-password).
