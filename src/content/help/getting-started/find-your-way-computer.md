Every page has the same bar across the top. From left to right:

- The Heliaxis logo: back to the dashboard from anywhere.
- The menu: **Dashboard**, **CRM**, **Operations**, **Finance**, **Team**, **CMS** and **Apps**.
- **Search** (Ctrl K, or ⌘K on a Mac), for people with Enquiries or CRM: find a customer or project. See [Search for a customer or project](/admin/help/getting-started/search).
- The bell: your notifications. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).
- Your initials: your account menu.

![The top menu with the CRM group open](/help/getting-started/top-menu.jpg "The top menu: groups, Search, the bell and your initials")

## The menu

Click a group’s name to open it (with a mouse, just pointing at it opens it too), then click a page. Click anywhere else or press Esc to close it. The group holding the page you’re on is highlighted.

You only see the pages your account can open, so your menu may be shorter than a colleague’s. A group with nothing in it for you is left out, and a group with only one page for you shows as a plain link with that page’s name (for example just **Tasks**). **Operations** and **Finance** always stay as menus.

| Menu | Pages | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | The dashboard | Everyone |
| **CRM** | **Opportunities**, **Organisations**, **Contacts**, **Surveys and diary** | CRM |
| | **Enquiries**, **WhatsApp inbox**, **Smart replies & chasers**, **WhatsApp templates** | Enquiries |
| | **WhatsApp chats** (instead of **WhatsApp inbox**) | CRM without Enquiries |
| | **CRM reports**, **CRM settings**, **Metis** | CRM |
| **Operations** | **Projects** | CRM |
| | **RAMS** | RAMS |
| **Finance** | **Purchase orders** | CRM, once purchase-orders.sql has been run |
| **Team** | **Tasks** | Tasks |
| | **Subcontractors** | Subcontractors |
| | **Our team** | Our team |
| **CMS** | **Manage CMS**, **Blog** | Website CMS |
| | **Website analytics**, **Heatmap** | Analytics |
| **Apps** | **Design studio** | Design studio |
| | **Social media studio** | Social media studio |
| | **Get the app** | Everyone |

A number beside **WhatsApp inbox** (or **WhatsApp chats**), and on the **CRM** button, is how many people have sent WhatsApp messages nobody has read yet. It updates by itself.

**RAMS**, **Our team** and **Social media studio** open separate apps. See [Open RAMS and the social media studio](/admin/help/getting-started/open-rams-and-social).

You can also use the keyboard: Tab to the menu, then the arrow keys to move along it and up and down inside a group.

## Your account menu

Click your initials at the top right to see:

- your name and email,
- **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Match phone** (see [Light and dark](/admin/help/getting-started/light-and-dark)),
- **Help**: the Help articles about the page you’re on (see [Find your way around Help](/admin/help/getting-started/using-help)),
- **Android app**: the page to get the phone app,
- **Users**: who can sign in and what they can open (admins only),
- **Sign out**.

## On a tablet

On a narrower screen the Search button shrinks to a magnifying glass. On a tablet, on pages with the WhatsApp dock, a **WhatsApp** button beside the bell opens the dock over the page.
