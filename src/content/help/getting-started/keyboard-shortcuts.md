On a computer, these keys work across the app. On a Mac, use **⌘** wherever it says **Ctrl**.

| Keys | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ctrl K** | Any page (with Enquiries or CRM) | Opens the search over the page. On the dashboard it puts the cursor in the dashboard’s own search box instead. |
| **/** | The dashboard and the CRM page | Puts the cursor in the page’s search box, as long as you’re not already typing in another box. |
| **↑** **↓**, **Enter**, **Esc** | Search results | Move through the results, open one, or close the search. **Enter** straight after typing opens the first result. |
| **Ctrl Enter** | The message box in a WhatsApp chat | Does what the send button says. **Enter** on its own starts a new line. |
| **Ctrl Enter** | The note, call or visit box on a job’s **Activity** tab | Saves it. |
| **Esc** | The **New task** and **Edit task** boxes, the search pop-up, the bell, and the menu under your initials | Closes it without saving anything. |

## The top menu

- Press **Tab** until you reach the menu, then **←** and **→** to move along it.
- **↓** (or **↑**) opens a group and moves into it. Inside, **↑**, **↓**, **Home** and **End** move between its pages, and **Enter** opens one.
- **Esc** closes the group and takes you back to its name.
