Everyone has their own account. You ask for one yourself, then an admin approves it and chooses which parts of the app you can open.

## Ask for an account

1. Go to **heliaxis.co.uk/login**.
2. Under the sign-in box, next to "Need an account?", tap **Request access**.
3. Fill in **Full name**, **Email** and **Password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Press **Request access**.

You’ll see **Request received**: your account is pending approval by an administrator. You can’t sign in until it’s approved, so let an admin know you’ve registered.

> **Admins only:** A new sign-up shows on the dashboard under **Needs you** as **Users**, "awaiting approval". Approve it on the **Users** page (your initials at the top right › **Users**), where you also tick what the person can open. See [Add a new person (approve a sign-up)](/admin/help/admin/approve-a-new-user).

## Sign in

1. Go to **heliaxis.co.uk/admin** (or **heliaxis.co.uk/login**).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Press **Sign in**.

The app opens the page you were trying to reach, or the dashboard if you came straight to sign in.

- If the details are wrong, a red message appears above the boxes. Try again, or use **Forgot your password?** (see [Reset a forgotten password](/admin/help/getting-started/reset-password)).
- If your account hasn’t been approved yet, you’ll see **Awaiting approval** instead of the dashboard. Ask an admin to approve it. **Sign out** on that page takes you back to the sign-in page.

> **Good to know:** On an iPhone, the Heliaxis app on your Home Screen keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so you sign in there too. See [Add Heliaxis to an iPhone Home Screen](/admin/help/getting-started/iphone).

## Sign out

1. Tap your initials at the top right.
2. Tap **Sign out**.

Signing out also clears what the app keeps on that device: saved pages, your saved copy of Tasks (including any task changes not yet sent) and the surveys copied to the phone. On the Android app, that phone stops getting your notifications.

> **Watch out:** If survey changes or photos haven’t uploaded yet, the app asks first ("… haven’t uploaded yet. They stay on this phone and upload when you next sign in. Sign out anyway?"). Press **Cancel** and wait for a signal if you’re not sure. There’s no such warning for Tasks, so check the Tasks page says **All changes saved** before you sign out.
