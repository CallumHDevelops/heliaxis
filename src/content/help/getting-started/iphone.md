On an iPhone or iPad, Heliaxis isn’t downloaded from a store. You add it to your Home Screen from Safari, and then it works like an app: full screen, its own icon, notifications and an unread count on the icon.

## Add it

1. Open **heliaxis.co.uk/admin** in **Safari** and sign in. (Chrome on an iPhone can’t add it with notifications.)
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Heliaxis** from your Home Screen and sign in again. The Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.

When you open **Apps › Get the app** (or **More › Get the app**) from the Home Screen app, it says "You’re using the Heliaxis app from your Home Screen."

## Turn on notifications

1. In the Home Screen app, tap the bell at the top right.
2. Tap **Turn on**.
3. Tap **Allow** when the iPhone asks.

This needs iOS 16.4 or newer. To check, go to **Settings → General → About → iOS Version**.

## Good to know

- Notifications only work from the Home Screen app, not from a Safari tab.
- If an older Heliaxis icon opens the website’s homepage instead of the dashboard, delete it and add it again while you’re on the dashboard.
- Updates arrive by themselves. There’s nothing to download.

See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications) for what you’ll be told about, and [The Get the app page](/admin/help/apps/get-the-app) for the rest of that page.
