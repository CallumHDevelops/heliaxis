The Android app is your dashboard, Tasks and the other Heliaxis tools as an app on your phone or tablet. The dashboard and Tasks keep working with no signal and sync when you’re back online.

You’ll find the download page in four places:

- **Apps › Get the app** in the top menu (just **Get the app** if it’s the only page in **Apps** for you),
- **More › Get the app** on a phone,
- **Android app** in the menu under your initials,
- the **Android app** card under **On your phone** on the dashboard.

## Install it on the phone

1. On the phone, sign in at **heliaxis.co.uk/admin** and open the download page (see above).
2. Tap **Download the app**, then open the file when it finishes.
3. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from your browser. Tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**, and sign in as usual.
5. On Android 13 and newer, the app asks once if it can send notifications. Allow it, so tasks and messages reach you.

The app needs Android 7 or newer.

## Send it to your phone from a computer

Further down the page, **Send it to your phone** gives you two ways:

- Scan the code with your phone’s camera (the code isn’t shown on a narrow screen), or
- Under **Or email yourself a download link**, check the address (yours is filled in) and press **Email me the link**. Use any address you can read on your phone.

The link is personal and lasts 7 days. It opens a page with a one-tap **Download the app** button and needs no sign-in. Then carry on from step 2 above. An old link says **This link has expired**: tap **Sign in to get the app**, or send yourself a new one.

## Updates

- Most changes reach the app by themselves.
- When a new version of the app itself is out, a bar says **App update … available.** Tap **Update**, then **Download update**, then **Install**. Your data stays as it is. **Later** hides the bar for now.
- If your version is too old to keep using, **Please update the Heliaxis app** covers the screen until you tap **Get the update**.
- Opened in the app, the download page shows **You’re up to date**, or **Update available** with the build you have.

## What works where

- On a phone: everything your account can open. The bottom bar puts the dashboard and, depending on your access, the CRM (or Enquiries, or Tasks), WhatsApp and RAMS one tap away. See [Find your way around on a phone](/admin/help/getting-started/find-your-way-phone).
- Best on a tablet or computer: the Website CMS page editor, which needs a bigger screen.
- With no signal: the dashboard and Tasks, and surveys copied to the phone. Everything else needs a connection. See [Updates and working with no signal](/admin/help/getting-started/updates-and-offline).

> **Good to know:** In the app, notifications come through Android. To change them, go to your phone’s **Settings → Apps → Heliaxis → Notifications**. See [Notifications and the bell](/admin/help/getting-started/notifications).
