> **Admins only:** Only admins see the **Users** page and the links to it.

The **Users** page lists everyone who has an account, one account per person. From here you approve new sign-ups, make someone an admin, tick which parts of the app each member can open, and send password resets.

## Getting there

- Click your initials at the top right, then **Users**.
- On the dashboard, under **Admin**, press the **Users & access** card. It shows how many people are waiting, for example "2 awaiting approval".
- When someone is waiting, the dashboard’s **Needs you** strip also shows **Users** with the number "awaiting approval".

The old Approvals page now opens this page.

## What’s on the page

At the top it says how many people are **awaiting approval**, and there’s a **Search name or email…** box.

People waiting for approval come first, then approved people, then rejected ones. Each person has a card with:

- Their name (yours is marked **you**), email and the date they joined.
- **Status**: **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. Only approved people can sign in.
- **Role**: **Member** or **Admin**.
- **Portal access**: a tick box for each part of the app. For an admin these are all ticked and greyed out ("admins can open everything").
- The buttons: **Approve** and **Reject** for someone new, or **Save** for everyone else. **Save** only lights up once you’ve changed something. "Saved" appears when it has worked.
- A **Password** line with **Email reset link** and **Copy link**.

You can’t change your own **Status** or **Role**, so you can’t lock yourself out.
