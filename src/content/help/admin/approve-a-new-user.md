> **Admins only:** Approving people is done on the **Users** page, which only admins can open.

There’s no invite button. Each new person asks for their own account, then you approve it. That way they choose their own password and you never need to know it.

## 1. Ask them to request access

Send them to **heliaxis.co.uk/login** and ask them to tap **Request access** under the sign-in box. They fill in **Full name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters, then press **Request access**.

They then see **Request received** and can’t sign in until you’ve approved them. See [Get an account and sign in](/admin/help/getting-started/sign-in) for what they see.

> **Watch out:** Ask them to use their own work email. The app doesn’t check the address belongs to them when they sign up, so check the name and email look right before you approve.

## 2. Approve them

1. Open **Users** (your initials at the top right › **Users**). New people are at the top, with **Status** set to **Pending**. The dashboard also shows them under **Needs you** as **Users**.
2. Tick what they need under **Portal access**. See [What each access tick allows](/admin/help/admin/access-ticks).
3. If they should be an admin, set **Role** to **Admin** instead. Admins can open everything, so the ticks grey out.
4. Press **Approve**. Once you’ve ticked some boxes, it says how many, for example **Approve with 3 portals**.

"Saved" appears, and they can sign in straight away. They aren’t emailed, so let them know.

## If you don’t know them

Press **Reject**. Their status becomes **Rejected** and they can’t sign in. They see **Awaiting approval** if they try. You can approve them later by setting **Status** to **Approved** and pressing **Save**.

## Good to know

- If they’ll use **RAMS** or the **Social media studio**, tick those too. The first time they open either, they confirm their email with a code. See [Open RAMS and the social media studio](/admin/help/getting-started/open-rams-and-social).
- RAMS keeps its own accounts. One-tap sign-in only works for someone with an approved RAMS account under the same email. See [Set up one-tap sign-in to RAMS and the social studio](/admin/help/admin/one-tap-sign-in).
- If they’re a subcontractor, don’t give them an account here. Add their firm under **Subcontractors** instead: see [Add a subcontractor](/admin/help/team/add-a-subcontractor).
