> **Admins only:** These buttons are on the **Users** page. Anyone can also reset their own password from the sign-in page: see [Reset a forgotten password](/admin/help/getting-started/reset-password).

Each person’s card on **Users** has a **Password** line with two buttons. You never see or choose their password: they set a new one themselves from the link.

## Email them a link

1. Click your initials at the top right › **Users**, and find the person.
2. Press **Email reset link**.
3. Confirm the box that asks "Email a password reset link to …?".

It says "Reset link emailed to …". The email comes from Heliaxis with the subject "Reset your Heliaxis password", says you sent it, and has a **Set a new password** button.

## Copy a link instead

Use this when the email doesn’t arrive.

1. Press **Copy link**.
2. It says "Link copied. Send it to them; it works once, for an hour." The link also shows in a box underneath, in case copying didn’t work.
3. Paste it into a WhatsApp or text to them.

> **Watch out:** Don’t open the link yourself. It signs in as them.

## What happens next

They open the link and land on **Set a new password**. They type a new password (at least 10 characters) in **New password** and again in **Confirm new password**, then press **Save password**. They’re then signed in.

- Each link works once and lasts an hour.
- Making a new link, with either button, cancels any earlier one. If they say the link doesn’t work, send a fresh one.
