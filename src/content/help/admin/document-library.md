The **Document library** holds the guides and packs you send to lots of customers, such as an EV access guide, an intro pack or finance options. Anyone with CRM access can upload to it, and anyone can send its documents by WhatsApp or email from a customer’s page.

## Upload

1. Open **CRM › CRM settings** and scroll to **Document library**.
2. Press **⬆ Upload**.
3. Pick one or more files: a PDF, a photo, or a Word or Excel file, each under 25 MB.

The button says **Uploading…** while it works. Each document is named after its file (without the ending), and the list is in name order. Name the file clearly before you upload it, because that’s what everyone will see.

## Open or delete

- Click a document’s name (with 📚) to open it.
- Press **Delete** beside it and confirm to remove it from the library for everyone.

## Manuals and datasheets for the handover pack

Product manuals, datasheets, warranties and start-up and shutdown procedures go in this same library. Once they’re uploaded, an admin marks what each one is and which product models it’s for, under **Product library** further down the page. Every job with that model then gets it in its handover pack. See [Set up the handover pack](/admin/help/handover/set-up).

> **Once switched on:** The **Product library** part appears once handover.sql has been run.
