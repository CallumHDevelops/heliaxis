Saved replies are messages you send again and again, ready to drop into the WhatsApp and email composers with one tap. Anyone with CRM access can add, change and delete them.

> **Good to know:** Saved replies aren’t WhatsApp templates: they go into a normal message. WhatsApp only allows a normal message within 24 hours of the customer’s last one. Outside that, you need an approved template: see [WhatsApp templates](/admin/help/whatsapp/templates).

## Add one

1. Open **CRM › CRM settings** and scroll to **Saved replies**.
2. Press **+ New**.
3. Type a **Title** you’ll recognise, for example "Bank details" or "What happens at a survey".
4. Type the **Message**. Use `{first_name}` for the customer’s first name, and `{portal_link}` for their portal link (only for customers whose job has started).
5. Press **Save**.

It’s saved straight away; you don’t need **Save settings**.

## Change or delete one

Each saved reply is listed with its title and message.

- Press **Edit**, change it, then **Save**. **Cancel** leaves it as it was.
- Press **Delete**, then confirm. It’s gone for everyone.

## Use one

On a customer’s page, in the WhatsApp or email box, tap **⚡ Saved replies** and pick one. It’s added to your message with their first name filled in. Check it, then send as usual.
