Under each of Metis’s replies there’s a chip for every change she made. They’re the quickest way to check her work.

| Chip | What she did | Tapping it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Task: … | Made a task for you | The customer’s job, or the task in Tasks if it isn’t about a customer |
| Task for Esther: … | Made a task for someone else (they get a notification) | The same |
| Note on … | Added a note, call or visit to the timeline | The job |
| Next step: … · Tue 21 Oct, 10:00 | Set the job’s next step and when it’s due | The job |
| Email draft — Jane Smith | Saved an email for you to check | The draft on Metis’s page |
| WhatsApp draft — Jane Smith | Saved a WhatsApp for you to check | The draft on Metis’s page |
| New opportunity: … | Added a new customer and job | The new job |

If there’s no chip, she didn’t change anything: she only looked things up or answered your question.

> **Watch out:** The chips only show under replies from this visit. If you leave the page and come back, the conversation shows just the words. Open the job (or Tasks, or the drafts at the top) to see what was done.

## Putting a mistake right

Metis can’t undo her changes. Tap the chip to open what she changed and correct it by hand: edit or delete the task, change the next step, or discard the draft. Then tell her what was wrong if you want her to try again.

> **Good to know:** Notes and tasks Metis adds to a job also show on the job’s timeline, like anyone else’s.
