Metis works through the CRM the same way you would. When you ask for something, she does it, rather than telling you how.

## Look things up

- **Find a customer** by name, postcode, phone number or email (part of it is fine). If two customers match and it isn’t clear which you mean, she asks.
- **Get the full picture of a job**: contact details, where they came from, stage, value, products, address, survey and install dates, next step, notes, the latest timeline, recent WhatsApps and emails, open tasks and any drafts not yet sent.
- **Your day**: your open tasks, follow-ups due on your jobs (or jobs with no owner), new enquiries and unread WhatsApp chats.
- **The team**: who she can give a task to.

## Do things

- **Create a task**, for you or anyone on the team, with a due date and priority (low, normal or high), linked to the customer’s job if it’s about them. A task for someone else sends them a notification ("New task from …"). The task’s notes say "Added by Metis for …".
- **Add a note** to a customer’s timeline, as a note, a call or a visit.
- **Set the next step** on a job, with a date and time. The job’s owner is reminded when it falls due. See [Next steps and tasks for a customer](/admin/help/customers/next-step-and-tasks).
- **Draft an email** to a customer (they need an email address). Signed from Heliaxis with your first name.
- **Draft a WhatsApp** to a customer (they need a mobile number). Short, friendly and plain.
- **Add a new customer and opportunity**, for example someone you met at a show or a referral: name, mobile, email, postcode, the products they’re interested in, and a note. The new job starts at **Lead**, with you as the owner. The app doesn’t check for a duplicate when Metis adds one, so if they might already be in the CRM, ask her to look them up first.

Each thing she does shows as a chip under her reply. See [See what Metis did](/admin/help/metis/what-metis-did).

> **Good to know:** Emails and WhatsApps are only ever drafts. Nothing goes to a customer until a person opens the draft and presses **Send**. See [Check and send Metis’s drafts](/admin/help/metis/drafts).

## Dates

Metis knows today’s date and the UK time. If you say something vague like "next week", she picks a sensible working day and tells you which.

## Questions about using the app

Ask Metis how to do something in the app ("how do I send a booking link?") and she answers from these Help articles, so her answers match the screens you see. She gives the link to the article she used, so you can read the rest, and if Help doesn’t cover it yet she says so. See [Find your way around Help](/admin/help/getting-started/using-help).
