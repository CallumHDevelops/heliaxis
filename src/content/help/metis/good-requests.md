Say it the way you’d tell a colleague. One message can ask for several things: Metis does each one and lists them as chips under her reply.

## Your day

- "What’s on today?"
- "Any new enquiries or unread WhatsApps?"
- "What follow-ups have I got due?"

## After a visit or a call

- "Just left a survey with Jane Smith in NP44. She’d like the battery in the garage and wants a quote by Friday. Add a note and set a next step to send the quote on Thursday."
- "Spoke to Mr Davies on the phone, he’s going away until the 20th. Note it as a call and set a next step to ring him on the 21st at 10."
- "Met the Joneses at the show. New opportunity: Sian Jones, 07700 900123, CF23, interested in solar and a battery."

## Tasks for the team

- "Get Esther to send the EV guide to Rob Evans."
- "Remind me to order the scaffold for the Morgan job next Monday, high priority."
- "Give Callum a task to look at the loft photos on the Price job by Wednesday."

## Messages to check and send

- "Draft a WhatsApp chasing Jane Smith about her quote."
- "Write an email to Mr Hughes thanking him for his time today and saying the quote will follow this week."

Metis saves these as drafts. Open the draft at the top of her page, check it and press **Send**: see [Check and send Metis’s drafts](/admin/help/metis/drafts).

## Getting better answers

- Give a name and something else that narrows it down (a postcode, a town, the job) when the name is common.
- Say who a task is for and when it’s due.
- Give exact times and prices yourself. Metis won’t make them up.
- If she’s got something wrong, just say so: "No, the other Jane Smith, in Newport."
