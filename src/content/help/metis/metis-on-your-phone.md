On an Android phone with the Heliaxis app, Metis has her own full-screen chat: just the conversation, a big mic and the message box. It opens straight to her, not the dashboard.

## Open her from the app icon

1. Touch and hold the **Heliaxis** icon on your Home Screen.
2. Tap one of:
   - **Talk to Metis**: a new chat with the mic already on. Start talking.
   - **New Metis chat**: a new chat, ready to type or tap the mic.
   - **Last Metis chat**: carries on your most recent conversation.

To keep one on your Home Screen as its own icon, touch and hold it in that list and drag it where you want it.

These shortcuts need the app from version 1.0.6. If you don’t see them, update the app (**Get the update** on the banner at the top, or **Get the app** in the menu).

## The full-screen chat

- Each time you open it from a shortcut you get a new conversation, unless you chose **Last Metis chat**.
- Metis’s reply appears as she writes it. While she’s working, it says what she’s doing ("Looking up customers", "Adding a task").
- If the signal drops before she answers, tap **Try again** under your message.
- **New chat** at the top starts again. The back arrow goes to Metis’s page in the app, with her drafts.
- Your conversations are the same ones as on [Metis’s page](/admin/help/metis/the-metis-page): start in the van, carry on at your desk.

If you’re signed out, the app asks you to sign in, then takes you straight back to Metis.
