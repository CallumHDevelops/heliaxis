Metis is the assistant inside the app. Tell her what happened, after a meeting, a call or a survey, and she adds it to the CRM for you: notes, tasks, next steps, new opportunities, and drafts of emails and WhatsApps for you to check.

## Where to find her

- **On a computer**: **CRM › Metis** in the menu at the top.
- **On a phone**: tap **More** at the bottom, then **Metis**.
- **On Home**: tap **Talk to Metis** ("Just finished a meeting? Say what happened — notes, tasks and drafts done for you.").
- When Metis has drafts waiting, Home shows **Drafts** under **Needs you** with how many there are to send.

You need the CRM on your account to use her.

![The Metis page with a reply and the chips under it](/help/metis/metis-page.jpg "A request, Metis’s reply, and the chips for what she did")

## What’s on the page

- **… drafts to check**: emails and WhatsApps Metis has written, waiting for someone to check and send them. See [Check and send Metis’s drafts](/admin/help/metis/drafts).
- **The conversation**. On a fresh chat, Metis says hello and offers a few starters: **What’s on today?**, **Just left a survey with…**, **Get Esther to send the EV guide to…** and **Draft a WhatsApp chasing…**. **What’s on today?** asks straight away. The ones ending in "…" put the words in the box for you to finish.
- **The message box** ("Message Metis"), the mic button and the send arrow at the bottom.

## Sending a message

Type in the box and tap the arrow. On a computer, **Enter** sends and **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. On a phone, Enter starts a new line; tap the arrow to send.

While Metis works, three dots show under your message. Her reply is short and says what she did, with chips underneath for each thing she changed: see [See what Metis did](/admin/help/metis/what-metis-did).

## Your conversation

Each person has their own conversation with Metis, and it’s kept: start something in the van on your phone and carry on at your computer. **New chat** at the top (it shows once there’s a conversation) starts a fresh one when you change subject. The old one isn’t shown again.

> **Good to know:** Drafts are shared. Anyone on the CRM can see, change and send a draft Metis wrote for someone else.

> **Once switched on:** Metis needs assistant.sql to have been run, and an admin has to add her key. Until the key is added, her page says so.
