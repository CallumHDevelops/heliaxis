The Heliaxis system.

Energy that revolves around you
The idea

Helio (the sun) + axis (the point everything turns on). Heliaxis puts the customer at the centre of their own energy — the sun in orbit around them. The look is engineered warmth: warm sunlight on charcoal, sharp corners, hairline detail and confident, technical labelling.

Logo

Wordmark & reversed

Charcoal on light, reversed on dark. Clear space always; never recolour the spark or add effects.

The mark

The monopitch spark

Four separate tapered rays from an open centre, each with a single-slope monopitch tip. Rotates clockwise.

One ray rotated 0/90/180/270 about the centre. Path: M11 9.6 L13 9.6 L13 0.8 L11 3 Z

Rules

Do & don't

Do

  • Warm charcoal + gold; sharp corners ≤4px
  • Hairline borders over shadows
  • Mono eyebrows led by the spark
  • Real drone / install photography
  • Benefit-led, financial proof up front

Don't

  • Eco-green or bubbly UI
  • Clip-art or illustrated scenes
  • Gold as body text
  • The old solid-star spark
  • Stretch or recolour the logo
